Westport Harbor — Freight Gate Maintenance Schedule

Notice

The Westport port authority registers a preventive maintenance schedule for freight-gate weighing and entry equipment. Work is to be staggered across existing operating periods, with alternate lanes designated by the local operator.

Scope

The filing does not announce a full port closure, revised customs authority, or measured loss of trade. Carriers are directed to ordinary operating bulletins for any later schedule adjustments.

Next record

Equipment acceptance and queue records will be included in the port's normal operating return.

End of record.
