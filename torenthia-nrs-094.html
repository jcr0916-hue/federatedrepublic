Regional Water Laboratory — Sample Chain Review

Filing

The Regional Water Laboratory completes its scheduled review of sample receipt, transport and storage logs for the latest operating cycle. Two duplicate transfer entries were corrected against original signed receipt sheets.

Administrative scope

The corrections concern record transcription. The laboratory reports no alteration of test readings, compliance thresholds or previously published public findings through this administrative review.

Effect of record

Reconciled logs are retained for inspection. The next routine quality-control review remains on the laboratory's ordinary schedule.

End of record.
