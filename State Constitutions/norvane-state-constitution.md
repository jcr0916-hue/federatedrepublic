# CONSTITUTION OF THE STATE OF NORVANE

## Preamble

We, the people of Norvane, a State of the Federated Republic of Torenthia, situated
between the Torenthian Alps and the northern reach of Lake Varda — a border-facing land
whose shores have received the tired, the displaced, and the hopeful across the long
waters — do ordain and establish this Constitution in order that our State may govern
with the speed that arrival demands, the justice that human dignity requires, and the
accountability that republican government must always preserve.

Norvane is constituted on the premise that fragmented authority is dangerous where a
jurisdiction must respond quickly to sudden cross-border arrivals, transport
disruptions, public health strain, and emergency logistics. We therefore vest general
executive authority in a Governor directly accountable to the people, constrained by
the rights declared herein, the oversight of an elected Assembly, the review of an
independent judiciary, and the supreme authority of the Constitution of the Federated
Republic.

We are a permanent home and a place of passage. This Constitution speaks for those who
are here now, those who will arrive, and those who have not yet crossed the lake.

---

## Article I — Declaration of Rights

### Section 1.1 — Incorporation of the Federal Floor

The rights, liberties, and protections guaranteed by Article I of the Constitution of
the Federated Republic are hereby incorporated as directly enforceable state law. No
inhabitant of Norvane shall be required to exhaust federal remedies before asserting
federal constitutional rights in the courts of this State. Nothing in this Constitution
shall be construed to diminish or deny any right guaranteed by the Constitution of the
Federated Republic.

### Section 1.2 — State-Specific Rights

In addition to the federal floor, every person within the jurisdiction of Norvane
shall enjoy the following rights:

(a) **Access to Public Records.** Every person shall have a right of access to the
Norvane Record System and to records of state and local government, subject only to
such limitations as the Assembly may establish by statute for the protection of
personal privacy, law enforcement integrity, or matters committed exclusively to the
federal government. Any denial of access shall be reviewable by the courts of this
State.

(b) **Humane Reception Conditions.** Every person received into a facility operated,
funded, or overseen by the State for the purposes of arrival processing, temporary
shelter, or humanitarian intake shall be entitled to conditions consistent with human
dignity, including adequate shelter, nutrition, medical care, and protection from
harm. This right shall be enforceable against the State and its officers.

(c) **Non-Discrimination in Public Services.** No person shall be denied access to
state or local public services, benefits, or accommodations on the basis of national
origin, immigration status, asylum status, language, ethnicity, religion, or any
classification protected under the Constitution of the Federated Republic. This
subsection shall not be construed to require the provision of benefits to which a
person is not otherwise entitled under law.

(d) **Direct Challenge to Emergency Orders.** Any person affected by an emergency
declaration, or by any measure taken under its authority, may challenge the
declaration or measure directly in the courts of this State without exhausting
administrative remedies. The courts shall hear and determine such challenges without
undue delay.

### Section 1.3 — Construction

Rights declared in this Article shall be construed generously. Their enumeration shall
not be construed to deny or disparage other rights retained by the people. Where this
Constitution or any statute is capable of more than one construction, the construction
that better secures individual rights shall be preferred.

## Article II — The Governor

### Section 2.1 — Executive Authority

The supreme executive power of the State of Norvane shall be vested in a Governor, who
shall be the chief executive officer of the State and the commander-in-chief of its
organized militia. The Governor shall take care that the laws of the State are
faithfully executed and shall direct the civil administration of the State.

### Section 2.2 — Election and Qualifications

(a) The Governor shall be elected by the qualified electors of the State at a general
election held concurrently with elections to the federal Assembly. The Governor shall
be chosen by ranked-choice voting, with the winner determined by the vote of a
majority of electors voting after distribution of preferences.

(b) No person shall be eligible to the office of Governor who has not attained the age
of thirty years, been a citizen of the Federated Republic for at least ten years, and
been a resident of Norvane for at least two years immediately preceding the election.

(c) The Governor shall hold office for a term of four years beginning on the first day
of January following the election.

(d) No person shall serve as Governor for more than two consecutive terms. A person who
has served two consecutive terms shall be eligible for the office again after the
intervening term of another Governor.

### Section 2.3 — Powers and Duties

The Governor shall have the following powers and duties:

(a) **Appointment.** The Governor shall appoint the heads of executive departments,
members of boards and commissions, and all other officers of the State whose
appointment is not otherwise provided for by this Constitution or by statute. An
appointment shall be effective upon publication to the Norvane Record System. The
Assembly may, by statute, require confirmation by majority vote for offices
specifically designated in such statute; for all offices not so designated, no
confirmation shall be required.

(b) **Removal.** The Governor may remove any officer appointed by the Governor, or by
any predecessor in office, at pleasure unless this Constitution or a statute enacted
before the appointment provides otherwise.

(c) **Executive Orders.** The Governor may issue executive orders consistent with this
Constitution and the laws of the State. An executive order shall have the force of
law to the extent authorized by statute or by this Constitution. Every executive order
shall be published to the Norvane Record System upon issuance and shall expire at the
conclusion of the Governor's term unless sooner revoked or enacted into statute.

(d) **Pardons and Reprieves.** The Governor may grant reprieves, commutations, and
pardons for offenses against the laws of the State, except in cases of impeachment or
where a pardon would violate the Constitution of the Federated Republic. The Governor
shall report every grant of clemency to the Assembly and publish it to the Norvane
Record System.

(e) **Information and Recommendations.** The Governor shall deliver to the Assembly, at
the opening of each regular session, an address on the state of the State and may from
time to time recommend such measures as the Governor shall judge necessary and
expedient.

(f) **Extraordinary Sessions.** The Governor may convene the Assembly in extraordinary
session upon proclamation stating the purpose for which it is convened. No business
other than that stated in the proclamation may be transacted during an extraordinary
session.

(g) **Coordination with Federal Authorities.** The Governor shall coordinate with
federal domestic authorities in matters of asylum reception, public health, civil
transport, emergency sheltering, and such other matters as lawfully fall within the
State's constitutional sphere. Nothing in this subsection authorizes the Governor to
exercise powers reserved to the federal government under the Constitution of the
Federated Republic.

### Section 2.4 — The Budget Proposal

The Governor shall submit a proposed budget for the ensuing fiscal year to the Assembly
no later than the date established by statute. The Governor's budget proposal shall
constitute the framework within which the Assembly exercises its appropriations power
under Article V.

### Section 2.5 — Veto

(a) Every bill passed by the Assembly shall be presented to the Governor. The Governor
may approve the bill by signing it, in which case it shall become law.

(b) The Governor may veto the bill by returning it to the Assembly with a statement of
objections. The Assembly may override the veto by a vote of two-thirds of all members
elected, whereupon the bill shall become law notwithstanding the Governor's
objections.

(c) The Governor may veto any item or items of an appropriation bill while approving
the remainder. Any item so vetoed shall be returned to the Assembly and may be
overridden separately in the manner provided in subsection (b).

(d) If the Governor neither signs nor returns a bill within ten days of presentation, it
shall become law as if signed. If the Assembly by adjournment prevents return of a
bill within ten days, and the Governor does not sign it, the bill shall not become law.

### Section 2.6 — Delegation by the Assembly

The Assembly may by statute delegate regulatory, administrative, or emergency
preparedness authority to the Governor. A delegation shall state its purpose and
duration. Authority delegated under this section may be withdrawn by resolution of the
Assembly at any time; withdrawal shall be effective upon publication and does not
require the Governor's assent.

### Section 2.7 — Publication Obligations

The Governor shall cause to be published to the Norvane Record System:

(a) every executive order upon issuance;
(b) every appointment and removal of an officer;
(c) every veto and the statement of objections accompanying it;
(d) every emergency declaration and every report on an emergency's continued necessity;
(e) every grant of clemency;
(f) every refusal to discharge a duty imposed by this Constitution or by statute, with
a statement of the reasons for such refusal.

### Section 2.8 — Oath of Office

Before entering upon the duties of office, the Governor shall take and subscribe the
following oath or affirmation:

> "I do solemnly swear (or affirm) that I will faithfully execute the office of
> Governor of Norvane, that I will to the best of my ability preserve, protect, and
> defend the Constitution of the Federated Republic and this Constitution, and that I
> will discharge my duties in the interests of the present and future inhabitants of
> this State."

## Article III — The Deputy Governor

### Section 3.1 — Election

A Deputy Governor shall be elected on a joint ticket with the Governor and shall hold
office for the same term. The qualifications for Deputy Governor shall be the same as
those for Governor.

### Section 3.2 — Succession

The Deputy Governor shall act as Governor during any period in which the Governor is
unable to discharge the powers and duties of the office by reason of death,
resignation, removal, or incapacity. The Assembly shall establish by statute the
procedures for determining incapacity. A Governor who resumes the powers and duties of
the office following a period of incapacity shall publish a declaration of fitness to
the Norvane Record System.

### Section 3.3 — Vacancy in Both Offices

If the offices of Governor and Deputy Governor are simultaneously vacant, the officer
designated by statute shall act as Governor until a Governor is elected at the next
general election or at a special election called for that purpose.

### Section 3.4 — Delegated Authority

The Deputy Governor shall exercise such powers and perform such duties as the Governor
may delegate in writing. A delegation shall be published to the Norvane Record System
and may be revoked at any time. No delegation under this section diminishes the
Governor's authority or responsibility.

## Article IV — The Norvane Assembly

### Section 4.1 — Legislative Authority

The legislative power of the State of Norvane shall be vested in the Norvane Assembly,
which shall consist of members elected from districts established by statute. The
Assembly shall hold authority over legislation, appropriations, taxation, confirmation
of officers where required by this Constitution or by statute, oversight of the
executive administration, investigations, and impeachment.

### Section 4.2 — Elections and Membership

(a) Members of the Assembly shall be elected by the qualified electors of their
districts at general elections held concurrently with elections to the federal
Assembly.

(b) Elections shall be conducted by a method ensuring proportional representation of
the electorate. The Assembly shall establish the electoral method, district
boundaries, and number of members by statute, subject to the requirements of the
Constitution of the Federated Republic.

(c) No person shall be eligible to the Assembly who has not attained the age of
twenty-one years, been a citizen of the Federated Republic, and been a resident of
Norvane for at least one year immediately preceding the election.

(d) Members shall hold office for a term of four years beginning on the first day of
January following the election.

(e) No person shall serve as a member of the Assembly for more than three consecutive
terms. A person who has served three consecutive terms shall be eligible for the
office again after the intervening term of another member.

### Section 4.3 — Sessions

(a) The Assembly shall convene in regular session on the second Monday of January of
each year. The regular session shall continue for a period not to exceed ninety days,
unless extended by a vote of two-thirds of all members elected.

(b) Any business pending at the close of a regular session may be carried over to the
next regular session by resolution of the Assembly.

(c) The Assembly shall be convened in extraordinary session by the Governor upon
proclamation, or by a written request signed by a majority of all members elected
addressed to the presiding officer.

(d) The presiding officer shall be chosen by the Assembly from among its members.

### Section 4.4 — Legislative Process

(a) A bill shall become law upon passage by a majority of members present and voting,
approval by the Governor, and publication to the Norvane Record System, unless this
Constitution requires a greater majority.

(b) The Assembly shall establish its own rules of procedure, which shall include
provision for public hearings on proposed legislation and for the publication of
committee proceedings.

(c) No bill shall be passed on the day of its introduction without the consent of
two-thirds of the members present.

### Section 4.5 — Oversight and Investigation

(a) The Assembly may compel the production of records and testimony from any executive
department, agency, or officer of the State. No claim of privilege or confidentiality
shall be asserted against a duly authorized committee of the Assembly, except upon a
specific finding by the Norvane Supreme Court that the information requested
implicates matters committed exclusively to the federal government under the
Constitution of the Federated Republic.

(b) Any temporary withholding of records or testimony on grounds of an ongoing law
enforcement investigation or an imminent threat to public safety shall be authorized
by statute, shall be limited in duration, and shall be subject to judicial review.

(c) The Assembly may request information from the Governor, any department head, or any
officer of the State, and such request shall be answered within a reasonable time.

### Section 4.6 — Impeachment

(a) The Assembly shall have the sole power of impeachment. Impeachment shall require a
vote of a majority of all members elected.

(b) The Governor, Deputy Governor, judges of the state courts, and such other officers
as the Assembly may by statute designate shall be subject to impeachment for
corruption, constitutional breach, conviction of a qualifying offense, or gross
misconduct in office.

(c) The Norvane Supreme Court shall try all impeachments. No person shall be convicted
without the concurrence of two-thirds of the justices sitting. Judgment in cases of
impeachment shall not extend further than removal from office and disqualification to
hold any office of honor, trust, or profit under the State. The party convicted shall
nevertheless be liable to indictment, trial, judgment, and punishment according to
law.

### Section 4.7 — Compensation

Members of the Assembly shall receive such compensation as may be established by
statute. No change in compensation shall take effect during the Assembly term in which
it is enacted.

## Article V — Budget, Revenue, and the Public Treasury

### Section 5.1 — The Public Treasury

All public money received by the State, from whatever source, shall be deposited into
the Norvane Public Treasury. No money shall be withdrawn from the Treasury except by
appropriation enacted by the Assembly in accordance with this Article.

### Section 5.2 — The Budget Process

(a) The Governor shall submit a proposed budget to the Assembly no later than the date
established by statute.

(b) The Assembly may reduce or reallocate any item of proposed expenditure but shall
not increase the Governor's proposed total expenditure without the Governor's
concurrence.

(c) If the Assembly fails to enact a budget before the commencement of the fiscal year,
the Governor's proposed budget shall take effect as if enacted. The Governor may, in
such circumstances, authorize the continued expenditure of funds necessary for the
preservation of public health, public safety, and the operation of services required
by this Constitution or by federal law.

(d) The Governor shall certify all appropriations enacted by the Assembly. If the
Governor determines that an appropriation would cause total expenditure to exceed
estimated revenue in a manner inconsistent with Section 5.3, the Governor shall return
the appropriation bill to the Assembly with a detailed statement of the discrepancy.
The Assembly may reenact the bill with adjustments or override the Governor's
certification by a vote of two-thirds of all members elected.

### Section 5.3 — Fiscal Responsibility

(a) The State shall maintain a balanced budget over the economic cycle. The Governor
shall certify in the proposed budget that projected recurring expenditure does not
exceed projected recurring revenue for the fiscal year and for each of the two
succeeding fiscal years.

(b) This requirement shall not apply, and deficit expenditure shall be permitted:

  (i) during a declared emergency under Article VI, provided that the Governor
  publishes a finding that the deficit arises from emergency-related expenditure or
  from a severe revenue shock attributable to the emergency, and submits a recovery
  plan with a fixed not exceeding three years;

  (ii) upon a finding by the Governor, concurred in by resolution of the Assembly,
  that an extraordinary revenue shock arising from arrival-related or economic
  circumstances requires temporary deficit expenditure, provided that a recovery plan
  with a fixed horizon is published to the Norvane Record System.

(c) Any deficit expenditure authorized under this section shall be reported to the
State Auditor within thirty days and shall be subject to annual audit until the
recovery plan is fully executed.

### Section 5.4 — Taxation

(a) The Assembly shall have power to levy taxes by statute, subject to the Constitution
of the Federated Republic. Taxes may include income taxes, property-related taxes
where not reserved to local governments, excise taxes, and service or administrative
fees.

(b) Local governments may levy taxes only as authorized by state statute.

(c) No tax shall be levied except by law. Every statute imposing a tax shall state
distinctly the object of the tax and the purpose to which its proceeds shall be
applied.

### Section 5.5 — State Auditor

(a) There shall be a State Auditor, who shall be appointed by the Governor from a list
of nominees submitted by an independent selection panel. The State Auditor shall serve
for a term of seven years and shall not be removed except for cause by a vote of
two-thirds of all members elected of the Assembly.

(b) The State Auditor shall annually review and report on all receipts, disbursements,
emergency expenditures, procurement, and equalization transfers of the State. All
reports shall be published to the Norvane Record System.

(c) The State Auditor shall have full access to all state records, accounts, and
personnel necessary for the performance of the Auditor's duties. No claim of
confidentiality shall be asserted against the State Auditor, except upon a specific
finding by the Norvane Supreme Court that the records requested implicate matters
committed exclusively to the federal government.

### Section 5.6 — Local Fiscal Equalization

The State shall maintain a mechanism ensuring that municipalities and districts facing
sudden arrival-related fiscal pressure are not destroyed by the concentration of need
within their boundaries. The Assembly shall establish the formula by statute. The
mechanism shall be reviewed no less frequently than every five years by the State
Auditor, whose findings and recommendations shall be published to the Norvane Record
System.

## Article VI — Emergency Provisions

### Section 6.1 — Gubernatorial Declaration

The Governor may declare a state of emergency upon finding that a natural disaster,
cross-border humanitarian crisis, public health emergency, infrastructure failure, or
comparable crisis requires immediate statewide action. The declaration must state the
specific emergency authorities it activates, drawn from those this Constitution or
statute makes available for emergency use; no declaration may grant open-ended or
undefined authority, and any authority not stated in the declaration is not conferred.
The declaration and its stated authorities shall be published immediately to the
Norvane Record System.

### Section 6.2 — Duration and Positive Extension

A declaration shall lapse automatically fourteen days after issuance unless the
Assembly, by a fresh majority vote of all members elected taken before that date,
positively extends it for an additional period not to exceed thirty days per
extension. A declaration does not continue by default, and Assembly inaction or
silence shall never extend it. Each extension shall be published to the Norvane Record
System together with the Governor's report on the emergency's continued necessity.

### Section 6.3 — Legislative Termination

The Assembly may terminate a declared emergency at any time by simple majority vote of
all members elected, regardless of how much time remains under Section 6.2.
Termination requires no supermajority and no showing beyond the vote itself; the
Assembly's judgment that an emergency has ended or its authorities are no longer
justified is sufficient. A termination under this section shall be effective
immediately upon publication to the Norvane Record System and does not require the
Governor's assent.

### Section 6.4 — No Derogation of Rights

No emergency declaration or extension may derogate from any right guaranteed by Article
I of the Constitution of the Federated Republic beyond what that Constitution permits
during a lawfully declared federal emergency, and never from any right that Article
designates as non-derogable.

### Section 6.5 — Judicial Review

Any inhabitant affected by an emergency declaration, or by any measure taken under its
authority, may challenge it before the courts of this State at any time. A court may
rule the declaration or a measure taken under it unconstitutional during the emergency
itself. No doctrine of preclusion, ripeness, or exhaustion of remedies shall bar such
a challenge where the complainant alleges a direct and present harm.

### Section 6.6 — No Permanent Measures by Emergency Order

No measure adopted under authority granted by an emergency declaration shall outlast
that declaration. Any measure intended to continue after the emergency's expiry or
termination must be separately enacted as ordinary statute through the Assembly under
Article IV.

## Article VII — The Judiciary

### Section 7.1 — Judicial Power

The judicial power of the State of Norvane shall be vested in a Norvane Supreme Court,
a Norvane Court of Appeals, and such trial courts as the Assembly may establish by
statute. The courts shall have jurisdiction over all matters arising under this
Constitution and the laws of the State, subject to the appellate jurisdiction of the
federal courts under the Constitution of the Federated Republic.

### Section 7.2 — Judicial Nominations and Appointments

(a) There shall be an independent Judicial Nominating Commission, whose composition
shall be established by statute. No more than one-third of the Commission's members
shall be appointed by the Governor; the remainder shall be drawn from the practicing
bar, the judiciary, law faculties, and civic institutions in proportions established
by statute.

(b) The Commission shall evaluate candidates for judicial office and submit a list of
qualified nominees to the Governor. The Governor shall nominate judges from the list
submitted by the Commission.

(c) The Assembly shall confirm judicial nominees by majority vote of members present
and voting.

### Section 7.3 — Terms and Security of Tenure

(a) Judges of the Norvane Supreme Court shall serve for terms of twelve years. Judges
of the Court of Appeals and trial courts shall serve for terms established by statute.

(b) A judge may be reappointed, subject to the same nomination and confirmation
procedures applicable to an initial appointment.

(c) A judge shall not be removed from office except by impeachment under Section 4.6 or
upon determination by the Norvane Supreme Court, after hearing, that the judge is
permanently incapacitated or has engaged in conduct incompatible with judicial office.

(d) The compensation of judges shall not be diminished during their continuance in
office.

### Section 7.4 — Judicial Review

The courts of this State shall have the power to declare any statute, executive order,
emergency declaration, regulation, or official act void if it conflicts with this
Constitution or the Constitution of the Federated Republic. No statute shall be
declared unconstitutional except by the concurrence of a majority of the justices of
the Norvane Supreme Court sitting en banc.

### Section 7.5 — Standing

Any inhabitant of Norvane who alleges a direct and present injury arising from a
violation of this Constitution shall have standing to bring a claim in the courts of
this State. The Assembly may by statute broaden standing further; it shall not narrow
standing below the minimum established by this section.

## Article VIII — Local Government

### Section 8.1 — Recognition

Municipalities, counties, and other local governmental bodies are recognized as
institutions of this State. They hold only such powers as are conferred by this
Constitution or by statute and exercise those powers subject to the superior authority
of the State.

### Section 8.2 — Guarantees

(a) Every local government shall have an elected council chosen by the qualified
electors resident within its boundaries.

(b) The State shall not abolish a local government except with the consent of its
electors expressed at a referendum conducted for that purpose.

(c) The State shall ensure that every local government possesses reasonable fiscal
capacity to discharge the functions assigned to it. No unfunded mandate shall be
imposed upon a local government unless the Assembly has determined that the mandate
serves a compelling state interest and has provided for the costs of compliance.

### Section 8.3 — State Authority

The State may by statute:

(a) establish minimum service standards for local government functions;
(b) create regional coordination authorities for matters crossing local boundaries;
(c) authorize temporary state administration of a particular function of a local
government where the local government is demonstrably and persistently unable to
discharge that function, subject to judicial review and a maximum duration of two
years;
(d) during a declared emergency under Article VI, establish temporary reception or
logistics zones exercising such functions as are necessary to respond to the
emergency, provided that the boundaries of such zones and the functions they exercise
shall be published to the Norvane Record System and shall lapse with the emergency.

### Section 8.4 — Local Functions

Subject to the authority of the State under Section 8.3, local governments shall
exercise authority over local roads, sanitation, municipal policing where authorized
by statute, housing administration, local planning consistent with state law, and such
other local services as the Assembly may assign.

### Section 8.5 — Lake Varda Shorelands

The shorelands of Lake Varda within the jurisdiction of Norvane are held in public
trust. The State shall ensure reasonable public access to the lakeshore and shall
coordinate with the State of Rhovane and the State of Kelvant in the stewardship of
the lake waters. The Assembly may by statute establish a Lake Varda Shorelands
Commission and delegate to it such regulatory authority as may be necessary for the
protection of the public trust.

## Article IX — Federal Integration

### Section 9.1 — Supremacy

This Constitution is subordinate to the Constitution of the Federated Republic in all
respects. Federal law prevails over any conflicting provision of this Constitution or
of state law. No office created by this Constitution shall exercise any power
inconsistent with the Constitution of the Federated Republic, including its
protections for courts, voting, equality, due process, asylum, and non-refoulement.

### Section 9.2 — State Emergency Powers and Federal Supremacy

No emergency power vested in the Governor under Article VI, nor any power delegated to
the Governor under Section 2.6, shall be exercised in a manner that displaces,
obstructs, or contravenes federal authority. The Governor shall coordinate with
federal authorities in matters of asylum reception, public health logistics, civil
transport, and emergency sheltering, without assuming or interfering with federal
coercive, border, or asylum-determination powers.

### Section 9.3 — The Norvane Record System

(a) There shall be a Norvane Record System, which shall serve as the permanent public
record of the acts, proceedings, and publications of the government of this State.

(b) Every constitutional officer, department, agency, and local government shall
publish to the Norvane Record System such documents as this Constitution or statute
requires.

(c) The Norvane Record System shall be synchronized with the federal National Record
System in respect of matters of shared constitutional relevance. The Assembly shall
establish by statute the procedures for such synchronization, consistent with federal
requirements.

### Section 9.4 — Federal Constitutional Compliance

The State of Norvane acknowledges and accepts the federal audit framework established
under the Constitution of the Federated Republic. The Governor and all other officers
of the State shall cooperate with federal auditors and Monitors in the lawful
discharge of their duties. Nothing in this Constitution shall be construed to impair
the authority of federal institutions to ensure state compliance with the Constitution
of the Federated Republic.

## Article X — Amendment

### Section 10.1 — Proposal

(a) The Assembly may propose amendments to this Constitution by a vote of two-thirds of
all members elected.

(b) A proposed amendment shall be published to the Norvane Record System and shall lie
over until the next regular session of the Assembly following the next general
election. If at that session the Assembly again approves the amendment by a vote of
two-thirds of all members elected, the amendment shall be submitted to the qualified
electors of the State.

(c) The Assembly shall set the date of the referendum, which shall be no sooner than
sixty days and no later than one hundred and twenty days after the second approval.

### Section 10.2 — Ratification

An amendment shall become part of this Constitution when ratified by a majority of the
electors voting on the amendment at the referendum.

### Section 10.3 — Sealed Provisions

This Article shall not itself be amended except by the procedure established in this
Article. No amendment shall diminish the rights declared in Article I, nor alter the
subordination of this Constitution to the Constitution of the Federated Republic
declared in Section 9.1, without ratification by two-thirds of the electors voting on
the amendment.

## Article XI — Transition and Continuity

### Section 11.1 — Effective Date

This Constitution shall take effect upon:

(a) its ratification by the qualified electors of the State at a referendum conducted
for that purpose;
(b) the establishment of the initial electoral districts for the Assembly;
(c) the certification of the Norvane Record System as operational;
(d) the scheduling of the first elections for Governor and Assembly;
(e) the publication of the full ratified text to the Norvane Record System.

### Section 11.2 — Continuity of Local Government

All municipalities, counties, and local governmental bodies existing at the effective
date shall continue in existence with their existing boundaries, officers, and
functions until altered in accordance with this Constitution and state law.

### Section 11.3 — Continuity of Courts

All courts existing at the effective date shall be deemed courts established under
Article VII. Judges holding office at the effective date shall continue in office
until the expiration of their existing terms, and shall thereafter be subject to the
nomination and confirmation procedures established by this Constitution.

### Section 11.4 — Continuity of Statutes

All statutes, regulations, and ordinances in force at the effective date shall remain
in force until amended, repealed, or declared void in accordance with this
Constitution, to the extent they are consistent with its provisions.

### Section 11.5 — First Governor

The first Governor and Deputy Governor elected under this Constitution shall take
office upon the effective date and shall serve until the first day of January
following the next general election for Governor.

### Section 11.6 — Severability

If any provision of this Constitution is held invalid by a court of competent
jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The
invalid provision shall be severable and shall not affect the validity of the
remainder.
