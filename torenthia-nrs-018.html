Certification

The Executive Monitor certifies that the equalization mechanism established under §12.8 is operating as the Legislature has defined it by statute. Distributions are calculated according to the current formula without deviation. No procedural or administrative failure in the mechanism's operation is found. This certification does not constitute a finding that the mechanism has failed within the meaning of §12.8.

Supplementary Findings

The Executive Monitor is not limited by §12.8 to a binary certification and includes the following data as context for the Legislature's own consideration, without characterizing it as a failure finding.

MeasureYear 10Year 13
Territories with capacity requests pending >3 years25
Median request-to-funding interval, Territories2.1 years4.4 years
Median request-to-funding interval, disadvantaged States1.8 years2.0 years
Temporary infrastructure authorizations exceeding 3 renewals411

The gap between the Territory median and the disadvantaged-State median has widened in each of the last four annual certifications. The mechanism's formula does not distinguish between a request that clears in two years and one that clears in six; both are recorded identically as "funded." This certification is the first to include the underlying interval data rather than the funding outcome alone, at the Joint Committee's specific request.

Disposition

Because no failure is found, the 90-day Legislative response period under §12.8 is not triggered. The supplementary data is provided for the Joint Committee's use in its ongoing review and does not itself require any legislative act.

End of record.
