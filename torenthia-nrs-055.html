Archive Scanner Maintenance — Quarterly Service Record

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0719
Record Type:Routine equipment maintenance record
Filing Authority:NRS Panel — Administration
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Service completed

Quarterly preventive maintenance has been completed on the high-volume scanners used to digitize paper records submitted for public archival retention. Feed assemblies, image sensors and document-count calibration were inspected and tested.

Availability

Routine public-record intake remained available during the service period. No record-loss or publication interruption was reported.
