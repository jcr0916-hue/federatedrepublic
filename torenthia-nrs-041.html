Basis for Filing

The Notice of Committee Formation (NRS-Y13-0701) stated that a complete membership roster would be filed separately upon the Committee's first organizing session. That session has occurred. This record supplies the roster.

Committee Membership

Eleven delegates in total. Co-Chairs: Jalen Mire (lake-corridor district) and Tomas Rell (inland district). The remaining nine seats are apportioned across corridor, interior, and unaligned Assembly districts in rough proportion to the Convention's overall composition. Individual member names beyond the Co-Chairs are recorded in the Convention's internal minutes and are not separately restated in this filing.

First Organizing Session

Held Year 13, Month 12. The Committee adopted a working schedule for data requests to relevant offices, including the Elections Panel, the Executive Monitor, and Korda's own transportation authority.

Anticipated Report

The Committee set an internal target of delivering a preliminary compilation within thirty days. This target is the Committee's own choosing and is not a constitutional deadline. Any delay against this internal target has no effect on the Convention's ninety-day period under §15.5.a(4), and does not toll, pause, or extend it.

Disposition

Filed to the NRS as required. Further notice will be filed upon the Committee's preliminary compilation or upon adoption of any proposed resolution, whichever occurs first.

End of record.
