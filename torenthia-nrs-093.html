National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0005

District Records Office — Public Counter Equipment Renewal

Filing

The District Records Office records replacement of two aging public-counter document scanners and associated receipt printers at its municipal service counter. Existing paper intake and receipt procedures remain available during installation.

Administrative scope

The purchasing return identifies delivery verification, inventory registration and a short staff familiarization period. No new application category, identification requirement, filing deadline or fee has been adopted.

Effect of record

Local supervisors will record equipment acceptance in the ordinary maintenance ledger. This notice certifies a completed administrative replacement, not a change in substantive rights.

End of record.
