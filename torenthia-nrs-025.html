Request

The Federal Prosecution Service requests supplemental appropriation in the amount of 340,000 units against Case File FPS-Y13-0619, an active investigation opened Year 13, Month 4. The request covers forensic accounting services, records retrieval from three financial institutions under standing subpoena, and travel costs associated with interviews conducted outside the investigating district.

Basis

The investigation's original allocation, set at case opening, has been exhausted ahead of the projected closure date. The Service attributes the shortfall to the volume of records produced under subpoena exceeding initial estimates. No extension of the investigation's statutory review period is requested at this time.

Scope Note

Consistent with the independence of individual prosecutorial decisions under §2.15, this filing does not identify the subject matter, parties, or jurisdiction of the underlying investigation. The requested amount and its stated categories of use are a matter of public record; the investigation itself is not.

Disposition

Forwarded to the Assembly Appropriations Committee for consideration at its next scheduled session. No committee date has been set as of this filing.

End of record.
