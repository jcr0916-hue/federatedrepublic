Backup Power Installation Return — Caldenmere Second Group

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0727
Record Type:Installation and site-test return for accepted equipment
Filing Authority:Elections Panel — Equipment Certification
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Second installation group

The Panel records installation of twelve additional backup power units from the thirty-six accepted for Caldenmere public-access terminals. Together with the first group recorded in NRS-Y13-0724, twenty-four units are now installed. Twelve units remain outstanding.

Site tests

The returns record transfer from mains power, operation during the supported interval and recovery after restored supply at each newly installed site. No variance from the accepted equipment configurations is reported for this group.

Scope

This filing records equipment installation only. It establishes no election date, electoral period, turnout figure or vote result. The remaining installations require a later return.

End of record.
