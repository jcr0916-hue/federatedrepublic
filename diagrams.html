Constitutional Infrastructure The National Record System (§10.1) is the permanent, tamper-evident public record underlying every institution above. How a Law Is Made

Ordinary legislation starts in the Assembly; both executives review concurrently after final passage.

1
Introduced in the Assembly §3.4
Every law starts here and must address a single subject.
2
Assembly Passage §3.7
Absolute majorityMore than half of the full seated membership — not just those present and voting.§3.7 of the full seated Assembly.
✓ passes → Senate  ·  ✗ fails → dies
3
Senate Review §3.1 §3.7
Senate must take it up within the statutory window.
✓ passes  ·  ↩ amends → back to Assembly  ·  ⏱ no action → deemed approved
4
Concurrent Executive Review §2.7
Both executives review within 10 business days of final passage. The CC may use its suspensive veto except on excluded bills; the LC may veto on a genuine concern within its specified security domain.
CC return: tabled one month; Senate may override by 2/3. LC veto: Senate may override by 2/3 during that session; otherwise the bill lapses for the session. Repassage and one-use limits differ — §2.3.a and §2.7 govern.
5
Enactment §2.7
Absent an applicable blocking veto or recall, enactment follows the concurrent 10-business-day review rules. A CC fiscal notice is informational but permits the Assembly Speaker to recall the bill within the remaining window.

Military Authorization

One immediate power, and every other use of force gated behind authorization. Cessation is always the default.

Immediate Defensive Force §2.2
Legat Consul acts alone against an active or imminent attack — notify both chambers within 24 hours. Strictly defensive.
All Other Force §2.2 §14.1
Anything beyond immediate defense needs legislative authorization naming a §14.1 purpose. Cessation is the default — the burden is on authorizing, never on stopping.
Treaty-Based Path §14.4 §3.6
Where an existing treaty requires action, the Legat Consul may act on that authority alone.
Funding & Oversight §14.2
All military spending needs legislative authorization plus ordinary budget approval — Senate oversees throughout.
Second Renewal §14.3
After authorization is renewed twice, the Legat Consul must submit a transition and reconstruction plan.

Constitutional Candidate Pools

The Elections Panel maintains the registries and applies objective criteria; Monitors audit administration. Selection and confirmation follow each office's own constitutional procedure. The JMC conducts the pool lotteries assigned to it; expressly assigned Elections Panel lotteries remain with that Panel.
