National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0014

Elections Panel — Legat Consul Declarations of Candidacy

The Elections Panel registers declarations of candidacy for the spring Legat Consul election from Ines Carrow, Seren Mak, Tobias Vael and Pell Sandris.

Registration records that a declaration was received. Qualification review under the Panel's published standards remains open for each declaration, and this record enters no qualification determination. Applications for public campaign financing under §8.4 are filed and reviewed separately.

This record sets no ballot order, debate arrangement or election date. Further filings will be published to the National Record System as they are received.

End of record.
