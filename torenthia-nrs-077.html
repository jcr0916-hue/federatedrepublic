NRS-Y13-0741

Municipal Service-Desk Accessibility Equipment Return

National Record System · Year 13, Month 12

Record Identification

Reference: NRS-Y13-0741
Type: Facilities completion return
Filing Authority: Federal Public Access Administration
Date: Year 13, Month 12

Work completed

A scheduled replacement cycle installed accessible reading terminals and adjustable-height counter equipment in the designated municipal service desks. The return records completion of equipment testing and staff orientation.

Public service continuity

The service desks remained open under temporary counter arrangements during installation. Existing application procedures, eligibility requirements, and filing deadlines were unchanged.

Scope

This is an ordinary procurement and accessibility maintenance record. It creates no new program or constitutional determination.

End of record.
