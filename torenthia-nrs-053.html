Partial Response — Shared Facilities Reconciliation

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0717
Record Type:Partial response to committee reconciliation request
Filing Authority:Korda Territorial Administration
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Material supplied

The territorial administration submits a first reconciled schedule responding to NRS-Y13-0711. The schedule identifies the recorded owner, current operator, principal service area and routine maintenance responsibility for facilities appearing in the administration's earlier fiscal and service returns.

The response also separates facilities serving the certified lake corridor from facilities serving both the corridor and the remainder of Korda.

Outstanding entries

Several entries remain incomplete where title records, long-term maintenance agreements, or transportation-service contracts are held by another public body or require verification against archived records. Those entries are marked pending rather than estimated.

Status

This filing supplies evidence for the Joint Committee on Transition Facts. It does not establish a valuation, allocate transition costs, determine ownership disputed by another record, propose a boundary, or recommend a status outcome.
