Date Certified

The referendum on the merger of the Kelvant-Korda lake corridor with the State of Kelvant is set for Year 13, Month 12, Day 8. All prerequisites under §15.4 and §15.5.a having been satisfied, this date falls within the period established by statute following the scheduling certification of Year 13, Month 10.

Voter Eligibility

Eligible voters are those registered within the defined portion of Korda Territory identified in the certified petition, authenticated through the NVS using Citizen Voting Credentials. Passage requires 60% approval with at least 50% participation, per §15.4.

Outstanding Matters

This certification addresses referendum scheduling only. It does not constitute or depend upon publication of a judicial transition framework for the two Territorial court seats within the defined portion, and does not resolve the dissent recorded in NRS-Y13-0447.

Disposition

Certification published to the NRS as required. Following the referendum, the 30-day post-certification challenge window under §15.4 applies to the result.
