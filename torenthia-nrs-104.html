National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0016

Orath — Dorven Assembly District Qualification Roll

Orath's State election administration publishes the qualification roll for the Dorven-area Assembly seat. Three candidates are qualified to appear, listed in order of filing: Renna Ostrow, Dev Marrick and Fenwick Lund.

Qualification confirms eligibility to appear on the ballot. It is not an endorsement, and the order of filing is not the ballot order. The seat is filled by Ranked Choice Voting. This roll sets no ballot order and no election date.

Challenges to qualification, if any, follow Orath's established procedure and will be noted on the roll.

End of record.
