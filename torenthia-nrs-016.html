Date of Certification:Year 13, Month 9, Day 30
Certification

The Elections Panel certifies that the referendum held in the lake corridor portion of Korda Territory on the question of merger with the State of Kelvant achieved 60% approval of ballots cast, with participation exceeding the 50% floor required under §15.4. The threshold is met. The petition is certified to proceed.

Next Phase

Under §15.4, a Territory or defined portion of a Territory may merge with a geographically contiguous existing State upon: (1) a 60% referendum with at least 50% participation, satisfied by this certification; (2) approval by the existing State through its own constitutional process; (3) a joint LM-JM Statehood Audit confirming the existing State's integrity is maintained; and (4) Senate ratification by simple majority.

This certification satisfies condition (1) only. The petition now enters the joint LM-JM Statehood Audit phase to confirm that Kelvant's own constitutional integrity is maintained by the proposed merger, and Kelvant's own approval process under its constitution proceeds independently and on its own timeline. No Senate ratification vote may be scheduled until both remaining conditions are satisfied.

Committee Note

The Panel records, without comment on the merits, that this petition concerns the lake corridor portion of Korda Territory specifically and does not extend to Korda Territory as a whole.

Disposition

Published to the NRS as required. The joint LM-JM Statehood Audit will be scheduled and separately reported upon completion.

End of record.
