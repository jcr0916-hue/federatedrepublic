Evidence now before the Convention

The committee has received transfer schedules, service and asset returns, preliminary transition estimates, title records and contract material after the first freight evidence. It now says the reconciled record is sufficient to begin preparing its non-binding preliminary compilation, while residual archive, contract-amendment and cost-allocation questions will remain marked as unresolved. A service area does not itself allocate costs, and present ownership or operation does not determine future transfer. The compilation is not yet published, the committee has recommended no disposition, and the Convention has adopted no resolution or replacement boundary.
