Evidence and proposals now before the Convention

The Joint Committee has published its preliminary factual compilation (NRS-Y13-0746), comparing northern corridor, southern lake and interior service links while identifying unresolved title, contract-amendment and shared-cost entries. It recommends neither a boundary nor a status outcome. As individual delegates, Jalen Mire and Tomas Rell have since filed a non-binding joint drafting memorandum (NRS-Y13-0747) exploring a smaller northern-corridor adjustment with continued southern Korda lake access. Dessa Orin has separately filed conditions for any possible settlement (NRS-Y13-0748), including clear service responsibility, a defined boundary and the continuing Statehood Audit path. These filings are political contributions, not committee recommendations or an adopted Convention resolution. No boundary, final cost allocation, status change or referendum has been approved.
