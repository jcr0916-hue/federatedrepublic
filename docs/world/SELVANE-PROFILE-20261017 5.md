No present Governor, Deputy, Administrator, appointment deadline length or dated transition is invented.

Career independence does not authorize policy veto, and lawful refusal is distinguished from political disagreement.

Authorized programs, language services and statutory options are not asserted as newly implemented acts. Record publication is not presented as a universal condition of legal validity.
- **Amendment:** §§53–57 and 60 preserve the citizen proposal route (petition benchmark: ten percent of valid votes at the latest statewide general election; ratification: sixty percent), the legislative route (two-thirds proposal and majority ratification), ministerial ballot placement after certification and continuing amendability.
