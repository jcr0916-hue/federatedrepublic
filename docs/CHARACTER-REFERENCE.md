## MAREN SOLLIS — full entry, since this is the character that caused the error

Civics teacher, Ardenmere Secondary School, Corindal State. Corindal is landlocked, interior,
agricultural, and has been in **Provisional status for the entire span of her writing** —
audit failures over a contested electoral-district redraw, senators not yet seated.

**Two separate publications, same person, deliberately different registers:**
- **Dispatch** (`torenthia-dispatch-sollis.html`) — one long, single, accumulating file running
  Year 12 Month 9 through at least Year 13 Month 5. First-person, classroom-anecdote style,
  built around named recurring students (below). Personal, occasional, not tied to a single
  topic — whatever struck her as worth writing down that month.
- **Show Your Work** — a separate, more structured explainer blog she started later (first
  post: seq 71, Year 13 Month 9), one topic per post, still first-person and still opens from
  a classroom moment, but tighter and more focused than a Dispatch entry.

**Do not invent a second civics-teacher character. This is her, in either format.**

**Named recurring students (Dispatch):**
- **Deva** — introduced Year 12 Month 9 asking about unseated senators. Reads NRS entries
  independently, including routine ones nobody else reads. Sharp, direct.
- **Orren** — introduced Year 12 Month 11. Tracks the LC race closely (favored Sandris in the
  mock ballot), asks about constitutional mechanism with real precision. Appears in the Show
  Your Work rewrite of news-057 asking about "defined portion."
- **Tor** — quieter, speaks rarely. Identified the EM confirmation vote as more structurally
  important than the LC race — a genuinely sharp read Sollis flagged as unusual for his age.

## NAME-COLLISION WARNINGS (found in the 260831 audit — verify before writing)

**Two unrelated people share the surname Fenn.** They have never appeared in the same piece,
and "Senator Fenn" is currently unambiguous because only one is a senator — but this is a real
trap for future beats:
- **Aldous Fenn** — Senator, Caldenmere. Senate appointee to the Joint Committee on Fiscal
  Equalization.
- **Aleth Fenn** — journalist, The Torenthian, northern correspondent. Covers the Argent Ridge /
  Norvane thread specifically.

If both ever need to appear in the same piece, use full names throughout. Do not write a bare
"Fenn" in any piece that touches both the fiscal committee and the northern beat.

**Dara Voss changed outlets — this is intentional, not an error.** The Torenthian through
Year 13 Month 6 (seq 38, 41), then RNN from Month 9 onward (seq 64, 70, 79, 88, 93). Clean
chronological split with no reversions. **Do not "correct" this to a single outlet.** Contrast
with the Corwin Hale case (news-037), which was a genuine one-off mis-attribution against five
consistent pieces — that one was fixed 260822.

**RNN pieces before seq 46 have no byline, by design.** Every RNN piece through news-043 is
unattributed; Dara Voss appears from news-046 onward and is consistent thereafter. Reads as a
correspondent joining the beat. Not a metadata gap to backfill.

## RE-EXTRACTING / MAINTAINING THIS FILE

This was built by cross-referencing `worldAuthor` frontmatter (mechanical, reliable) against
manual name-frequency greps for in-world characters (judgment-based, needs periodic re-check).
When adding a new named character:

1. **Grep first:** `grep -l "Full Name" torenthia-*.html` — if it returns hits you didn't expect,
   stop and check them before writing anything new.
2. **Check outlet consistency** if the character is a journalist — one mismatched outlet
   attribution (Hale/Dorven Ledger, this session) is easy to miss without checking every file.
3. Add the new character here immediately, don't defer it.
