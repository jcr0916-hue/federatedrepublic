## Method and scope

The [per-file register](constitutional-compatibility-261023.json) inventories all 54 root scenarios and 253 Torenthia pages, with baseline and reviewed source hashes, classification, references and editorial decisions. Related live explainers, discovery metadata, API authorities and authoring guidance are included as dependencies. Every corpus file has a decision, including unchanged files.

The audit compared authority, decision-maker, scope, threshold, timing, consequences and remedies against the candidate. Full-text clause, obligation and terminology screening was followed by mechanism-focused reading and linked-record reconciliation. Identifier validity alone does not certify substantive compatibility. State-law citations remain in their own context. Dated constitutional editions and earlier audit notes are historical evidence rather than current guidance.

Classifications are compatible, citation correction, substantive repair, replacement and historical reference. Mixed cases receive the more substantial classification. The completed register records 338 decisions: 190 compatible, 19 citation corrections, 128 substantive repairs and one replacement. Counts include dependencies; they are not counts of newly published stories.

## Editorial decisions and completed batches

1. **Military authority and the replacement scenario.** Replaced the removed §14.3 second-renewal/reconstruction-plan mechanism with **The Changed Mission**, retaining `scenario-the-second-renewal.html`, Halvern, Josk and the Year 6–12 sequence. Senate operational review, legislative authorization of expanded scope, Assembly-originated appropriations and Legat command are distinct. Immediate defense may cross the border when necessary; notification is without undue delay. No invented constitutional annual renewal, 48-hour decision, 180-day report or universal 90-day withdrawal allowance remains. The exact superseded scenario and its provenance are preserved in [the scenario archive](../archive/scenarios/constitution-261023/README.md).
2. **Statehood, remediation and restoration.** All three Monitor findings complete the audit record; affirmative legislative determinations decide compliance. Territorial proceedings initially belong to the Assembly; existing-State matters and separate Provisional placement initially belong to the Senate. Deadline expiry transfers authority exclusively before judicial enforcement, without a default merits result. Timely audit completion survives decision delay. The Audit, Third Strike and Restoration were substantively reconciled, including separate Provisional placement, no compulsory devolution referendum, two qualifying biennial determinations for restoration and an explicit alternative Valdrath timeline.
3. **Korda and linked Torenthia records.** Kept petition identities, dates, the 33 delegates, the court's 6–3 lineup/outcome and the Northern Lake Settlement's 28–5 proposal pending ratification. Recorded the necessary same-date Assembly viability determination on the existing 2–1 JMC evidence, without inventing an Assembly tally. JMC assessments inform rather than permit constitutional action. Elections Panel and Civic Consul duties arise independently when certified competing petitions trigger the Convention rule in §15.5.a(4). Corrected the receiving State to Kelvant and reconciled the dependent news, NRS, court, profile and homepage summaries together.
4. **Other Torenthia chronology.** Solara's first audit now includes its Assembly compliance determination; Statehood still requires the later qualifying audit and decision. Corindal's Year 9 Provisional status precedes its Year 11 procurement case. Rehn's Year 10–14 term remains four years; obsolete Year 12 expiry and renewal assumptions were removed. NRS-005's inconsistent Day 7 completion date was reconciled to the Day 29 date used by the established downstream fiscal calendar. Historical fiscal reporting periods, public-financing allocations and founding-treaty phases remain identified as statutory, administrative or treaty choices. Existing State Constitutions were not amended.
5. **Rights, executive continuity and legislative independence.** Removed emergency-renewal/reset reasoning under §1.16, obsolete public-trial sealing limits, prior-year funding floors under §3.10 and legislative extensions of executive incapacity. Recurrence within 90 days requires two-thirds; each incapacity determination lasts at most six months and continuation needs a fresh determination. Succession and declination follow the current numbering and actual acquisition of authority. General federal-statute or electoral-map challenges no longer acquire Supreme Court original jurisdiction merely from their subject.
6. **Monitors, courts, elections and pools.** Separated audit evidence from institutional decision-making; corrected external approval of suspensions, removal routes and the absence of invented turnout or missed-deadline dismissal rules. Distinguished ordinary Elections Panel lotteries from the JMC vacancy fallback. Corrected pool minimum, eligibility and judicial-review paragraphs, NRS audit intervals, electoral finance permissions and politically neutral communication access. Naturalization proficiency applies to military applicants; asylum adjudication belongs to independent courts, and court-granted residency is distinct from protection against removal.
7. **Fiscal guarantees, direct democracy and territorial relations.** Preserved the enforceable substance of social guarantees, sustainability duties, Endowment funding and equalization capacity. Removed obsolete monetary-deficit thresholds and automatic pay penalties. Statutory response periods do not create a right to a preferred policy remedy. Updated repeal, initiative and amendment eligibility, thresholds and timing. Preserved compatible compact notice terms and founding-treaty schedules without presenting them as universal constitutional rules; transition capability is institution-specific.
8. **Reader tools and authoring dependencies.** Rewrote all twelve quicksheets and seven diagram views; refreshed the glossary, current design-principles supplement, world story bible/status, scenario bridges, homepage updates, Navigator deterministic authorities/retrieval and benchmark rubrics. Removed stale §14.3 discovery routes. Browser checks caught and repaired same-page diagram hash navigation, obsolete panel handlers and an Escape-search handler referencing a removed menu. The PDF renderers accept an optional local browser executable while retaining their ordinary CI default. Print styling keeps inline glossary terms in normal flow; all 96 quicksheet headings/body paragraphs match the PDF text in order.
9. **Generated outputs, archives and durable checks.** Regenerated/verified annotated text, Markdown, quick reference and search assets from the unchanged candidate. Rendered the Constitution and all twelve quicksheet PDFs. Archived the exact previous main JSON/Markdown pair as `Constitution-261024.*`; the archive date is UTC, while the editorial release date is 23 October in Los Angeles.
