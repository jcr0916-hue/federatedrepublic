# About the Federated Republic

The Federated Republic is a universal constitutional framework designed for societies seeking to establish or reform democratic government.

It provides a complete, adaptable system of government built around individual sovereignty, democratic accountability, institutional independence, transparent public administration, and the distribution of political authority.

The framework is designed to function under ordinary conditions and withstand political disagreement, institutional failure, and constitutional crisis.

It is intended not as the constitution of one particular nation, but as a foundation that different peoples may adopt and adapt to their own circumstances.

The Constitution itself begins with Article I. This description is explanatory project material, **not** an operative preamble or constitutional provision. Torenthia is a fictional implementation and testing environment, not the founding author of the framework.
