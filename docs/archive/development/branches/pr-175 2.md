## Purpose
Review Article XII (Social State and Economic Rights) with John one provision at a time and apply the result, with a conforming change to §9.6. Follows PR #174 (merged). Guiding rule: make it possible for the government to function; do not solve every imaginable case.

## Decisions and lessons
- The 90-day Legislature response in §12.6 stays constitutional because statute would otherwise set the deadline for the Legislature's own response; the MA's 30-day clause in §12.1.b was cut because §9.5.a covers it.
- The MA certifies the other protected bodies' funding floors and the EM certifies the MA's, so no body certifies its own.
- §12.5's second sentence stays: Article III gives the Legislature no enumerated commerce power.
- The §9.6 heading still reads "Monitor Minimum Funding Guarantee" although it covers the Panels and the MA; left as is.
