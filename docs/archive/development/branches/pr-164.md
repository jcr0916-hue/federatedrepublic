## Decisions and lessons
- Term limits are a plain count (6 Assembly, 2 Senate); partial-term counting is left to statute. §7.4(3) still makes early departure count as a completed term.
- Recall: States (and Territories, for Assembly members) may establish it under §3.2(6); no executive, administrative body or court may alone recall. §7.9's second sentence ("no additional mechanism … including popular recall … by statute") was cut because it could be read to bar §3.2(6).
- New Senate vacancy rule in §3.5 (State provides; period by statute, max 90 days), because §2.9 vacates a Senate seat when the Speaker succeeds the Legat Consul.
- §3.6.a folded into §3.6; trade agreements need an absolute majority of the full seated membership of both chambers. The Senate exclusive-authority list in §3.5 was cut (each function is assigned in its own provision).
- **Rejected:** an LM petition to the SC for failure to legislate. The LM observes and reports only; failure to legislate is a political question, so §3.7 paragraph 2 and §3.1's "indefinitely delay" sentence were cut.
- §3.11 keeps NRS publication of delegated exercises (§10.1(2) does not clearly reach acts made under a statute); its EM audit sentence was cut because §9.1.b already covers the executives.
- §1.1 now covers any violation of rights "whether by act or omission", so inaction can be challenged (relevant to Elections Panel failure).
- **Reuse:** when text drops a citation, check tests and fixtures that relied on it (PR #157 needed three test edits and the f16 fixture). Generated files regenerate through `npm run sync`; never hand-merge them.
