## Revision 3 (John, 2026-11-18): ask a desk
John wants to ask a desk what is going on in its jurisdiction and who the players are, and hear its own viewpoint rather than the editor's.
- `api/newsroom-ask.js` (`/api/newsroom-ask?desk=<id>&q=<question>`): the desk answers in its own voice from its brief, sources, notes, assignments and the story status and bible, with established, approved, proposed and open material labeled. Planner model (DeepSeek V4 Pro). Answers only on protected `*.vercel.app` hosts (Deployment Protection), 404 on the public domain; files nothing, so it never trips the desk's six-hour filing guard.
- `api/_newsroom-core.js` exports the loaders and prompt builders it reuses; `vercel.json` build and route; `scripts/newsroom-ask.test.mjs` (3 tests), run with `test:newsroom-run`.

## Revision 4 (John, 2026-11-18): desks declare, a desk editor sorts
John: desks produce, the Central Editor filters and edits, John and the editor discuss and publish; the Central Editor shouldn't go back to writing. Canon candidates must be precise, ideas needn't be, records can be anything a government would record. Desks have the canon, so the desk editor needs only what's going on now and how much attention each piece needs, which the desks declare.
- **Declarations.** Every submission names its `track` (canon, idea or record) and `attention` (importance, time-sensitive, touches, why now). The runner writes them as a tag at the start of the summary (`attentionTag`), so the inbox list shows them without a Worker change. `NEWSROOM-RULES.md` gains "Declare what you are submitting": the standard follows the track.
- **Desk editor.** `api/_newsroom-triage.js` + `api/newsroom-triage.js`, schedule `newsroom-triage` at 16:37 UTC (Bearer `CRON_SECRET`). Claude Haiku 5.5 (`roster.triage`) reads the last 26 hours of non-test filings via the editor key, 2,500-character excerpts of canon filings and the story status (no canon), ranks canon for three editor slots, routes ideas and records, flags, and files its triage as desk `desk-editor` with the runner key. Filings it fails to sort are listed as "unsorted", never dropped.
- **Records desks write with Flash alone** (planner null), about 1.5¢ a batch.
- `callModel` takes `json: false` for models called without `response_format`.
- `docs/torenthia/IDEA-BOARD.md` started; `NEWSROOM.md` flow updated; `scripts/newsroom-triage.test.mjs` (4 tests).
