# Branch history — fix/annotator-voice

**PR:** [#371](https://github.com/jcr0916-hue/federatedrepublic/pull/371)

## Purpose
After #370 John read a live annotation (§2.6) and objected to its voice: it explained what the text and sources do not say ("does not itself state why…", "the supplied sources record no further rationale"), cited its own material by log number and date, and read like a history paper.

## Shipped in this PR
- `api/_annotator-core.js`: new VOICE rule (plain colleague-to-reader prose; recorded history worked into the explanation without attribution; no commentary on what is missing; no scholarly apparatus). Removed the instructions that required attributing history to the change log with its date, the "no rationale recorded" sentence and the "defined elsewhere" wording. Structural inferences are now phrased as what the mechanism does rather than as labeled inferences. The anti-invention rules stay: no invented motives, rules, remedies, provision numbers or cross-reference effects.
- Packet: history entries are labeled `[HISTORY n]`; the empty case reads `(none)`.
- Tests updated; the cache revision hashes the annotator code, so cached annotations regenerate after merge.

## Added after #372 merged
- #372's first test batch ran against the old rules (#371 was still a draft): outputs cited the log and packet, several ran over 525 words, and one hit the output limit. This branch now also merges main and makes the generator retry (up to 3 attempts, larger token budget after the first) until the output passes the quality gate, reporting `attempts` and any remaining `issues`. Tests added.

## Follow-ups
- John floated a cheaper drafter (DeepSeek) with Sonnet checking. Recommendation sent separately: pre-generate and store annotations (nothing is cached server-side today; every reader click calls the model), which makes per-reader cost zero and leaves room to review quality before publishing.
