# Branch history — newsroom/continuity-system

**PR:** [#355](https://github.com/jcr0916-hue/federatedrepublic/pull/355)

## Purpose
John wants delegated systems to carry the continuity work so the Central Editor can focus on the whole project: an election tracker that keeps the cast in line and shows coverage gaps and balance, built as a general mechanism instead of an election-only desk.

## Shipped in this PR
- **Cast registry** `_data/worldRegistry.json` (seeded from the Story Bible and published bylines: people, outlets, avoided names, a name-collision group, threads) and `lib/world-registry.mjs`. `npm run check:world-registry` (in `world:publish-check`, warnings only) checks records changed on a branch for avoided names, bylines at the wrong outlet, unregistered bylines and outlets (noting first names one letter from an existing one), bare surnames when both members of a collision group appear, and locked-pronoun contradictions. `--all`, `--since N`, `--evidence` and `--strict` are available. Pronoun locks (Thoss, Carrow, Mak, Orin she; Vael, Sandris, Rehn he) were seeded from published text counts and are open to John's correction; Aleth Fenn is deliberately not locked.
- **Coverage report** `lib/world-coverage.mjs`, `scripts/world-coverage.mjs`, generated `docs/torenthia/COVERAGE.md` (`npm run world:coverage -- --write`): exact counts for the last 20 stories and 40 NRS records, thread and person recency, and flags. On first run it shows The Torenthian at 40% of recent stories, no coverage of Merath or Rhovane, and none of the four Legat Consul candidates named in the last ten stories.
- **Election Tracker desk** (`docs/torenthia/agents/election-tracker/`: brief, seeded `ELECTION-TRACKER.md`, notes, assignments). Mondays, files kind `report`, planner off, ends 2027-02-28. Seeded only from published records, each cited.
- **Runner:** optional `until` date on a desk (`withinTerm` in `api/_newsroom-core.js`, honored by scheduled and manual runs and by off-week tasking). **Triage:** `triage.passThroughDesks` sends listed desks' reports straight to the Central Editor under a `tracker` section at the top, without a model call; a day with only tracker reports needs no model call.
- **Planning:** `STORY-STATUS.md` records the mid-January 2027 real-world target and the Year 14 Month 3 plan as internal planning, not canon, with no date advance. A Federal assignment asks for a pitch on how the Elections Panel's notice could settle the open scheduling questions.
- Tests: registry and coverage unit tests (`test:world-registry`), until and tracker pass-through tests in the newsroom suites. `npm run world:publish-check` passed on the branch.
