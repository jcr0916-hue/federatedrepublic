# Selvane State Historical Test 01 — The Appointment No One Could Block

## Scope
Add proposed canonical State constitutional historical test in Year 10, Month 3.

## Source provisions
- §7: independent Civil Service Commission, certified public pools and merit functions.
- §28: Chief Administrator nomination, confirmation, two shortlists and public-lot fallback after four affirmative rejections.
- §31: distinguishes affirmative rejection from silence and provides anti-inaction appointment rule.
- §64: authorized-membership denominator not diminished by vacant Assembly seats.

## Editorial choices
- New historical actors Governor Adela Renn and administrator Leren Voss; no claim they are established elsewhere.
- Does not name the number of Assembly seats, statutory nomination time limits or precise voting counts.
- Emphasizes public lot from **second** certified shortlist after two affirmative rejections from each shortlist; never treats rejection as deemed confirmation.
- Distinguishes professional administration from elected policy authority.
- Does not affect the current Year 14 Month 1 plot, candidate races or federal constitutional timelines.
