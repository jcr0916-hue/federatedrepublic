## Purpose
Record two of John's design decisions as working amendments: (2) change §12.1's education sentence from "Parents" to "Parents or guardians", so children who are wards of the State or in guardianship are covered; (3) reword the first sentence of §12.2(2) to drop "over the long term" and the ambiguous "sustain … public debt".

## Shipped in this PR (on merge)
- `docs/WORKING-CONSTITUTIONAL-AMENDMENTS.md`: new "Proposed amendment 2" (§12.1) and "Proposed amendment 3" (§12.2(2)), each with exact wording, scope, rationale, rejected alternatives and downstream updates; Updated date set to 23 November 2026.

## Decisions
- Recorded as a working amendment, not applied to the adopted text: the file's own rule is that adopted text is not modified silently and that settled proposals reach the Constitution in a release.
- Wording chosen by John: "parent or guardian", matching §5.3. Rejected: "a child's guardian" alone (can read as removing parents), "legal custodian" (custody-law narrow), "caregiver" (no legal standing). The proposal was first offered as "a child's parent or legal guardian"; John chose the plural form that matches §5.3's phrase.

- Amendment 3: "The Republic conducts its fiscal affairs to keep its public finances, including public debt, sustainable while fulfilling its constitutional obligations." Chosen because "over the long term" has no anchor and "sustain … public debt" can read as keeping the debt going. Rejected: moving "over the long term", a numeric horizon (over-specification, as in amendment 1), "responsibly" (vaguer), and a negative duty. Added to this PR, not a separate one, because both entries live in the same file and neither is merged yet.
