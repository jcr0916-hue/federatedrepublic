## Decisions and lessons
- No single branch holds classification power: executives classify under standards they publish, statute prevails, and a court tests every withheld portion. The old list of banned motives was replaced by one affirmative standard (national security) because none of the banned motives is national security.
- The "void" rule was dropped with the motives list. Improper classification now buys time only until someone with standing petitions; the EM's access and assessment, staff minority reports, and the stated-ground record surface abuse. Existence of a classified record is not published; the EM's unrestricted access covers records whose existence is secret.
- Domestic criminal proceedings, personal data, and ordinary deliberative records are not classified; they are protected by §1.13 sealing, §1.8, and the fact that only constitutional acts must be published.
- **Reuse:** a conforming-edit helper that replaces every occurrence will rewrite text you later assert on; run assertion-bearing steps before the bulk replace, or assert counts first.
