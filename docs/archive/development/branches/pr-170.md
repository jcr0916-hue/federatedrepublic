## Decisions and lessons
- Party-to-candidate transfers are deliberately left unsettled: the express bar was narrowed so the permission sentence carries the default, leaving room for a future government to allow limited transfers. The same applies to unions and associations.
- Disqualification for foreign assistance is pre-certification only, so §7.2(7) finality stays universal; afterward the Legislature's removal power applies.
- §8.3 governs access and price on public resources, not speech. Government-run channels and official accounts are governed by §8.2(3); private websites are not public resources and are outside the section. "Free" access was dropped; §8.4's allocation is what pays for reasonable-rate access.
- **Reuse:** when a cloud PDF check fails on text that is in `constitution_data.json`, first confirm the scratch site copy was refreshed from the latest `_site` (a shell that died on `pkill -f` matching its own command line left a stale copy). Stop servers by port (`ss -ltnp`), not `pkill -f`.
