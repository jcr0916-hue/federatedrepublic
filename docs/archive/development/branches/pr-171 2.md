## Decisions and lessons
- One removal standard for Monitor Generals and other officers: serious misconduct, abuse of office, or permanent incapacity; "bad faith" was undefined and dropped.
- Filing a removal against a Monitor General does not suspend authority; suspension exists only through §9.7.a (misconduct) and §9.1.e (incapacity), both decided by the other two Monitor Generals.
- Both Monitor nomination tracks end in a JMC lottery when the nominating body fails to act within 60 days.
- **Reuse:** trimming a provision can drop it out of navigator retrieval ranking (the f37 case lost §9.5.a). Fix with a routing term in `api/_navigator-core.js`, not by editing the fixture.
