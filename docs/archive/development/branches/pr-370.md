# Branch history — fix/annotator-recorded-history

**PR:** [#370](https://github.com/jcr0916-hue/federatedrepublic/pull/370)

## Purpose
John reported that the annotated Constitution is "missing context." Two symptoms: it says there is no explanation for a provision when the project has one, and for a provision that relies on a term defined in another provision (the Civic Consul's domain depends on the Legat Consul's) it says the term is "presumably defined somewhere else."

## Root causes
1. The annotator's source packet held only the current constitutional text, and its prompt forbade any claim about drafting history. The project's recorded reasoning (the Constitutional History change log) never reached it, so for most provisions it truthfully reported nothing.
2. Related provisions were chosen only from the same Article by explicit citation or word overlap. A term defined in another provision was missed. For §2.5 the packet picked §2.4 and §2.16 and left out §2.1, where the Legat Consul's domain is defined.

## Shipped in this PR
- `scripts/build-annotation-history.cjs` builds `annotation-history.json` from the two history pages: 94 dated entries from May 30, 2026 on, each with the exact §-numbers it cites (145 of 177 provisions have at least one entry). `npm run annotation-history` rebuilds it; `--check` runs inside `npm run test:annotator` and fails with that instruction when the history pages change and the JSON is stale.
- `api/_annotator-core.js`: new `RECORDED DESIGN HISTORY` packet section (up to three most recent entries citing the target's number, clipped, with date labels; an explicit "no rationale recorded" line otherwise); new defining-provision lookup that pulls the provision whose name defines a term the target uses, across Articles (up to two, ranked by how distinctive the term is); related limit stays at 4 (defining provisions take priority over same-Article word-overlap relatives).
- SYSTEM prompt: the model may report recorded reasons and rejected alternatives, attributed to the change log and its date label; when none is supplied it may say only that the supplied sources record no rationale, never that no explanation exists; it must use a supplied defining provision and may not call a definition "presumed."
- `scripts/build-search-index.py`: `CONSTITUTION_REVISION` now also hashes `api/_annotator-core.js` and `annotation-history.json`, so cached annotations in readers' browsers regenerate after this merges and after any later change to what the annotator is given. The client is unchanged.
- Four new tests in `scripts/annotator.test.cjs`.

## Known limits
- Log entries before May 30 are excluded: they use the numbering from before the May 29 reorder, so their section numbers point at different provisions now.
- The log shows "529" in place of real section numbers from its first commit on. Those sentences are dropped, not guessed.
- Entries from mid-June and September may cite numbers that have since moved. The prompt tells the model to use an entry only when its subject matches the target text.
- One entry (h045, "v177 Fine Detail Review") has no date in its tag; it inherits June 19 and is flagged `dateApprox`.
- The AI gateway is unreachable from the workspace, so live model output was not tested. Spot-check a few provisions after merge (suggested: §2.5, §2.16, §9.3, §3.6).

## Follow-ups
- A fuller reconstruction of early (pre-May 30) reasoning belongs with the evolution-index work, which can supply a mapping from old to current numbers.
