# Merath historical Test 01 — The Promise at the Foundry Gate

## Scope
New proposed canonical State Constitutional Historical Test, Merath Year 9 Month 6.

## Constitutional basis

- §2.5: any two Councillors may convene; majority decision and publication of Council votes.
- §2.6: Chair presides and communicates but cannot independently bind Council.
- §2.11: departmental lawful implementation is distinct from Council policy decisions.

## Editorial choices

- Chair's public commitment does not become a binding Council act by apparent authority or retroactive clarification.
- No existing Year 14 news timeline or current industrial inspection schedule modified.
- Test is canon only if the editorial PR is approved and merged.
