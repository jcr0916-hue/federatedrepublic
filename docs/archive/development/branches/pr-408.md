## Purpose
John observed that §9.4.a (Judicial Pool eligibility) would sit better in Article IV, and that §9.8 (the pool framework) would sit better in Article XI, since the Elections Panel manages pools. After weighing two layouts he chose design A: criteria with the office the pool feeds, machinery with the Panel.

## Decisions
- Moved, not rewritten: no wording change beyond the three cross-references, so the move carries no change of meaning.
- Article IX keeps its existing numbering gaps (no §9.6, now no §9.8) rather than renumbering later provisions, which would have forced citation changes for §9.9 and §9.9.a for no gain.
- Left as written on purpose, because they record what was true when written: older Constitutional History entries and the archive, `docs/operations/constitutional-compatibility-261023.json` (frozen audit register), `docs/CONSTITUTIONAL-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md`, `docs/ESSAY-OUTLINE-TWELVE-TESTS.md`, `docs/AI-MODEL-ROUTING-PLAN.md`, `docs/torenthia/ELECTION-PUBLICATION-20261027.md`.
