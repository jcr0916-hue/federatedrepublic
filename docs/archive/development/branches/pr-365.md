# Branch history — newsroom/story-clock

**PR:** [#365](https://github.com/jcr0916-hue/federatedrepublic/pull/365)

## Purpose
John asked the Central Editor to own the clock because he cannot track every storyline and due date (fiscal equalization, the election, the Korda vote, Argent Ridge and others). This adds one master page that puts the in-world clocks and the real-world calendar together.

## Shipped in this PR
- `docs/torenthia/STORY-CLOCK.md`: frame (fictional date, mid-January target, pace, capacity), the five threads that must finish before the election vote, background threads with their due dates, three decisions for John with defaults, and slips to watch.
- `docs/torenthia/agents/DESK-CLOCK.md` and `desks.json` (`sharedSources`): a desk-facing clock that every desk reads each run. John's direction: the desks choose how a thread moves, but it must reach its decision point on the clock. It carries in-world timing windows only, with no real-world target and no planned outcome.
- `docs/torenthia/NEWSROOM.md`: a short "Story clock" section.

## Design choices
- The page adds sequencing and decisions. Published facts stay in `STORY-STATUS.md` and `_data/worldClocks.json`; nothing here is canon.
- The three decisions come with defaults, so John only needs to speak if he disagrees. Those defaults are Korda after the election, EM confirmation in Month 3 on the same ballot, and no Argent Ridge repeal endpoint until after the election.
- The pace figure (about three real weeks per in-world month) is an observation from Year 13 Month 12 (October 10) to Year 14 Month 2 (November 21), marked approximate.
