## Desk mandate
John clarified that a desk is the voice of its jurisdiction, not only a newsroom. The shared rules and every brief now say so: a desk speaks for its jurisdiction's whole civic life (government, courts, elections, records, public opinion, press, businesses and ordinary people) and develops its storylines. The qualified TypingMind prompts are source material, not templates. The Federal desk leaves Monitor audit work to the Monitors desk.

## Not carried over
The TypingMind operating parts: qualification tests, the VP role, the training period, text labels in the body (the inbox `kind` field covers them), and file-avoidance rules (protected planning files are never in a desk's prompt).
