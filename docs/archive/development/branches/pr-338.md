The shared rules and every brief now say so: a desk speaks for its jurisdiction's whole civic life (government, courts, elections, records, public opinion, press, businesses and ordinary people) and develops its storylines. The qualified TypingMind prompts are source material, not templates. The Federal desk leaves Monitor audit work to the Monitors desk.
