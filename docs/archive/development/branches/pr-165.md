## Decisions and lessons
- Removal is a political act with a plain-language standard: serious misconduct, abuse of office, or permanent incapacity, with the Legislature's determination final. "Constitutional breach" was never defined and is gone from §2.13, §4.2 and §4.3.a. No Monitor finding is prerequisite. Inferior judges keep a statutory discipline track on conduct- and competence-based grounds.
- No mandatory retirement age (lifespans may change). The 18-month new-seat expiry, the abolished-court placement rule and the State specialized courts sentence were cut.
- Nomination clocks: one 90-day cap for inferior and Supreme Court nominations; the Senate has up to 120 days; no deemed confirmation.
- The bar on nominating the Temporary Associate Justice was cut: the justice holds the seat by chance of seniority, and the bar existed only to remove a shadow of impropriety.
- §4.5(1): the sentence "No political actor may override a constitutional ruling" was cut. Courts may declare acts unconstitutional and void; "override" was undefined. §4.5 was not renumbered because §4.5(4), (7), §11.3, §13.2, §16.2(6) and §17.3 cite it.
- §4.5(4) gains an exception where the Constitution expressly confers standing.
- `scenario-the-cause` described an election-window Supreme Court review that §4.3.a no longer contained; the section was rewritten around the final legislative determination and the two-thirds threshold.
- **Reuse:** when text drops a citation, check tests and fixtures that relied on it (here, navigator fixture f09). Run `npm run sync` on a branch cut from fresh main so the archive pair is made against main.
