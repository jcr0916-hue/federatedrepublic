## Decisions and lessons
- John's 2-year idea became a 20-month cap, which equals the initiative's old two 10-month phases and shortens the longest a law can stay in force before a repeal vote.
- Lapse at the cap is not a "failed referendum", so the three-year bar does not apply.
- The Second Path scenario now has the Panel approve the filing as a threshold check; the Supreme Court still strikes the appropriation after the vote, because filing review does not foreclose a §4.5 dispute.
- Silence on whether the Legislature may promptly repeal an enacted initiative was flagged and left.

## Open follow-ups
- §17.1(3) says amendment initiatives follow "the same two-phase petition process as §13.2"; that now includes the 20-month limit and filing steps, and the Article XVII review should confirm that is intended.
- Article XIV onward continues the same article-by-article process.
