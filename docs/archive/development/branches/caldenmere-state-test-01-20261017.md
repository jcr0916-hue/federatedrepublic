# Caldenmere State Constitutional Historical Test 01 — The Minister Neither Could Appoint Alone

## Scope
Proposed new canonical Year 9 Month 10 test of Caldenmere's divided executive, after review of current State Constitution and existing State historical test format.

## Provisions
- State §§2.3(d), 2.8: Governor formally appoints State Ministers upon Senate President recommendation, and final directives and appointments are published.
- §2.4: Senate President holds administrative executive power.
- §2.5: supervision and Administrative Decrees may only implement existing statute, not replace constitutional appointment authority.
- §3.9: publication to Caldenmere Registry.

## Editorial decisions
- New historical figures Corlan Vey, Selda Marr and Mara Tellen, proposed only, not claimed as existing established figures.
- Avoid implying either the Governor can select a Minister alone or Senate President can make a recommended candidate a Minister by decree.
- Court addresses legal effect rather than selecting a candidate; final appointment still requires the Governor and administrative supervision remains with Senate President.
- No changes to the existing federal seat bidding historical episode or Year 14 Torenthia coverage.
