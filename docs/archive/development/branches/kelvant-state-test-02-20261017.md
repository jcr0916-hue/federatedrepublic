# Kelvant State Test 02 — A Year for the Shore

## Scope
Proposed canonical Year 11 Month 5 constitutional history of the State Service and Militia Act; statute and judicial decision are presented **within historical narrative**, not an independently enacted statute file. Keep separate from Kelvant Test 01 and current Year 14 coverage.

## Constitutional sources reviewed
Federal constitution:
- §1.4 exception for lawful military conscription, which is not a blanket authorization for State action.
- §§1.5–1.6 liberty, conscience, non-discrimination, age distinctions and review.
- §14.5 State forces allowed for disaster response, civil emergency and preservation of public order within State; federal call-up separately controlled, no action against constitutional order, federal equipment/training/composition standards by statute.
- §3.10 residual powers and federal supremacy where authorized.
Kelvant constitution:
- §§1.3–1.5 equality/conscience/organized militia, Governor commander-in-chief and subordination to civil authority.
- §1.7 due process.
- §2.3 Governor command and constitutional department-head appointment distinction.
- §2.9 activation must state basis, purpose, geography and duration; activation does not confer police authority; federal service separate.
- §§3.8 and 3.13 ordinary bicameral legislation and statutory militia limits.
- §7.1 appropriations, and §7.5 independent audit available.

## Story decisions
- Paid 12-month full-time or equivalent part-time duty; equivalent flexible scheduling available irrespective of college enrollment.
- Noncombatant roles and individualized accommodation, fitness exclusions, written reasons and review.
- No political/religious loyalty tests, no compelled expression, no uncompensated private-party labor.
- Ordinary promotion/retention through professional chain, Governor may recommend but cannot make position; honorary medals no command entitlement.
- Force funded and capped by statute, career posts applied for not guaranteed, Governor retains constitutional command.
- Court sustains law *as applied under specified safeguards* rather than finding compulsory service categorically immune from review. Federal rights remain applicable.
- New Governor Elian Rusk and case facts are proposed canon only on merge, not previously established historical facts.
- No change to federal Year 14 chronology.
