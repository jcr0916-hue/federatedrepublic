## Decisions and lessons
- The Civic Consul stays as last-resort designator of Acting panel members: limited to the panel's own civil servants and never a majority. A JMC lottery was the considered alternative.
- The cure period is deliberately uncapped; withholding certification is the safeguard when it runs past an election.
- §3.9's removal standard for panel members ("conduct, incapacity, or incompetence") was noticed but left alone, as it belongs to the earlier Article III review.
- The NVS sharing rule is now purpose-based because the old ban conflicted with Monitor audits, court review and NRS publication.
