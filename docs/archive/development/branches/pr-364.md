## Purpose
John wants fiscal equalization (§12.6) settled in the story before the election, passing so that Thoss comes out with somewhat stronger Assembly support. The thread has run for months in-world, but nothing tracked it as one storyline. This PR adds a thread tracker desk, modeled on the Election Tracker, so the thread moves through published records and nobody has to hold it in their head.

## Design choices
- The desk proposes and never decides. No outcome, date, amount, weight or vote is set by the desk; a step counts only when a published record says so. The fictional date stays Year 14, Month 2.
- The editorial plan (passes with a price; Executive Monitor audit left for after the election; passage is not a promise of Assembly votes) is internal and kept in the tracker file, not written as something a character knows.
- Seeded facts come from published records only. Items that still need verifying (Sandris's stance in news 025; Mak's endorsement of the Morantine-Autonomous remedy plan) are listed for the desk's first run.
