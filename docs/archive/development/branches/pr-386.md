## Purpose
Correct statements I made in the amendment release (#383, #384) that said the Constitution no longer requires any response to a Monitor finding under §12.6. It does: §9.5.a requires a body or officer subject to a Monitor report to publish a response to the NRS within a statutory period, and the response need not agree with the findings.

- The adopted constitutional text is unchanged.

## Decisions
- John's principle stands: Monitors must not compel action. The §9.5.a duty is to answer in public; the response may disagree, and nothing in it compels a remedy.
- Whether the body subject to an equalization audit is the Legislature or the administering agencies is not settled by the text. The corrected statements say so and do not pick one.
- In-world records are untouched: the ninety-day clock was already statutory, and the Torenthia notes ("established by the equalization reporting statute, not by the Constitution; §12.6 requires submission") remain accurate.

## Open question for John
Whether to leave §9.5.a as is (recommended: it is transparency only) or tighten it so the Legislature is expressly outside the "body subject to a Monitor report" for §12.6 audits. No change made.
