# Branch history — newsroom/desk-briefs

**PR:** [#337](https://github.com/jcr0916-hue/federatedrepublic/pull/337)

## Purpose
Brief every planned desk for the scheduled newsroom, all disabled. John or the Central Editor enables each desk once the inbox accepts it and its first output is reviewed.

## Shipped in this PR
- Briefs, assignments and notes for 12 States (Arvane, Orath, Merath, Corindal, Varek, Harren, Aldenmere, Caldenmere, Kelvant, Selvane, Rhovane, Norvane), 4 Territories (Korda keeps its qualified 01 instructions; Solara, Morantine, Verdmont) and 2 institution desks (Monitors; Partisan Media with Druse, Varn and Marek).
- Each State desk reads its published profile and its State constitution; Korda reads its compact and editorial context; Partisan Media reads two published columns as examples.
- Default cadence for the new desks is every two weeks on one weekday, staggered. Federal is unchanged.
- Each brief's identity is drawn from published profiles and the story bible, and every name was checked against the repository.

## Verification
13 offline newsroom tests pass, including a new check that every desk's files exist. A dry run measured each desk's prompt at 87k–160k planner characters and 240k–313k writer characters, an estimated 4–5 cents per run.

## Known limitations
- Verdmont, Solara and Morantine have no compact text in the repository; their approved compact references are not established here.
- The production inbox Worker accepts only the Federal, Korda, Varek and Corindal desks. The other desks need an inbox change before they can be enabled.

Results live on `main` once merged. **Deletion approval:** not granted by this note.
