## Purpose
The Election Tracker desk's writer call hit the fixed 105 s limit and failed. Give desks a way to set their own writer time limit and give the Election Tracker a longer one.

## Shipped in this PR (on merge)
- `api/_newsroom-core.js`: `writerTimeoutMs(desk, hasPlanner)` replaces the hard-coded 105 s writer timeout. Default unchanged. A desk's `timeoutMs` is floored at 30 s and capped to fit the 300 s function limit: 270 s with no planner; with a planner, what remains after its 175 s (so planner desks are unchanged).
- `docs/torenthia/agents/desks.json`: Election Tracker `timeoutMs: 200000`. No other desk changed.
- `scripts/newsroom-run.test.mjs`: two tests (default, junk, floor, both caps; the Election Tracker's setting and the failure message naming the desk's limit).

## Decisions
- 200 s rather than the 270 s maximum: it is nearly double the old limit, and the desk is a Monday report with no planner. The cap leaves room to go higher without another code change.
- Model, output limit, sources and schedule left alone.

## Results and verification
`npm run test:newsroom-run`: 46 of 46 pass. The full publish check was not run; the change is limited to the newsroom runner and roster.

## Known limitations
