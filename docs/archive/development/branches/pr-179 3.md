## Decisions and lessons
- John's view: the military article leaned toward over-specification. Process checks (authorization, 12-month renewals, notification, appropriation, audit, the Senate plan) do the work; content lists invite pretext and loophole reasoning, so only the two exclusive purposes remain.
- Civilian reconstruction after a war runs through the §14.3 plan; force may not be used to install or hold up a government because it is not an authorized purpose.
- Consul disputes over §14.1 use the existing §2.14 and §4.5(5) route; no new standing clause was added.
- Treaty ratification by the Senate stays sufficient authorization for treaty-based action, now limited to §14.1 purposes and 12 months.
