# Arvane State Historical Test 01 — The Rule That Ran Out

## Scope
Proposed canonical historical episode, Year 11 Month 8, about the Article VIII regulatory sunset and evidence-based renewal.

## Source
- Arvane §8.1 regulation must use least burdensome reasonably available means.
- §8.2(c–d) public explanation of authority, evidence, costs and benefits and alternatives; judicial review, 30-day public comment for ordinary rules.
- §8.4 reauthorization before ten-year expiration requires meaningful public review and current compliance with §8.2(c).
- §9.2 official publication required before ordinary enforceability.

## Editorial decisions
- Fictional historical port equipment rule and operator Ilen Marr are new local canon subject to editorial approval.
- A rule may lawfully be reissued in a revised form; a bare conclusory continuation does not renew it.
- Avoids claiming that the expired rule nullifies independent statutory safety duties, and avoids using the emergency regulation exception as a means to evade the sunset.
- No Year 14 Month 1 Westport equipment schedule or other current canon altered.
- No specific fictitious day assigned; earlier effective date is described as almost ten years before the Year 11 sunset.
