## Purpose

## Decisions
- Adoption is framed as after Day Zero, during formation (John, 2026-11-24), at the level of procedure: no dates, vote counts, signatories or named officeholders unless a published source gives them. The predecessor mapping stays undefined.
- Solara, Morantine and Verdmont use the §15.1 standard statutory framework; Korda keeps its §16.2 and §15.6 route. Korda's task is lighter (check and up to 400 words) because its Compact already carries its origin.
- Verdmont's two-per-section Executive Council gets a constitutional-equality review inside its account; it is not presented as adopted until John decides.
- Working reports (kind `report`, track `idea`) go directly to the Central Editor, as the Corindal and Monitors reports do. A side effect: any later `report` from these four desks (for example a simulated-opinion report) also goes direct.
- Territory pages and the rendered Compact presentation wait for the approved accounts (Round 3).
