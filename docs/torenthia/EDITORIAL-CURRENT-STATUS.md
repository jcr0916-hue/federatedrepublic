## Editorial leadership and agent permissions

- **President (user):** controls strategic choices and final approval/merging/publication.
- **VP (ChatGPT):** delegates, reviews independent submissions, constitutional/canon cross-checks, chronology, GitHub/site integration on feature branches, and recommends release.
- **Agent desks are independent editorial peers:** Federal, States/Territories, Monitors, Partisan Media. No local agent is subordinate to the federal desk. Agent proposals are NOT canon.
- **Common rule:** all agents receive the current Federal Constitution as a built-in reference; additionally supply their own State constitution or approved territorial compact and refreshed published source packet. When the federal Constitution changes, refresh agent references. Agents do not commit/publish, assign sequence numbers, advance fictional time, issue official action, or represent other institutions without central authorization.
- **Supreme Court:** no separate SC agent presently recommended; judicial cases may be proposed by relevant federal writers but must be legally and procedurally reviewed and must not be treated as governmental executive action. The SC is independent.
- **Desired future:** most fiction and official-record *drafting* originates with agents; VP does editorial integration, not every article's first draft. Pace by actual worth, not quotas.
