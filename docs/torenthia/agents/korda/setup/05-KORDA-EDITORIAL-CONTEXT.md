# Korda editorial context — approved non-spoiler operational packet

**Jurisdiction:** Korda, Indigenous Territory of the Federated Republic. **Time:** supplied published frontier Y14 M2. **Status:** all items below distinguish verified public canon from editorial guidance; not permission to publish.

## Korda is a living place, not only a constitutional petition
Korda lies on the southeastern/southern shore of Lake Varda and borders Sunderland, with an inland and ancestral zone toward Kelvant. The published story distinguishes the northern corridor, southern lakefront, and interior, with differing service, transport, political and community interests. Indigenous community relationships to lake access must not be reduced to freight or title valuations. Nor should every resident be assigned one political identity.

Korda elected **Territorial Integration** at founding, under Article XVI. Its current status is not a provisional State, sovereign Associated Community, or an independent country. Its governing institutions and powers must be checked under the Territorial Compact and law. It elects federal Assembly representatives but does not elect federal senators as a Territory. Do not invent a Governor or State constitutional record system.

4. Only LC-N1/LC-N2/LC-N3 are included in the proposed receiving-State transfer. Southern lakefront and interior would remain Korda. An enforceable continuity schedule for affected services and obligations must precede any actual transfer.

6. No final electorate list, ballot wording, referendum date/result, Kelvant approval, LM–JM assessment, Senate ratification, completed merger or Statehood exists in the supplied frontier.

**Do not conflate:** authenticated borders ≠ certified electors; Convention adoption ≠ popular ratification; merger ≠ Statehood; historical viability assessment ≠ final approval; proposed service commitment ≠ funded, delivered service.

## Recommended independent beats
- Ordinary lake access, local passenger service, seasonal road works, maintenance queues, healthcare and education service delivery.
- Territorial budgets, lawful local administrative notices, public records, Indigenous cultural continuity, generational differences, commercial and cross-border links.
- Federal Assembly representatives taking Korda concerns to committees; national social-state, immigration and equalization policies affecting Korda; limits of federal versus territorial authority.
- Northern and southern community voices with separate and sometimes overlapping interests, without steering them to a chosen constitutional outcome.

## Official records
Suggest an **official Korda/Territorial record** only if the actual issuing office, legal competence and publication path can be established. For federal Elections Panel or Monitor acts, propose an NRS item for **central federal publication** instead; the Korda agent may report and recommend, but does not issue federal acts. A routine administrative notice does not have to advance the merger or Statehood process.

- New places, people, budgets, positions, survey measurements and quotes are **proposed fiction**, not facts until central approval/publication.
- An existing published character or representative must not be given a newly binding action solely because the plot needs one.
- Always check whether a proposed article duplicates the published Korda Press record. Preserve chronological distinction among preliminary, superseded and most recent documents.
