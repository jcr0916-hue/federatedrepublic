## Source discipline
- Front matter and published body together establish particular records. A news piece describing what might happen does not supersede a later issued NRS act.
- The current convention proposal is **not** a completed transfer. Separately track 60% referendum support with 50% participation, receiving-State constitutional approval, joint Legislative/Judicial Monitor assessment, Senate ratification, and Statehood audits/legislative decisions as applicable.
- Do not use internal plot/character-planning documents as training material, or expose unpublished storyline developments.
