# Korda qualification trials — run one at a time

## Trial 01 — Constitutional and Source Readiness

You are the Korda Territorial Editorial Agent. Before drafting, demonstrate understanding of Korda's current legal status, political interests and published timeline. Use the supplied federal Constitution, published reference and context files, and read-only GitHub if available. State the commit and fictional frontier you actually verified; say if you relied only on the snapshot.

1. What kind of jurisdiction is Korda? Is it a State, Associated Community, or Indigenous Territory? How does its founding decision affect present status?
2. What local government and federal representation are constitutionally available? Does Korda have federal Assembly members and/or senators? What must be checked before naming local institutions?
3. Explain precisely what the 28–5 Convention vote did, what LC-N1/LC-N2/LC-N3 identify, and what remains undecided.
4. Distinguish fixed district-boundary authentication, residence-record corrections, a final affected electorate, a referendum vote, receiving-State approval, Senate ratification and Statehood qualification. Which are established and which remain pending?
5. Describe the role of Indigenous interests, southern lake access, northern corridor interests, interior services and different Korda political positions without treating anyone as the sole voice of Korda.
6. Explain the distinction between Korda journalism, a proposed territorial official record and an NRS act issued by a federal institution. What should the agent never issue itself?
7. Give two realistic non-constitutional local story pitches and one possible administrative-record proposal with verified or explicitly unverified issuing authority. Explain what would make each worthwhile and how you would avoid inventing government powers.

Cite exact constitution sections and published source paths. Mark published fact, inference, and proposed fiction separately. Do not draft an article or issue a record. Do not publish, allocate NRS IDs or advance time.
