# Korda Territorial Agent — Trial 01

## Constitutional and Canon Readiness

You are acting as the independent editorial correspondent for Korda Territory.

Before undertaking original reporting, demonstrate that you understand Korda's governing institutions, constitutional relationship with the Federated Republic, and current political situation.

Using your training documents and read-only GitHub access, complete the following assessment.

### Part I — Territorial Government

Explain:

1. How the Korda Territorial Compact originated during the Republic's founding.
2. The composition, election procedures, terms, and powers of the Korda Territorial Assembly.
3. How the Chief Minister is elected, the limits on executive service, and the procedures for removal and succession.
4. Korda's authority over local government, traditional institutions, courts, taxation, and budgets.
5. The relationship between territorial law, the Compact, applicable Republic law, and the National Record System.

Distinguish territorial institutions from Korda's federal Assembly delegation and the temporary Territory Convention.

### Part II — Current Canon

Identify Korda's current political and constitutional circumstances at the latest published fictional date.

Explain the relationship between:

- The lake corridor's petition involving Kelvant.
- The whole-Territory Statehood petition.
- The Territory Convention and its constitutional responsibilities.
- The proposed Northern Lake Settlement.
- The affected-electorate process and any unresolved approvals.

Identify what has actually happened and what remains undecided.

Do not infer that a referendum, merger, Statehood transition, or electoral result has occurred merely because a proposal or administrative step exists.

### Part III — Editorial Independence

Identify three potential stories that a Korda-focused correspondent could investigate.

For each, explain:

- Why it matters to Korda's residents.
- Which published events or constitutional provisions make it relevant.
- Whose perspectives should be represented.
- Which developments remain unresolved.

At least one story must originate in an ordinary local concern rather than the Convention or Statehood dispute.

### Part IV — Source Verification

Cite specific constitutional provisions, Compact articles, and published NRS or news references supporting your answers.

Clearly distinguish:

- Established published canon.
- The approved editorial reconstruction of Korda's founding Compact.
- New suggestions or possible future developments.

Do not invent official decisions, election results, dates, or historical events.

Do not access confidential future political or character planning files.

Do not publish, modify the repository, or advance fictional time.

**This is an evaluation, not a request to draft a publishable story.**
