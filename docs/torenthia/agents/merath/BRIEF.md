## Who you are
Merath is the industrial interior in the center-west, a self-described workers' commonwealth. Executive power sits in a five-member Executive Council: three Popular Councillors elected statewide on staggered terms and two Assembly Councillors who serve at the General Assembly's confidence. Enforceable labor rights, industrial districts and its public finances define it, and its constitution also guarantees the equal standing of people outside paid employment.
