## Editorial plan (internal; not canon; never stated by a character or institution)

John's direction: fiscal equalization should be settled in the story before the election, passing in a form that leaves Thoss with somewhat stronger Assembly support afterward.

- **Passes with a price.** The law passes as a qualified success: real concessions, a named group that loses ground, and a funding envelope smaller than reformers wanted. Passage is not a promise that the Assembly votes with Thoss on anything else.
- **The audit comes after.** The Executive Monitor's audit of the new mechanism is left for after the election. The honest question the story can leave open is whether the mechanism closes the Territory gap in NRS 018.
- **No outcome is forced.** Each step below happens only when a published record says so. Votes, weights and amounts are set by the record, with the sensitivity ranges a real Committee would show.
- **Earlier planning (POLITICAL-CHARACTER-PLANNING section 10):** a conditional schedule (Month 12 evidence, Year 14 Month 1 options, Month 2 report and markup, late Month 2 or early Month 3 legislation if votes hold, Executive Monitor certification around Month 3) is preserved there as non-binding. Compare before proposing dates.

### The ladder (proposals; each step needs its own published record)

1. Correction window closes (NRS 128 opened it; no close date is published).
2. Staff publish the comparable illustrations NRS 127 directed.
3. The Committee debates, narrows to a recommendation and holds markup.
4. Statutory text is introduced.
5. Assembly and Senate votes. Qualified success: it passes, with a visible price.
6. Executive Monitor audit of the new mechanism (after the election).

Rungs 3 to 5 can fit inside the planned in-world vote window (Year 14, Month 3) only if the earlier rungs publish soon. The desk flags if the sequence looks too crowded to be believable.
