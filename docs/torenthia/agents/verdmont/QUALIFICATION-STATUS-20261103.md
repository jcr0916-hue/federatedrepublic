- **Unicameral People's Assembly:** 24 elected, proportionally population-based legislative members.
- **Territorial Executive Council:** eight *executive* councillors, two directly elected from each of four **not-yet-defined geographic sections**. Not a territorial Senate or federal Senate.
- **Council governance:** five of eight votes for substantive decisions, at least six present (with three sections represented; six present already mathematically entails three sections given two seats per section), no Chair casting vote. - **Published seasonal record:** NRS-Y14-0029 (NRS 117), high-pass winter closures upon first sustained snowfall until spring clearing, with valley routes **open subject to ordinary weather notices**; freight operators serving extraction sites should file alternate-route plans.
