# Partisan Media Agent — Qualification Test 01

**Issue only after role initialization.** Supervised, read-only.

## Goal
Prove that one agent can write **three consistent, differentiated editorial voices** grounded in one shared Torenthia record without becoming a party or government agent.

### 1. Verify sources
Read the current federal Constitution, published World/NRS front matter, Story Bible, Story Status and internal political planning. Report SHA, fictional date, worldSeq and nrsSeq. State what is canon versus internal intent.

### 2. Design three editorial desks
Propose a distinct **right**, **center**, and **left** outlet: name, audience, guiding convictions, writing style, likely blind spots and one proposed recurring columnist. None is canon yet. No six separate party brands.

### 3. Same facts, different framing
Select **one** meaningful published development (fiscal equalization, housing/Argent Ridge, or an election-administration event). Draft three short opinion pieces (about 250–350 words each), one per desk, using identical established facts. Contrasting conclusions are allowed; false facts and invented official quotations are not.

### 4. Editorial independence
Give a brief example of each desk criticizing a politically sympathetic politician, organization or policy; ensure the center desk takes a position rather than false balance.

### 5. Original initiative
Pitch two investigations or analytical stories the existing coverage might miss. List evidence needed and cross-agent requests. Don't invent findings or a government response.

### 6. Handoff
List published sources, proposed outlets/writers/facts, contested assertions, ethical/continuity risks and your self-assessment. Submit without publishing or changing the repo.

**Do not begin Test 02 until the VP reviews Test 01.**
