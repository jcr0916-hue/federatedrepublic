- `docs/constitution-current.md`: constitutional framework, especially executive domains, Assembly/Civic Consul, Senate/Legat Consul, elections, political finance, speech, transparency and public-record obligations.
- `docs/torenthia/STORY-BIBLE.md`: durable canon, character logic, continuing stories and editorial constraints.

- `docs/torenthia/POLITICAL-CHARACTER-PLANNING.md`: **internal planning only**, particularly the LRP, CRM, SDA, NRP, WSP and GRA identities. These six party names/identities are an editorial plan, not automatically published public manifestos, seat allocations, polling or official leaders.

- Current published news and NRS front matter. Follow related-record links to support claims.

## Story lines to follow without commandeering
Election administration and campaigning; legislative coalition negotiations; fiscal equalization; social-state implementation; public transport and local economy; Argent Ridge; Korda's constitutional procedures; Lake Varda diplomacy; inter-State disagreements.

## Political structure
The editorial reference uses six significant parties: Liberal Reform Party (LRP), Civic Renewal Movement (CRM), Social Democratic Alliance (SDA), National Republican Party (NRP), Workers' Solidarity Party (WSP), Green and Regional Alliance (GRA). **These do not map one-to-one to right/center/left editorial desks.** A party may contain factions; an outlet may favor one argument and criticize another.

Current coverage does **not** license inventing numerical party polling or a federal election outcome. Confirm officeholder names, mandates, legislative thresholds and whether they have actually spoken before attributing any view.

## Source ladder in every submission
Separate:
- **Established:** exact citation to published file, constitutional provision, fictional date and link.
- **Proposed:** new outlet, writer, editorial conclusion, public interview, nonofficial speaker, position, or development that requires approval.
- **Unknown:** disputed status, unverified quote, inferred motive, pending procedural step.

Do not let a persuasive rhetorical voice turn a prediction or private planning into an established event.

## Cross-agent coordination
A proposed reaction by Thoss, Rehn, a State governor, a political party's official representative or a constitutional institution should be routed as a **REQUEST TO RESPONSIBLE AGENT**, not drafted as an already-delivered statement. State and federal agents may be asked to propose partisan speeches or political action by their own officials.

## Updating
If central editorial approves an outlet or recurring journalist in a published story, carry the established name and editorial voice into later work, update continuity documentation when requested, and preserve previous opinions as part of the outlet's record. Do not retroactively change a published outlet's position to suit a new angle.
