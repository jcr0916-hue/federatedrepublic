## Universal sequence and boundaries

1. Confirm the State's agent has its *complete current* State and Federal Constitutions and access to current published records. A packet's condensed summary is **not** a substitute for full law. The 2026-11-03 reference packet is an **archived snapshot**; newer published records, updated constitutions and state profiles control.
2. Administer **Test 01: constitutional readiness**; central VP independently verifies constitutional provisions and historical-case status, scores /100, passes at **85+**, and records exact corrections.
3. Only on a Test 01 pass, administer **Test 02: an 800–1,100-word independent local feature**, normally three or more credible resident voices, with a proposed-facts ledger, source verification, and self-critique. A passing article is merely an *unpublished candidate*.
4. Only after Test 02 approval, administer **Test 03: State/interregional/federal competence**. Require specific carry-through of Test 02 edits, lawful division of powers and peer-desk referral reasoning. Test 03 scenario may be adapted to avoid duplication or known editorial hazards. Three passing tests qualify the agent for **supervised** editorial work, not independent publication authority.
5. Keep the VP scoring guides **strictly private to central VP**; do not reproduce their rubric weights, expected answers, or examiner-only notes in public repository documents or agent-facing materials. The previously established passing threshold is **85/100**.
6. On each completed response, preserve its result, detailed corrections, source checks, any accepted-but-unpublished article edits, and next authorized step in dated `agents/<state>/QUALIFICATION-STATUS-YYYYMMDD.md` on a **feature branch**. Do not grant qualification based on file receipt, preparation, or an agent's self-assessment.
7. Never assign fictional World/NRS/State-record numbers, advance fictional time, assert current historic officeholders, invent official findings/legislation/referenda or publish agent proposals without central editorial verification and Presidential authorization. State records are not automatically NRS records. Agents are independent peers, not subordinates of the Federal desk; coordinate only on genuine cross-jurisdictional issues.
