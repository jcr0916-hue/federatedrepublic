# Clock for desks

Every desk reads this each run. It tells you **when** the big threads need to reach a decision point in the published record. You choose **how**: your jurisdiction's voice, people and details are yours. The timing is not up for choice. The Central Editor keeps this page current and sets the dates; the fictional date stays Year 14, Month 2 until a published record moves it.

## How to use it

- Windows below are timing guidance, not announced facts. Never state a date, deadline or "expected" step in a story or record unless a published record already says so.
- Do not advance the fictional date, and do not settle a thread's outcome. Move a thread by publishing the next dry, ordinary step the record allows.
- If your jurisdiction touches one of these threads and you have a believable beat that moves it, file it. If a thread looks like it is slipping, say so in your filing's notes for the editor.
- Silence is allowed only where a thread says "background".

## Threads and windows (in-world)

- **Fiscal equalization (§12.6).** The evidence phase should finish in Month 2 and reach a decision point in Month 3: a Committee recommendation, a decision to take no action, or a call for more evidence. Any decision point must come from published records (staff estimates, the correction window closing, Committee discussion). Federal, the Assembly and Senate desks, and State and Territory desks with corrected source tables or local effects are best placed.
- **Election package.** The Elections Panel's formal notice, the Legat Consul ballot-order lot, practice-ballot steps and the election date should be set by published records during Month 2 or early Month 3. Four Legat Consul candidates are qualified. Do not state a date or an order until a record does.
- **Executive Monitor succession.** The Executive Monitor's term-end date and the confirmation trigger still need to be established by a published record. The Speakers' joint nomination (or the sixty-day lottery fallback) belongs in the current legislative session, with the Judicial Monitor's disclosure findings published before the vote. Under §9.3 the nominee is then confirmed by national popular vote at the next federal electoral period, not by a legislature.
- **Lake Varda / Valedon.** The conference has opened. It should reach a decision point in Month 3: agreed text and ratification steps, or a recorded stall. Sunderland's silence is established character.
- **Argent Ridge.** Background. The housing bill (NRS 088) should reach a recorded outcome; the repeal signature period runs on its own clock. Keep repeal and Trust protection separate. Norvane and Varek desks: John would find a citizen initiative at State level interesting (Trust designation needs the host State's consent, §18.4). Use it only if Norvane's own constitutional process supports it and it fits your State; the Norvane desk judges.
- **Korda ratification.** The Elections Panel's electorate determination comes first; no ballot language or date exists. The ratification vote comes after the election, so Korda can be argued in the campaign without being decided in it.
- **Corindal audit reconstruction.** Round 1 filings are due from the Monitors desk (Sundays) and the Corindal desk (Thursdays). See the joint working file.
- **Fixed dates already in canon:** the Monetary Authority State of the Economy report and the Judicial Pool quarterly return are each due no later than Year 14, Month 5.
