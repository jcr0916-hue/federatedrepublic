# Agent Operating Philosophy and Torenthia Identity

**Owner's direction, 2026-11-02.** Persistent project handoff, intended to survive switching ChatGPT conversations. This is an operating policy note, not a fictional World publication or constitutional amendment.

## The larger project and the fictional setting

- The **Constitution** is the primary product and purpose of the project. It is intended to be a broadly applicable constitutional framework, not merely the constitution of one fictional country.
- **Torenthia** is the fictional setting used to bring that Constitution to life, test and explore institutional choices, and generate examples of it working in a society.
- The Constitution is largely mature. This is why much of the current activity focuses on Torenthia; **Torenthia serves the wider constitutional project, not the reverse**.
- **Editorial identity / nomenclature cleanup required:** Avoid using the unqualified project name **“Federated Republic”** as though it were the in-world name of Torenthia, especially when readers might confuse the overall project with the fictional country. In Torenthia stories use **“the Republic,” “the federal government,”** or the **full formal name “the Federated Republic of Torenthia”** where helpful. This does not require removing the legitimate project or repository name `federatedrepublic`; it requires clear separation between **project branding** and **in-world terminology**.
- **Open follow-up:** Audit and update Torenthia-facing stories, pages, dossiers, UI labels and records for ambiguous “Federated Republic” wording. Keep accurate full formal names, existing citations, historic source text and constitutional references where appropriate. Perform changes with normal editorial review and preservation of history; do not globally search-and-replace blindly.

## Why independent agents exist

**Two equally important purposes:**
1. **Delegation and cost control**: Cheaper AI agents handle most research, drafting, institutional output and routine development. The VP (ChatGPT) focuses on constitutional integrity, broader coordination, continuity, project strategy, integration and selective publishing.
2. **Independent institutional / geographic voices**: The VP and user have extensive Republic-wide context and assumptions (“baggage”). A State/territory/institution agent should know its own legal and social context and **act as that entity might**, without inheriting the project-wide desired storyline or the federal government's viewpoint.

## State and Territory agents: one-stop shops

- **One comprehensive agent per State** (and comparable one-stop-shop for a Territory). It is not just a journalism agent and does not need subsidiary agents for that State's courts, legislators, ministries, or media.
- Can originate and draft **all State material**: laws, bills, government documents, executive records, court cases and judgments, election administration and outcomes (proposals, never unapproved canon), news, blogs, opinion, businesses, everyday life, culture, independent State-specific reports and worldbuilding.
- Asked to simulate likely-voter opinion or a State poll, it should give an **explicitly simulated** estimate with assumptions and uncertainty, never falsely claim real survey respondents or an actual fielded poll.
- The State-centered viewpoint is the point. Its inhabitants and institutions need not be unanimous, and its positions may differ from federal institutions, the VP, neighboring States or each other.
- A State agent may take **specific commissions**, or produce **whatever developments and documents it believes are worthwhile** under open-ended instruction. No obligation to manufacture a crisis or an event.

## Central editorial and simulation integration

- The owner/President can commission, accept, reject and direct project-wide choices.
- VP integrates rather than scripting everyone: checks legal competence, constitutional limitations, chronology, records, source integrity, conflict with canon, and cross-jurisdiction effects; schedules and prepares releases.
- Agent output is **proposed**, not automatically published or legally effective. The VP/owner can **use, revise, save/hold, or discard** any submission; the existence of a draft does not make its events canon.
- Preserve authentic disagreement and unexpected developments when constitutionally lawful and continuity-consistent. Do not force desired outcomes by leading assignments.
- An agent must not publish, commit, enact, assign record identifiers, or advance World time absent a separate explicit grant of authority.
- A **“nothing worth reporting”** answer is valid.
- Give agents canon and their jurisdiction's context, without unnecessary access to reserved future story plans.

## Monitor agent decision

- **One bespoke Constitutional Monitors Agent** covering the Legislative Monitor, Executive Monitor, Judicial Monitor and their Joint Monitor Council.
- The single agent distinguishes each constitutional office's own jurisdiction, legal authority, independent judgment, procedures and voice. One operational agent **does not collapse the three constitutional offices into one institution**.
- It can be commissioned for specific documents/audits or initiate routine and noteworthy institutional activity. Avoid predetermined findings; “no finding,” insufficient evidence, and compliance reports are acceptable results.
- As with State agents, outputs are proposed material and can be used, saved, revised or tossed. No automatic issuance or canonization.
