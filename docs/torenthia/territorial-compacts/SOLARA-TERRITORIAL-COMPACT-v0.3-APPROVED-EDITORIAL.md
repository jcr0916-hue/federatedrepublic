# Solara Territorial Compact

**Working draft v0.3 — institutional and legal review for author approval and future Solara Agent preparation**  
**Status:** Proposed standard territorial framework under §15.1 of the current federal Constitution. **Not enacted, ratified, or published canon.**  
**Editorial revision:** 3 November 2026 (real-world). **In-world date:** not assigned.  
**Source baseline:** `jcr0916-hue/federatedrepublic`, `main` `c8d43dbd2aabfe0260fb9eb095529382a6bb0764`.

> **Editorial note:** Solara is an existing Territory. The public record does not establish that its present governance was created by an incorporation or devolution agreement. This is therefore drafted as a **proposed standard statutory Territorial Compact**, not a fictitious newly negotiated founding agreement. Its institutions, selection rules, and amendment procedures below are **new proposals** until the author approves them and the Republic's lawful enactment history is determined. It must not be treated as evidence that a new political act occurred in Year 14.

## Purpose

This Compact sets out a proposed framework for Solara's elected local government, territorial administration, public finances, coastal and island services, rights, and relationship with the Republic. It preserves Solara's lawful Statehood process without prescribing its outcome. It does not award the federal seat of government, decide a dispute with Marisvia, or create obligations inconsistent with the Constitution.

---

## Article 1 — Territorial Status and Legal Order

1. Solara is a Territory of the Federated Republic of Torenthia, situated in the Western Sea, with Ostara as its historic territorial capital.
2. Solara is governed by the federal Constitution, valid federal law within federal competence, and this Compact as lawfully established under §15.1. Territorial legislation is valid within the authority provided here and by law, and is subject to constitutional judicial review.
3. The Republic holds the governing authority over the Territory. This Compact provides elected local self-government over matters of local concern. It does not make Solara a sovereign State of the Republic before completion of §15.2.
4. Rights guaranteed by Article I apply throughout Solara. This Compact neither diminishes those rights nor establishes a separate citizenship, foreign policy, military command, or federal electoral system.
5. In matters not settled by this Compact, applicable Republic law governs. The principles of reasonable local capacity and subsidiarity under §§15.8 and 15.8.a guide the exercise of allocated functions.

## Article 2 — The Solara Territorial Assembly

1. A unicameral **Solara Territorial Assembly** is the elected governing authority required by §15.1. It legislates on matters of local concern and oversees territorial administration.
2. **Agreed design, pending legal adoption:** The Assembly has **twenty-four members** elected by proportional ranked-choice voting (single transferable vote) in territorial constituencies established by territorial law. Territorial constituencies may be organized around island communities and population centers and **need not coincide with the nine federal Assembly seats or their districts**. Constituency boundaries and seats must preserve equal suffrage, fair representation, and Article I rights. This Compact does not itself establish constituency names or boundaries.
3. **Proposed term:** Members serve **four-year terms**. Territorial election arrangements shall be fixed by law and administered consistently with federal electoral guarantees. The Elections Panel retains its federal constitutional jurisdiction; this Compact does not transfer federal election administration to the Territory.
4. The Assembly elects a Speaker and publishes its standing orders, votes, committee reports, enacted territorial legislation, and financial oversight reports as required by Article X and applicable law.
5. It may conduct inquiries, summon territorial ministers pursuant to law, and require accounting for appropriated territorial funds. It may not act as a federal chamber or bind independent federal institutions.
6. Membership in the Territorial Assembly is distinct from a seat in the federal Assembly; Solara has no federal Senate seats while it remains a Territory.

## Article 3 — Chief Minister and Territorial Administration

1. A **Chief Minister of Solara** heads the Territorial Administration and is responsible for implementing territorial legislation, maintaining ordinary public services, and directing territorial departments within their lawful competence.
2. **Agreed parliamentary design, pending legal adoption:** After each general territorial election, the Territorial Assembly elects a Chief Minister by an absolute majority of its full membership. The Chief Minister serves while retaining the confidence of the Assembly, subject to the safeguards in Article 4.
3. The Chief Minister appoints and may dismiss territorial ministers under territorial law. Portfolios may include transport and ports, public works, finance, local services, health administration, and heritage; these are **possible portfolios**, not a declaration that any office currently exists.
4. The Chief Minister and ministers may not exercise federal constitutional authority, conclude foreign treaties, or alter Solara's territorial status. External communications affecting Marisvia or other foreign governments must respect the Legat Consul's domain.
5. Executive decisions requiring publication shall be entered in the NRS under Article 10 and applicable law.

## Article 4 — Confidence, Vacancy and Continuity

1. The Assembly may replace the Chief Minister only through a **constructive vote of no confidence** electing a named eligible successor by an absolute majority of its full membership (**thirteen votes while the Assembly comprises twenty-four members**). A motion that does not elect a successor has no effect. The replacement takes office under procedures established by territorial law; ordinary political disagreement alone does not create a vacancy.
2. Upon resignation, death, disqualification, or permanent incapacity of the Chief Minister, an eligible member of the existing territorial ministry serves as **Acting Chief Minister** under a publicly established order of succession adopted by territorial law. If no minister is available, the Assembly elects a temporary acting head by an absolute majority of its full membership. The Assembly must elect a permanent successor within **thirty days** of the vacancy, subject to a narrowly tailored extension where a court determines that an extraordinary impediment makes compliance impossible.
3. During a vacancy, the existing ministry continues in **caretaker service** to maintain lawful administration and essential services. It may perform urgent duties within existing authority but may not use the vacancy to alter this Compact, assume federal competence, or make irreversible institutional changes absent an express lawful necessity.
4. A temporary incapacity is governed by territorial law providing for prompt acting service, transparent notice, due process, and access to independent judicial review. Temporary incapacity does not itself vacate the office.
5. Unlawful removal, an unlawful succession designation, or an unlawful exercise of office remains subject to independent judicial review. Article I rights apply throughout.

## Article 5 — Island Government and Local Autonomy

1. The Territory may establish, recognize, combine, and reorganize island councils, municipalities, districts, and other local authorities by territorial law, consistently with §§15.8 and 15.8.a.
2. Ostara retains its role as the historic civic center of Solara. No provision predetermines the location of future federal facilities.
3. Local authorities shall possess reasonable fiscal, legal and administrative capacity to perform the functions assigned to them. Matters suited to island or municipal administration should be exercised at that level unless a lawful and justified higher-level arrangement is necessary.
4. The Territory may recognize local language, maritime customs, cultural heritage, and community institutions consistently with Article I. This creates no invented Indigenous status or treaty entitlement.

## Article 6 — Territorial Competence and Public Services

1. Subject to valid federal law, Solara may legislate and administer ordinary local matters, including municipal services, public works, ferry and harbor services within lawful territorial competence, local infrastructure, education and health administration, coastal stewardship, and internal economic affairs.
2. The Solara Territorial Authority may publish transport timetables, passenger notices and operational conditions through competent departments. This clause recognizes the type of routine activity already appearing in the public record; it does **not** prescribe a number of sailings or require continuation of any particular timetable.
3. Interjurisdictional agreements concerning ordinary services may be made only as law permits and may not infringe federal constitutional competences or substitute for treaties.
4. Territorial law shall provide transparent procedures for material service changes, including publication of the responsible authority, effective date, and public-access arrangements where applicable. It need not convert each operational adjustment into a constitutional determination.

## Article 7 — Maritime Transport, Ports and Emergency Services

1. Solara may administer island transport connections, local ports, maritime access works, and associated public facilities within its lawful authority, including coordination with mainland jurisdictions and private operators under applicable law.
2. When publishing ordinary ferry and port notices, the responsible territorial authority shall distinguish scheduled service from weather-related cancellation, freight conditions, fares and passenger rights, as applicable.
3. Territorial emergency response and marine safety arrangements shall coordinate with the Republic and neighboring jurisdictions where lawful; no separate armed service or independent foreign coast-guard authority is created by this Compact.
4. Transport policy and service funding remain subject to ordinary appropriation and review. This Compact does not establish that the existing Ostara–Westport winter reduction resulted from fiscal incapacity.

## Article 8 — Justice and Rule of Law

1. Solara is subject to the federal judicial system under Article IV and to the constitutional guarantees of an independent, impartial court and due process.
2. Local courts, tribunals, and alternative dispute-resolution bodies may be established or organized **only where authorized by applicable Republic law**. Territorial legislation may regulate their organization, procedure, and lawful local jurisdiction within the federal judicial framework; it cannot create jurisdiction denied by federal law.
3. The Territory shall not establish a separate territorial supreme court or claim constitutional finality against the Republic's courts. No territorial body may displace the Supreme Court's constitutional jurisdiction or the lawful appellate jurisdiction of other federal courts.
4. Administrative decisions affecting rights must provide reasons and available review in accordance with law.

## Article 9 — Public Finances and Fiscal Equalization

1. The Territorial Assembly enacts territorial budgets, appropriations, taxes and fees within the Territory's lawful jurisdiction, subject to §§12.3, 12.5 and other applicable federal provisions. Territorial revenue remains distinct from money received by the Republic into the National Revenue Fund; collection or disbursement of federal money must follow federal appropriation law.
2. Territorial accounts, procurement decisions, revenues and expenditures shall be maintained in auditable form, with periodic public reporting under territorial law. Federal constitutional audit and oversight powers remain with the competent Monitors; this Compact does not assign those offices new powers or replace lawful territorial audit functions.
3. Solara participates in applicable federal public-service and equalization mechanisms under Article XII. Such participation does not establish a claim to any particular transfer or presume fiscal insufficiency.
4. Transport-service changes, including the Ostara–Westport timetable, are not presumed to constitute a §12.6 noncompliance finding. Evidence of any asserted fiscal-capacity concern must be assessed under the governing constitutional and statutory process.

## Article 10 — Official Records and Transparency

1. The National Record System is the permanent authoritative public record for constitutional and official acts requiring publication under Article X.
2. Solara shall submit territorial legislation, legally significant orders, and other required records to the NRS under the applicable procedures. Ordinary service notices may also be published in accessible local formats, without creating a rival authoritative constitutional record.
3. Corrected official records are published as new entries alongside originals when Article X requires; published records shall not be silently erased or changed.
4. This Compact creates no independent Solara NRS, election certification authority, or power to restrict constitutional records.

## Article 11 — Statehood and Constitutional Transition

1. Solara's ongoing Statehood process is governed by §15.2 and the established published audit record. This Compact neither initiates a new petition nor determines any audit result.
2. Any required first or second Statehood Audit is conducted by the three Monitors within their independent mandates and subject to the required legislative determinations. Neither the Territorial Assembly nor the Chief Minister may announce Statehood by their own act.
3. If Solara lawfully becomes a State, Statehood takes effect by constitutional operation under §15.2(5), with the consequences for Senate representation under §15.2(6). Existing territorial laws and offices continue **only to the extent consistent with the federal Constitution and valid transitional law**; the State must establish its lawful State constitutional and institutional order without assuming that this Territorial Compact itself becomes a permanent State constitution.
4. Until the relevant constitutional completion, Solara remains a Territory governed by the lawful territorial order.

## Article 12 — Seat of Government and Host Arrangements

1. This Compact does not designate Solara as the federal Seat of Government, alter Verentum's existing position, create a relocation proceeding, or confer any right to host federal institutions.
2. If a lawful federal seat-selection or hosting process under §18.2 is initiated, Solara's competent local institutions may participate **only to the extent permitted by the governing statute**, without deciding the outcome or claiming federal authority.
3. Any actual federal hosting arrangement is governed by §§18.1–18.3, including designation of the federal operational footprint, the Republic's exclusive constitutional operations within it, continuing host territorial sovereignty, and applicable judicial review.
4. Solara's previously reported capital bid is a **historical background fact**, not a live application created by this Compact.

## Article 13 — Maritime and External Relations

1. Solara's local maritime culture and commerce may involve extensive contact across the Western Sea. Such contacts do not confer independent federal foreign-affairs or treaty-making powers.
2. Disputes or competing claims involving Marisvia are addressed through the Republic's competent constitutional institutions. This Compact does not recognize, concede, resolve or enlarge an external territorial claim.
3. Ordinary trade, cultural, tourism and transport arrangements remain subject to federal constitutional powers and applicable law.

## Article 14 — Amendment and Interpretation

1. This proposed standard statutory Compact is established and amended through constitutionally valid federal legislation under §15.1, with consultation of Solara's elected territorial authority under procedures established by law. Nothing here invents a bilateral constitutional veto or the special ratification formula applicable to a different type of territorial compact.
2. The Territorial Assembly may adopt ordinary legislation and administrative arrangements within the powers conferred by this Compact without treating every amendment of local law as a compact amendment.
3. Where any provision conflicts with the federal Constitution, the Constitution prevails; provisions capable of lawful independent operation remain effective to the extent permitted by law.
4. No construction of this Compact may impair Article I rights, the Monitors' independence, the Elections Panel, the Judiciary, or the constitutional functions of the federal executives and Legislature.
