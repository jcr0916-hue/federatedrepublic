## Narrative boundaries

The initiative begins during founding and remains nonpartisan. The four finalists are considered by the newly constituted federal Assembly. Adoption follows through ordinary legislation, the National Flag Act, during the scenario's operating-Legislature period; it is not a constitutional amendment or part of the ratification ballot. No exact adoption day, legislative vote count, designer identity, or public popularity result is supplied.

Korda is the fourth founding **political community** represented by the arms, not a fourth predecessor sovereign that ratified the merger. Its existing transition story and separate Territorial Integration choice remain intact. The central star represents union as one Republic.
