# Federated Torenthia editorial desk — four-agent launch plan

**Real-world date:** 2026-11-01. **Status:** Assignments drafted, awaiting agent activation and supervised responses. **Not published canon.**

## Organization and authority

President: editorial and release authority. VP (ChatGPT): manages assignments, cross-jurisdiction review, chronology, and recommendations. Local agents are independent editorial peers, not subordinate to the federal agent. Every agent uses read-only repository access and submits pitches/drafts for central review. No autonomous publishing, World/NRS numbering, clock movement, constitutional changes, PR merges or deployments.

**Roster:** Corindal State agent (supervised pilot accepted), Varek State agent (activation/readiness to recheck), Korda Territorial agent (three supervised trials passed; two accepted candidates), federal Republic-wide editorial agent (activation/readiness to verify). The existence of a named role is not proof of connection to this chat or an agent run.

## Initial delegated briefs — send to each agent when online

### Corindal — locally initiated reporting
- Refresh constitutional/profile and published World source packet from live `main`.
- Review current national political/development records and the most recent Corindal-specific record.
- Propose up to **two** distinct local stories, at least one arising from local daily life or an institution rather than mirroring an NRS headline.
- Return **pitch, monitor, or no story** on each federal development; investigate civic consequences and internal differences of opinion.
- Do not assume any proposed events have occurred or that all residents agree with the State government.

### Varek — independent second State perspective
- **Activation gate:** confirm operating instructions, reliable read-only source access, current Varek constitutional facts and one readiness review before assigning publication drafts.
- Then propose up to **two** Varek-grounded stories; at least one should be self-directed, not a federal echo. Distinguish any cross-State consequences from published facts.
- Return short watchlist of current federal/neighboring developments worth monitoring, explicitly including a “no local response” option.

### Korda — local continuity and publication support
- Do not redraft accepted versions of “When the Lake Thaws Before the Road Does” or “The Table Across the Lake” unless a real canon/source discrepancy is found.
- Check geographical and seasonal setting (especially proposed Y14 M2 thaw) and sources against the newest canon; classify any discrepancies with precise source links, not unsupported fixes.
- After publication, propose up to **two** *new* locally motivated story pitches; avoid reporting proposed Compact details as a verified historic NRS transcript.
- Keep the Korda constitutional file, transfer docket, and Lake Varda negotiations distinct and do not invent their outcomes.

### Federal editorial agent — independent Republic-wide desk
- **Activation gate:** check read-only access, editorial boundaries and ability to distinguish national public interest from the federal government's position.
- Review national chronology, constitutional/electoral/fiscal developments and meaningful State/Territory stories.
- Propose up to **two** Republic-wide stories based on genuinely federal public-interest consequences; identify which local stories merit national syndication or follow-up.
- Do **not** supervise, approve, or rewrite State agents; independent disagreement may be a legitimate story, not an error.
- Never write an all-knowing national summary of unpublished local drafts.

## Standard assignment response contract

Every response includes: source commit + World/NRS frontier; jurisdiction and perspective; fictional month (if verified) or proposed timing; source links; pitch headline and 2–4 sentence premise; why this community cares; new characters/places identified as proposals; constitutional dependencies; cross-jurisdiction effects; uncertainties; and a decision **draft / monitor / no action**. Reserve canon-affecting outcomes for central review.

## Coordination cadence

1. VP refreshes published canon and offers each active agent the **same public facts** plus the jurisdiction's own context, without sharing private future plot outlines or one agent's unpublished drafts as events.
2. Agents submit independent pitches. VP creates a compact cross-desk matrix: overlap, conflicting claims, source discrepancies, novelty, and affected arcs.
3. VP chooses candidate priorities and requests full text only for accepted pitches; President approves final canon-affecting editorial decisions.
4. Approved publication uses a single canonical World article, visible in Torenthia's main updates and relevant local filters; NRS remains a separate official-record category.
5. Keep a reasonable cadence: not every desk needs to publish every week. Prefer natural local consequences and ordinary-life texture over mechanically generated articles.
