Rhondel Shore Services — Public Meeting Register

Notice

Kelvant's Rhondel municipal administration opens a public meeting register for ferry access, service counter capacity and emergency-contact coordination on the lake shore.

Scope

The register will receive submissions from residents, operators and local service offices. It is ordinary State-level consultation, not Kelvant's constitutional approval of any territorial merger.

Next record

No Korda territory has transferred, and this consultation neither schedules nor substitutes for the pending affected-voter ratification of the Convention proposal.

End of record.
