Territorial Compact — editorial reference

The Verdmont Territorial Compact v0.2 is the approved editorial design for a 24-member population-based People's Assembly and a separate eight-member Territorial Executive Council elected across four geographic sections. It is not enacted or ratified law, and no new Year 14 event or election is established by this reference. The exact sections and their populations are not fixed; the proposed equal two-per-section executive representation expressly requires further constitutional-equality review before any portrayal of enactment.

Territory and Statehood

Verdmont remains a Territory and has not begun a Statehood Audit. Federal territorial government, local capacity and the possible future Statehood process remain subject to the Constitution, particularly §15.1 and §15.2.
