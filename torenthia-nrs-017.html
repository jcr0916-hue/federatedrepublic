Date of Session:Year 13, Month 9, Day 34
Attendance

Both executives present. Ministers of the Civic Consul's domain: full attendance. Domain officers of the Legat Consul's domain: full attendance, with the Director of Foreign Affairs participating for items 3 and 4 only.

Items and Dispositions

Item 1 — Winter readiness reporting, northern corridors. Departmental status received. No action required. Next report scheduled at the ordinary interval.

Item 2 — Coast guard temporary authorizations. Ministry of Territorial Infrastructure reported nine stations operating under temporary authorization, three of which have been renewed more than four consecutive times. Referred to the Ministry for a consolidated report to the Council at its next ordinary session.

Item 3 — Consular staffing, western missions. Received. No action required.

Item 4 — Border reporting protocols, Lake Varda. Joint status received from the Ministry of Health and the Director of Foreign Affairs. Existing protocols continue unchanged.

Item 5 — Fiscal Administration quarterly close. Received. Published separately in the ordinary course.

Item 6 — Departmental records retention schedule. Deferred to the next ordinary session at the request of the Ministry of Fiscal Administration.

Cross-Domain Matters

No temporary operational lead designation under §2.14.a was proposed or made. No request for assistance under §2.14.b was filed by either executive.

Disposition

Summary published to the NRS as required. The consolidated report requested under Item 2 will be separately recorded upon receipt.

End of record.
