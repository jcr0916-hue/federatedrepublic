Submissions Register — Fiscal Equalization Statutory Review

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0738
Record Type:Committee submissions register
Filing Authority:Joint Committee on Fiscal Equalization
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Submissions received

The committee clerk records the first group of written submissions received under NRS-Y13-0710. The filings include material from Territory administrations, State fiscal offices, the Civic Consul's fiscal administration and public bodies responsible for infrastructure and service-capacity data.

Morantine's submission emphasizes request age, recurring delivery cost and the difficulty of financing capacity expansion while projects remain in the queue. Korda's submission emphasizes infrastructure backlog and the administrative burden of maintaining service commitments across a Territory already undergoing a separate constitutional-status process.

Other positions in the record

Several State submissions caution against making request age alone determinative, arguing that a revised formula should continue to distinguish temporary project delay from durable fiscal-capacity need. Other filings support adding measurable service burdens or infrastructure backlog to the statutory factors considered by the mechanism.

The register records those positions as submissions only. It does not treat the number of filings supporting a position as a committee vote or as evidence that the current mechanism has failed constitutionally.

Next committee work

Committee staff will prepare a comparative options memorandum identifying the principal statutory choices raised by the submissions and the data required to estimate their distributional effects. The committee may then recommend legislation, recommend no amendment or request additional evidence.

Constitutional status

The Executive Monitor's prior finding of no failure remains unchanged. No ninety-day response period under §12.6 is running. Any change to the equalization mechanism requires ordinary legislation; this register itself changes no formula, funding entitlement or constitutional obligation.

End of record.
