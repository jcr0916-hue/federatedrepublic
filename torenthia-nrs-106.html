Selvane — County Registrar Ranked-Choice Tabulation Training

Selvane schedules regional training sessions for county registrars and their deputies on ranked-choice tabulation, ballot reconciliation and the procedures for public observation of the count.

Staff who will supervise a county count are expected to attend one session. Observation procedures are unchanged from the prior election. The schedule lists session locations and times by region; it concerns procedure only and contains no ballot content.

End of record.
