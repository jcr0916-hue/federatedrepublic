Solara Territorial Authority — Winter Mainland Ferry Timetable

The Solara Territorial Authority publishes the winter timetable for the Ostara–Westport ferry. Weekday sailings are reduced from three to two for the season; weekend sailings are unchanged. Fares are unchanged.

Freight bookings follow the same timetable. Sailings may be cancelled for weather on notice posted at both terminals. The summer timetable will be published before it takes effect.

End of record.
