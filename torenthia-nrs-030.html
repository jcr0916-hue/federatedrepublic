Petition

The petitioner, sister of one of the two Korda border personnel killed in the Month 6 incursion, seeks release of the portions withheld from NRS-Y13-0291 under §10.2. The petition does not contest the original classification. It asserts that the stated ground — protection of source-identifying detail — no longer supports withholding the portions describing the circumstances under which the formation withdrew.

Standing

Standing is asserted as a person directly affected by the events described in the record. No objection to standing has been filed.

Scope Note

This petition concerns only the redacted portions of a record already published in part. The classification period established under §10.2, and its ceiling under §1.19.b, remain otherwise unaffected. The Directorate's Month 6 statement that "nothing is withheld permanently" is cited in the petition but is not itself the ground asserted.

Disposition

Forwarded to the applicable court as designated by statute. The Directorate has not yet filed a response. No hearing date is set.

End of record.
