Weller County — Kelvant Road Bridge Inspection Completion Notice

Weller County reports completion of the scheduled inspection of the four Kelvant Road bridges; no span is closed or load-restricted by this notice.

Selvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0097

Record system: Selvane State Record. Filing body: Weller County, County Engineer's Office. Record type: Inspection completion notice.

The County Engineer's Office has completed the scheduled inspection of the four bridges on Kelvant Road between the county seat and the eastern county line: the two-lane crossing at Mill Creek, the Elm Ford structure, and the paired spans at the old millrace. Inspectors recorded span alignment, deck surface, guardrail condition, and the waterway openings beneath each structure during the low-flow window.

No span is closed, load-restricted, or recommended for immediate repair by this notice. The office will post individual bridge records once the notes are compiled.

End of record.
