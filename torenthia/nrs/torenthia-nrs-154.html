Aldenmere Department of Health and Social Services — Terenne Regional Service Schedule

The Terenne regional office files its quarterly outreach and intake schedule; eligibility criteria and benefit amounts are unchanged.

Aldenmere State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0066

Record system: Aldenmere State Record. Filing body: Department of Health and Social Services (State constitution §2.3), Terenne Regional Office. Record type: Quarterly service schedule return.

The Terenne Regional Office confirms its outreach and intake schedule for the quarter: intake assistance at the Terenne center on the first and third Tuesday of each month; health information and benefits-enrollment assistance at the Seamark Hill outreach room on the second Thursday; and a mobile benefits-application window at the Terenne community hall in the last full week of each month. Two temporary intake staff support the mobile window through Month 4.

This return records service schedules only. It changes no eligibility criterion, benefit amount, service standard or departmental budget.

End of record.
