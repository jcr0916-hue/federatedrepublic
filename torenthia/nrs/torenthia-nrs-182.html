State Auditor — Notice of Commencement of the Scheduled Local Equalization Review

The State Auditor opens the periodic review of the local fiscal equalization mechanism under the State constitution; no finding, formula change or recommendation yet.

Norvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0094

Record system: Norvane State Record. Filing body: Office of the State Auditor. Record type: Review commencement notice.

The State Auditor commences the scheduled review of the local fiscal equalization mechanism under Section 5.6(c) of the State constitution, which requires the Auditor to review the mechanism no less frequently than every five years. Municipalities and districts will be asked to submit service-load, expenditure and tax-capacity data for a common reference period. The Auditor will also review the mechanism's published criteria and the most recent prior findings. A work plan and the data request will be circulated.

This notice opens the review only. It makes no finding, selects no formula, proposes no change to the mechanism, and does not affect any pending equalization payment.

End of record.
