Public Counter Queue-System Maintenance Return

The Federal Public Access Administration records scheduled queue-system maintenance at its public counters and the replacement of one display unit; service hours and access rules are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0052

Filing body: Federal Public Access Administration. Record type: Routine operations return.

The Federal Public Access Administration records completion of scheduled maintenance of the queue-management systems serving its public counters, following the return recorded in NRS-Y13-0744. The queue server and ticket printers were serviced, and one display unit that intermittently failed to show the next number was replaced with a matched unit from existing spares.

Servicing did not interrupt counter hours on any day. The return reports no change to accessibility standards, appointment priority, eligibility, statutory filing periods or retention rules.

A maintenance note on the replaced unit has been placed in the counter's equipment file. No new administrative authority or policy is established.

End of record.
