Morantine — Kessel Culvert Survey Register

Morantine logs the first field measurements from the commissioned Kessel culvert engineering survey; the register authorizes no construction or expenditure beyond the existing survey.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0045

The Morantine territorial works office records receipt of the first field-measurement sheets from the engineering survey of the Kessel culvert commissioned in Year 14, Month 1.

The sheets identify drainage-channel dimensions, the recorded position of the inspection access, and locations where additional measurements will be needed after the next scheduled rainfall. The office has entered them into the existing public works register and requested a cross-check against older maintenance drawings.

No construction contract has been tendered. The preliminary register is neither a completed engineering recommendation nor an authorization to enlarge the culvert, close the road or draw on a new capital allocation. Further findings will be entered through the ordinary works-record process.

End of record.
