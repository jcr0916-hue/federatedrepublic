Clerk of the House of Districts — Sitting Calendar Notice

The House clerk posts the sitting calendar for the House of Districts; it introduces no bill, schedules no vote and changes no procedure.

Caldenmere Registry · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0128

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Clerk of the House of Districts (Article III of the State constitution). Record type: Sitting calendar notice.

The Clerk of the House of Districts has published the House’s sitting calendar. The calendar is available at the clerk’s office and through the Caldenmere Registry. Any revision will be posted as a correction alongside the original entry, as §8.2 of the State constitution requires.

This notice lists sitting days only. It introduces no bill, schedules no vote and changes no committee membership or chamber procedure.

End of record.
