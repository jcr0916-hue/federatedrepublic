National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0036

Harren — Hadrin Municipal Records Counter Schedule

The Harren administration has published a temporary public-counter schedule for the municipal records office in Hadrin while ventilation equipment is replaced in the records room.

In-person document collection will move to the adjacent service counter for two working weeks. Existing electronic access and filing channels remain available. Requests already submitted retain their recorded receipt dates.

The administration will post a completion notice when the usual counter reopens. This is an operational notice and does not alter records-access entitlements or constitutional deadlines.

End of record.
