Department of Health — Refrigerated-Storage Maintenance Award DH-13-224, Closing Return

The Department accepts the completed preventive maintenance visits under award DH-13-224 and closes the award with no outstanding work items.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0099

Filing body: Department of Health Procurement Office. Record type: Award closing return.

The Department of Health Procurement Office closes preventive maintenance award DH-13-224 for the six regional refrigerated-storage units. All six scheduled visits were completed under the award's staggered schedule; each visit included compressor and door-seal inspection, sensor calibration, and backup-power testing.

No off-schedule parts or repairs were installed, since the award requires separate authorization for any replacement component. The Office accepts the completed services, and final payment follows under the award's terms. No work items remain outstanding under the award.

This record changes no unit's operating status, storage procedures, inspection schedule, or regulatory requirement, and it creates no maintenance obligation beyond the award.

End of record.
