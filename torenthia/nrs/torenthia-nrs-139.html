Department of Health — Refrigerated Storage Service Return, First Visits

The Department of Health records the first two scheduled preventive-maintenance visits under its refrigerated-storage award; the other four units remain on the existing schedule.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0051

Filing body: Department of Health — Procurement Office. Record type: Service contract return.

The Procurement Office records completion of scheduled preventive-maintenance visits to two of the six regional refrigerated-storage units covered by contract DH-13-224 (NRS-Y13-0713). Each visit included compressor and seal inspection, temperature-sensor calibration and backup-power testing, as listed in the award.

The visits were staggered so that no regional facility was left without temperature-controlled storage. No parts outside the listed maintenance schedule were installed, and no emergency callout was used.

The remaining four units keep their places on the existing service schedule; no unit has been added to or removed from the award. The return does not change the award's terms, departmental storage policy or eligibility for any health service. Further visits will be returned as they are completed.

End of record.
