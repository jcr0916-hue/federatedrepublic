Aldenmere Department of Transportation and Infrastructure — Seamark Hill Road Culvert Maintenance

The Department’s Maintenance Division records seasonal culvert cleaning under Seamark Hill Road and a later re-inspection of one culvert; no closure or condition finding is made.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0065

Record system: Aldenmere State Record. Filing body: Department of Transportation and Infrastructure (State constitution §2.3), Maintenance Division. Record type: Maintenance return.

The Maintenance Division completed the seasonal inspection and cleaning of the drainage culverts under Seamark Hill Road, Terenne County, between kilometer 4.2 and kilometer 6.8. Two culverts were flushed and cleared of silt and brush, and the culvert near kilometer 5.6 received a spot repair to a damaged headwall. The work ran under two days of single-lane flagging; no road closure was needed.

A re-inspection of the kilometer 5.6 culvert has been entered on the district scheduling list for Month 4.

This return records completed maintenance and an internal scheduling entry only. It makes no finding on any culvert’s condition, changes no inspection interval and commits no funds.

End of record.
