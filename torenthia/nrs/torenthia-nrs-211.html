Tern Landing Harbor Committee — Annual Mooring and Slip Inspection Schedule

The town's harbor committee posts the inspection dates for registered moorings and slips in its tidal basin; no permit is renewed and no ordinance or fee changes.

Caldenmere Registry · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0123

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Tern Landing Harbor Committee (municipal home rule, §5.1 of the State constitution). Record type: Inspection schedule.

The Harbor Committee of Tern Landing has posted the annual mooring and slip inspection schedule for the coming season. Inspectors will visit the registered moorings and slips in the town’s tidal basin between the first and third weeks of the season, checking tackle, anchors and clearances against the town’s mooring ordinance. Holders may ask the committee for the date assigned to their mooring.

This notice sets inspection dates only. It renews no permit, assigns no mooring and changes no ordinance or fee.

End of record.
