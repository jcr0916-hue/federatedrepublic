Elections Panel — Judicial Pool Quarterly Return

The quarterly return records no admissions or removals and leaves the standing Judicial Pool at 214; no judicial nomination is made.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0037

Filing body: Elections Panel. Reporting period: Year 13, Month 12 through Year 14, Month 2.

This quarterly return follows NRS-Y13-0591 and records the standing Judicial Pool administered under §9.4.a.

No admission or removal is entered for this reporting period. The published standing total remains 214 eligible individuals. The two applications returned for insufficient service documentation in the preceding notice have not produced an admission in this return; this notice enters no new denial of either application.

Pool membership establishes eligibility for consideration. It does not nominate an individual, establish a judicial vacancy or change the appointment procedure. This Judicial Pool return is separate from the Executive Monitor candidate pool and its succession process.

The next quarterly return is due no later than Year 14, Month 5. The Panel's application channel remains open under the existing eligibility rules.

End of record.
