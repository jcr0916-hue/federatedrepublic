Department of Industry — Calibration Equipment Service Return

The Department records routine calibration of load-testing and measuring equipment, including one load cell sent for recalibration, without changing certification standards.

Merath State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0105

Record system: Merath State Record. Filing body: Department of Industry. Record type: Calibration service return.

The Department of Industry submits its routine calibration and service return for the measurement equipment used in freight-scale and load-testing verifications, under State §2.11(a). Calibrations due in the period were performed against the departmental reference set. One load cell failed its routine tolerance check and was removed from service; it has been sent to the original manufacturer for recalibration, and a replacement unit was placed into service.

This return does not change calibration standards, approve a new equipment purchase, or suspend any verification service.

End of record.
