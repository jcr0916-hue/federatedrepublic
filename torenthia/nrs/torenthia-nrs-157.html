Morantine — Kessel Culvert Survey, Second Measurement Instruction

The territorial works office instructs the survey crew to take the second set of Kessel culvert measurements after the next qualifying rainfall; no date, construction or expenditure is set.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0069

Filing body: Morantine territorial works office, Morantine Territorial Authority. Record type: Survey schedule; public works register continuation.

The territorial works office records a standing instruction for the field crew on the Kessel culvert survey (NRS-Y14-0032) to return after the next rainfall of at least one hour and take the further measurements identified in the first field sheets (NRS-Y14-0045). The crew is to record flow depth at the listed drainage-channel station and note any fresh debris or undercutting at the outlet.

The instruction sets no calendar date, because the measurements depend on the weather. The returned sheets will be entered in the public works register beside the first measurements and the older maintenance drawings.

No construction contract has been tendered, no road closure is scheduled, and no capital allocation is sought or authorized. The instruction is limited to the survey already commissioned and makes no finding about culvert capacity or a preferred repair.

End of record.
