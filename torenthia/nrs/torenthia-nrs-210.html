Calden Port Authority — Outer-Harbor Buoy Lantern Replacement Return

The Authority records replacement of the damaged lantern fittings on two outer-harbor buoys and the return of the temporary light floats; buoy positions and navigation rules are unchanged.

Caldenmere Registry · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0122

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Calden Port Authority (Article VI of the State constitution). Record type: Maintenance completion return.

The Authority has completed the replacement of the damaged lantern fittings on the two outer-harbor navigation buoys identified in the Month 2 inspection return for the approach channel and outer harbor (NRS-Y14-0072). Both buoys now carry matched lantern assemblies set to the published flashing schedule for channel marks, and the temporary light floats used in the interim have been recovered and returned to the Authority’s stores.

The remaining forty-four buoys and twelve daymarks checked in the inspection stand unchanged.

This return records maintenance work only. It makes no change to buoy positions, channel limits, light characteristics or navigation rules.

End of record.
