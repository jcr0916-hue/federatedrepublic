Morantine Territorial Authority — Year 14 Business-Registration Renewal Materials

The Business Registration Office makes Year 14 renewal materials available; the form, fees and requirements are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0070

Filing body: Morantine Territorial Authority — Business Registration Office. Record type: Publication notice.

The Business Registration Office has made Year 14 renewal materials available to businesses registered in the Territory. The form keeps the existing question asking whether any portion of receipts came from currency not issued by the Monetary Authority; its wording, definitions and filing requirements are unchanged.

Renewals may be submitted by mail or at the Tessom counter. Closing-date notices will be posted through the ordinary process when the renewal period opens.

The notice is informational and changes no registration fee, eligibility criterion or inspection schedule.

End of record.
