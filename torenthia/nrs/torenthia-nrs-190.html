City of Rhondel — Landing Light Maintenance Return

Rhondel reports its Month 2 check of the wayfinding lamps at the North and South ferry landings and the replacement of two lamps; both landings stayed open.

Kelvant State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0102

Record system: Kelvant State Record. Filing body: City of Rhondel. Record type: Maintenance return.

City maintenance crews completed the scheduled Month 2 check of the city's wayfinding lamps on the public approaches to the North and South ferry landings in Rhondel, under the city's ordinary municipal maintenance authority (State §5.1). Six lamps were inspected at North Landing and four at South Landing. Two lamps at North Landing failed the brightness test and were replaced; the remaining lamps and their electrical connections tested within normal range.

A corroded junction box at South Landing is recorded for replacement. It remains in service, and the city will report the replacement when it is done. Both landings remained open throughout the work, and no ferry service was affected.

This return authorizes no expenditure beyond the city's existing maintenance authority.

End of record.
