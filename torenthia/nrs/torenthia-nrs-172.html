Department of Health — Refrigerated-Storage Preventive Maintenance, Units Five and Six

The fifth and sixth scheduled preventive-maintenance visits under award DH-13-224 are complete; the award's closing return remains to be filed.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0084

Filing body: Department of Health Procurement Office. Record type: Maintenance return.

On the fifth and sixth scheduled visits under maintenance award DH-13-224, the contractor completed the award's listed services for the two remaining regional refrigerated-storage units. Compressor and door-seal inspections were performed, temperature sensors were calibrated against the reference standard, and backup-power systems were tested under load. Visits were scheduled on separate dates as the award requires.

No off-schedule parts were installed, and no repair authorization was requested. The contractor's visit reports have been received by the Procurement Office and logged against the award's work-order schedule. Payment remains subject to the award's acceptance procedure. A closing return for the award will be filed after acceptance.

This record does not change the award's scope, the maintenance schedule, or any eligibility or deadline.

End of record.
