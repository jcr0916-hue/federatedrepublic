Summary

The Northern Arrival Housing Capacity Act was introduced in the Assembly by members of the Norvane delegation, with co-sponsors from other delegations. The bill addresses a single subject (§3.4): federal funding for housing and reception capacity in States receiving arrivals across Lake Varda, beginning with the northern Lake Varda arrival corridor.

Provisions

The bill (1) authorizes capital grants to receiving States for permanent housing; (2) authorizes operating support for reception capacity; and (3) sets standards for the siting of temporary housing financed under the Act.

Text Publication

The full text of the bill is published to the NRS with this filing. The publication period required by §3.4 runs from the date of this filing, and a substantial amendment restarts it.

Referral

The bill is referred to the Territories and Fiscal Affairs Committee of the Assembly. The Senate’s review period under §3.1 begins on receipt of an Assembly-passed bill and has not begun.

Disposition

The Assembly will file further notice upon committee report, amendment, or final passage.
