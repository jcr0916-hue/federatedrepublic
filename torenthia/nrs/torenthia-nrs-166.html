Corindal Development Commission — Records Imaging Equipment Acceptance

The Commission accepts delivery of document-imaging workstations and scanners for its records administration; no development action is approved.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0078

Record system: Corindal Register. Filing body: Corindal Development Commission (Article V of the State constitution). Record type: Delivery acceptance.

The Corindal Development Commission accepted delivery of three document-imaging workstations and two flatbed scanners for its records administration. The equipment arrived complete and will be used by staff to image historical project files. Operator familiarization is scheduled during the present month.

No records have been transferred, deleted or reclassified as part of this acceptance, and the Commission’s publication duties to the Corindal Register are unchanged.

This acceptance authorizes no development project, grant, loan, bond issue or partnership, and changes no Commission membership, staffing authority or public-purpose finding.

End of record.
