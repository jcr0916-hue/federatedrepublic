Solara Territorial Authority — Winter Ferry Maintenance Return

The transport office completes a routine inspection and minor repairs on the Ostara–Westport ferry; the two-sailing weekday timetable and fares are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0057

Filing body: Solara Territorial Authority, transport office. Record type: Maintenance return.

The transport office carried out the scheduled winter maintenance inspection of the vessel assigned to the Ostara–Westport run. Work covered hull fittings, winter greasing of the deck gear, replacement of a worn gangway cleat at the Ostara terminal, and a check of the passenger-notice displays.

Minor corrosion was found on a starboard rail bracket; the bracket was patched and scheduled for replacement at the spring inspection. No sailing was cancelled for this work.

This return authorizes no timetable change. The published winter timetable, with two weekday sailings, unchanged weekend sailings and unchanged fares, remains in effect.

End of record.
