Ardenmere County Water Works — Pump Station and Meter Schedule Return

The county utility reconciles its pump-station logs and posts next month’s meter-read routes, with no change to rates or service.

Corindal Register · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0077

Record system: Corindal Register. Filing body: Ardenmere County Water Works (State constitution §6.1). Record type: Utility service return.

Ardenmere County Water Works completed its monthly reconciliation of the logs for eleven pump stations and the county’s elevated tank. A pressure-relief valve at the High Street station was replaced from existing parts inventory. No service interruption was reported during the period.

Meter-read routes for the coming month are posted at the county service office. Three addresses are scheduled for off-cycle readings because of building work at the premises.

This return records ordinary utility administration. It changes no water rate, connection policy, service-area boundary or treatment standard, and authorizes no expansion of the system.

End of record.
