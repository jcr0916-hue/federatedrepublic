NRS-Y13-0742

Regional Water-Sampling Equipment Calibration Notice

National Record System · Year 13, Month 12

Record Identification

Reference: NRS-Y13-0742
Type: Technical maintenance return
Filing Authority: Federal Environmental Laboratory Service
Date: Year 13, Month 12

Inspection return

The laboratory service records completed calibration and seal checks for a rotating group of water-sampling instruments assigned to ordinary regional monitoring. Units meeting standard tolerances were returned to the equipment pool.

Exceptions

Two instruments were referred for routine component replacement and will remain out of service until retested. Spare instruments cover the scheduled sampling calendar.

Scope

No abnormal sample finding, water-quality advisory or change in inspection requirements is made by this equipment return.

End of record.
