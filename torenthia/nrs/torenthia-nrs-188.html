NRS Panel — Quarterly Records Imaging Return, Administrative Archive Series

The NRS Panel files its quarterly records imaging return for the administrative archive series for the period to Year 14, Month 2, with no items carried over.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0100

Filing body: NRS Panel — Records Administration. Record type: Quarterly return.

The NRS Panel's Records Administration submits its quarterly records imaging return for the administrative archive series for the period to Year 14, Month 2. During the period, Records Administration completed imaging of the series, performed optical character recognition on the scanned records, and updated the corresponding catalogue entries.

Source documents remain retained under the existing records schedule, and no record's public availability was affected during imaging. No item carries over to the next quarter.

This return does not alter the classification, retention, or publication status of any record, and it does not change the schedule of future imaging work.

End of record.
