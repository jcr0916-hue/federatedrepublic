State Treasury — Monthly Receipt Reconciliation, Month 2

The State Treasury reconciles Month 2 receipts to its ledger and corrects three lines by new entry; no funds move and no appropriation authority is exercised.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0101

Record system: Kelvant State Record. Filing body: State Treasury. Record type: Register reconciliation.

The State Treasury has completed its Month 2 reconciliation of public money received by the State, which State §7.2 requires to be deposited in the Treasury. Receipts in the incoming register were matched against the Treasury ledger by account line. All deposit dates and account codes agreed except for three lines: in two, an interest accrual had been posted to the following business day's register, and in one, a deposit was dated by the forwarding office's calendar rather than the date of receipt.

Each discrepancy was corrected by a new reconciliation entry alongside the original line, as State §9.3 requires. No funds were transferred as a result of this reconciliation, no account balance was adjusted, and no appropriation or expenditure authority was exercised. Routine confirmations from two county offices and one municipal office are carried to the next reconciliation.

End of record.
