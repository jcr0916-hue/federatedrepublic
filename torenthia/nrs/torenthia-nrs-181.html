Office of the Secretary of Housing and Reception — Northern Corridor Reception Supply Delivery Acceptance

The Office accepts a cold-season supply delivery for northern corridor reception facilities; no change to any facility's capacity or to any person's legal status.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0093

Record system: Norvane State Record. Filing body: Office of the Secretary of Housing and Reception. Record type: Delivery acceptance.

The Office of the Secretary of Housing and Reception accepted a cold-season delivery of bedding, blankets, sanitation supplies and winter clothing for reception facilities in the northern arrival corridor. Delivery was completed in full and the quantities were checked against the receiving manifest. A second delivery lot is scheduled later in the season.

Nothing in this acceptance creates a new facility, changes the capacity or operation of any reception facility, authorizes confinement, or alters the shelter, sponsorship, certification or asylum status of any person.

End of record.
