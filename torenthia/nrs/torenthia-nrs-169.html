Rhovane Harbor Office — Shoreline Use Permit Renewals

Five existing dock and mooring permits are renewed, and one new seasonal-dock application at Lemmick Cove remains under review.

Rhovane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0081

Record system: Rhovane State Record. Filing body: Rhovane Harbor Office (State constitution §6.3). Record type: Permit batch.

The Harbor Office renewed five existing seasonal dock and mooring permits for the coming navigation season. Each renewal covers the same footprint, configuration and term previously recorded; no expansion or relocation was approved.

One new application, for a seasonal dock at Lemmick Cove, was received and remains under review. The office noted that the proposed footprint overlaps part of a shoreline area the county lists as a public access point, and asked the applicant for a revised drawing. No permit has been issued for that application.

This return alters no public access right, shoreline survey or public-trust determination.

End of record.
