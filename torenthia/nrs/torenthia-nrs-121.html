National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0033

Aldenmere — Terenne Water Main Replacement Schedule

Aldenmere publishes the winter schedule for water main replacement in central Terenne. Work proceeds district by district, with overnight service interruptions announced at least three days in advance.

Temporary water stations will operate in each affected district during interruptions. The schedule may shift for weather.

End of record.
