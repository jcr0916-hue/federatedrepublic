Department of Health — Refrigerated Storage Service Return, Third and Fourth Units

The Department of Health records scheduled preventive-maintenance visits to two more of the six refrigerated-storage units under its award; two units remain.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0062

Filing body: Department of Health — Procurement Office. Record type: Routine service-contract return.

The Procurement Office records completion of scheduled preventive-maintenance visits to the third and fourth of the six regional refrigerated-storage units covered by contract DH-13-224 (NRS-Y13-0713; first visits NRS-Y14-0051). Each visit included compressor and seal inspection, temperature-sensor calibration and backup-power testing, as listed in the award.

The visits were staggered so that no regional facility was left without temperature-controlled storage, and both units were back in ordinary service when the visits closed. No parts outside the listed maintenance schedule were installed.

Two units remain on the existing service schedule. The return does not change the award’s terms, departmental storage policy or eligibility for any health service. Further visits will be returned as they are completed.

End of record.
