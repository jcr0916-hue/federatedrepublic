Town of Garth — Public Counter Hours and Water-Sampling Calibration

Garth posts its town clerk’s counter hours and records the recalibration of its water-sampling instruments; no fee, eligibility rule or standard changes.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0056

Record system: Harren State Record. Filing body: Town of Garth, municipal administration. Record type: Publication notice and calibration return.

The Town of Garth announces its public counter schedule, effective the first day of the current month. The town clerk’s counter is open Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., closed from noon to 1:00 p.m., except on posted holidays. Counter services include building permits, dog licenses and utility bill payments.

The public works department this week completed recalibration of the town’s three water-sampling instruments at its two well sites, on its routine calibration schedule. Calibration records are kept at the town shop. No well, distribution or service change results.

This entry sets no fee, eligibility rule or regulatory standard and does not alter any standing order.

End of record.
