Morantine Territorial Authority — Kessel Culvert Engineering Survey

The Morantine Territorial Authority commissions an engineering survey of the Kessel culvert, where the Valedon road crosses the Kessel drainage outside Tessom. The survey will assess the culvert's capacity, the roadbed, and options for reducing seasonal flooding.

The survey commits no reconstruction funding and does not change any pending capacity request. Its findings will be published to the NRS on completion.

End of record.
