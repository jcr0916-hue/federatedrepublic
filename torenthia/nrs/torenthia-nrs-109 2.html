Solara — Ostara District Assembly Qualification Roll

Solara's Territorial election administration publishes the qualification roll for the Ostara district, which elects three members of the Assembly by proportional Ranked Choice Voting. Five candidates are qualified to appear, listed in order of filing: Ilka Saremi, Anse Dorvallo, Mira Tollan, Joss Brenner and Teo Varrin.

Qualification confirms eligibility to appear on the ballot. It is not an endorsement, and the order of filing is not the ballot order. This roll sets no ballot order and no election date.

Rolls for Solara's other Assembly districts are published separately.

End of record.
