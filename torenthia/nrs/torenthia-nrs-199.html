Brindle County Board — Spring Load-Restriction Notice for County Roads

Brindle County posts its annual spring load-restriction notice for county roads; effective dates and the appeal process are unchanged from the prior season.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0111

Record system: Harren State Record. Filing body: Brindle County Board. Record type: Notice.

The Brindle County Board posts its annual spring load-restriction notice for county roads. During the posted period, gross vehicle weights on the county roads listed in the notice are limited as the notice provides. The notice is posted at the county offices and at affected highway yards. Its effective dates and appeal process are the same as in the prior season.

This notice is administrative. It changes no county ordinance or road classification and does not affect State or federal highways.

End of record.
