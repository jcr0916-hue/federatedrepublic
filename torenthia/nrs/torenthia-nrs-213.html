Town of Ardenmere — Public Works Yard Readiness Checklist

The town records completion of its seasonal readiness checklist at the public works yard; no equipment purchase or service change is made.

Corindal Register · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0125

Record system: Corindal Register. Filing body: Town of Ardenmere (municipal administration, State constitution §6.2). Record type: Readiness checklist.

The Town of Ardenmere records completion of the seasonal readiness checklist at the public works yard. Items checked were the fuel-storage cap locks, the valve-box markers, a generator start test and the posting of route signs. Two traffic cones were replaced from existing stock.

No equipment was purchased, no service schedule was changed and no ordinance was adopted. The town’s regular collection and street maintenance schedules remain in effect. This entry does not affect the county’s separate responsibilities under §6.1.

End of record.
