Calden Port Authority — Harbor Navigation-Buoy Inspection Return

The Authority records its scheduled inspection of navigation buoys and daymarks in the Port of Calden; two buoys are slated for replacement and nothing else changes.

Caldenmere Registry · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0072

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Calden Port Authority (Article VI of the State constitution). Record type: Maintenance return.

The Authority completed the scheduled Month 2 inspection of navigation buoys and shore daymarks in the approach channel and outer harbor of the Port of Calden. Forty-six buoys and twelve daymarks were checked, with lights and sound signals verified after dark.

Two buoys were found with damaged lantern fittings and are slated for replacement at the next scheduled maintenance window, weather permitting. Both are marked in the meantime with temporary light floats. No channel closure or depth change is required.

This return records a completed inspection only. It does not alter port tariffs, berth allocations, dredging schedules or any pending contract.

End of record.
