Solara Territorial Archive — Winter Public-Service Hours

The Territorial Archive posts its winter reading-room hours and document-delivery schedule; no records are closed and no holdings change.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0060

Filing body: Solara Territorial Archive. Record type: Service schedule.

The Solara Territorial Archive has posted its winter reading-room hours, which apply through the end of the season. Public requests for documents are processed in weekly batches, with delivery notices issued by the archive office.

The archive keeps its ordinary public-service schedule and opens no new reading period and adds no restriction. All holdings remain available under the usual procedures, and no record is withdrawn or reclassified by this notice.

End of record.
