Ardenmere County Water Works — Hydrant Inspection Schedule

The county water works posts its current hydrant inspection schedule for the service area; no hydrant is identified for repair.

Corindal Register · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0126

Record system: Corindal Register. Filing body: Ardenmere County Water Works (State constitution §6.1(c)). Record type: Service schedule.

Ardenmere County Water Works posts the current hydrant inspection schedule for the county’s service area. Crews will work through the area street by street, beginning with the High Street corridor and adjoining streets. The work covers inspection, flushing and flow testing, and no hydrant is identified for repair. Residents may call the county office for the date assigned to their street.

The schedule does not change water rates, office hours or the monthly pump-station reconciliation recorded in NRS-Y14-0077.

End of record.
