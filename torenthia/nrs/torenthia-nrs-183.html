Sponsorship Administration Office — Language-Access Materials and Ilyr Office Schedule

The office publishes plain-language summaries of the State sponsorship criteria and its monthly schedule; no criterion changes and no application is decided.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0095

Record system: Norvane State Record. Filing body: Sponsorship Administration Office. Record type: Publication notice.

The Sponsorship Administration Office publishes updated plain-language summaries of the State sponsorship criteria in the languages most commonly used in the northern arrival corridor, together with translated versions of the published criteria where required for meaningful access to essential State services under State §1.2(e). The office also posts its public schedule for the month in Ilyr, including intake-session and question hours. The materials explain that sponsorship is the State stage only and does not confer legal residency, which requires federal certification (State §9.5(a)).

Publication of these materials changes no sponsorship criterion, approves no application, and makes no determination as to any person's immigration or asylum status.

End of record.
