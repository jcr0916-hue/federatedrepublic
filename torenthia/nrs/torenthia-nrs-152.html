Public Reading-Room Catalogue Reconciliation — Year 14, Month 2

The Public Record Access Service completes its next link-and-index sweep of the reading-room catalogue; no record is removed, altered or reclassified.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0064

Filing body: Public Record Access Service. Record type: Ordinary public-catalogue maintenance return.

The Service completed the next ordinary link-and-index sweep of the public reading-room catalogue after NRS-Y14-0001. Catalogue entries were checked against permanent record identifiers, mismatched link and index details were corrected, and the records posted during the period were confirmed in the catalogue.

No underlying entry, original timestamp or disclosure status was changed; any clarification is recorded as an access note, consistent with §10.1. Access hours and access rules are unchanged, and no publication timeline is affected.

End of record.
