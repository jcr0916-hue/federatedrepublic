Corindal Development Commission — Records Room Hours and Imaging Progress

The Commission posts the public hours of its records room and notes that a second batch of scanned files has been indexed; no development action is approved.

Corindal Register · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0127

Record system: Corindal Register. Filing body: Corindal Development Commission (§5.2 of the State constitution). Record type: Public hours and progress notice.

The Corindal Development Commission posts the public hours of its records room: weekdays from 8:30 to 4:30, with Friday visits by appointment. The second batch of scanned historical project files has been indexed using the imaging equipment accepted in NRS-Y14-0078, and the index is available at the records room.

This notice records office hours and imaging progress only. It approves no development project, and the Commission’s publication duties to the Corindal Register are unchanged.

End of record.
