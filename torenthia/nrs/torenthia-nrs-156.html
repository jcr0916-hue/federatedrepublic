Aldenmere Office of the State Auditor — Quarterly Records Reconciliation

The State Auditor’s office completes its routine quarterly reconciliation of expenditure and debt-service records; no finding is made.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0068

Record system: Aldenmere State Record. Filing body: Office of the State Auditor (Article V of the State constitution). Record type: Quarterly records reconciliation.

The Office of the State Auditor completed its routine quarterly reconciliation of expenditure records and scheduled debt-service entries through the close of Month 1. Sampled expenditure transactions were matched to their appropriation records; six variance items were returned to the responsible departments for documentation and will be re-reviewed before the next quarterly close. The office also updated its internal register of scheduled debt-service payments for the rest of the fiscal year.

This return records internal records work only and makes no finding of irregularity or non-compliance. The certification of projected revenue and the annual fiscal compliance report required of the Auditor by the State constitution are separate functions.

End of record.
