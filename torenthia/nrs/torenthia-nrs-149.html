Archive Environmental-Control Filter Replacement Return — Year 14, Month 2

The NRS Panel records the recurring filter replacement in four administrative archive rooms; no records were moved, and one room's humidity logger is to be checked for calibration.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0061

Filing body: NRS Panel — Records Administration. Record type: Routine facilities-maintenance return.

Facilities staff replaced the supply and return filters serving the four administrative archive rooms on the recurring environmental-control cycle (NRS-Y13-0739). Pressure and airflow readings taken after the replacement were entered in the facility maintenance log.

During the work, the humidity logger in Archive Room 3 recorded a brief reading above its normal band. The logger will be checked for calibration before the next cycle, and the room’s readings will be reviewed at that time.

No record was moved from its registered storage location. This filing concerns facilities maintenance only and changes no classification, retention period, access hours or publication obligation. Routine filing complete under §10.1.

End of record.
