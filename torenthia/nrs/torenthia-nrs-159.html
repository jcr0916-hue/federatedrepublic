Town of Tessom — Municipal Counter Hours, Year 14 Month 2

Tessom posts its winter public counter hours for the rest of the month; no service is added or withdrawn.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0071

Filing body: Town of Tessom, municipal office. Record type: Service schedule.

For the rest of Year 14, Month 2, the counter at the Tessom municipal office is open on weekday mornings and on two weekday afternoons, closing for the midday meal. The notice lists the telephone line for permit intake and the drop box for after-hours forms.

No counter service is added or withdrawn, and the posted hours are staffed within the existing office. The notice changes no zoning, permit or fee requirement.

End of record.
