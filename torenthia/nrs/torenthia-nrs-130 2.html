Solara — Ostara Winter Ferry Passenger-Notice Return

Solara posts updated passenger notices for the existing reduced Ostara–Westport winter timetable; no additional sailing is authorized.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0042

Solara's transport office has distributed replacement passenger notices for the Ostara–Westport winter ferry service. The posted weekday timetable continues to provide two scheduled sailings, rather than the three operated before the winter reduction.

The replacement notices clarify where timetable changes and same-day service interruptions will be displayed at the terminals and through the ordinary passenger-information channel. Staff have also been asked to keep printed copies available at the Ostara ticket window.

The notices do not restore the withdrawn sailing, authorize additional funding, or announce a permanent alteration of the route. Existing fares and ticket entitlements remain unchanged.

End of record.
