Eastvale Industrial District — Disclosure of Contracts and Governance Votes

The district files its periodic disclosure of routine contracts and governance votes; it adds no powers and no borrowing.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0106

Record system: Merath State Record. Filing body: Eastvale Industrial District. Record type: Disclosure.

The Eastvale Industrial District files its periodic disclosure of contracts and governance votes, as Section 5.5(b) of the State constitution requires of industrial districts. The disclosure covers the most recent reporting period and lists routine maintenance and supply agreements entered within the powers the district holds by law, together with the recorded votes of its governance bodies.

No new borrowing, land acquisition, or expansion of district services is included. The disclosure does not amend the district's establishing law, confer new powers on the district, or bind any participating municipality beyond the terms of the listed agreements.

End of record.
