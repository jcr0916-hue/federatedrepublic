Office of the State Auditor — Quarterly Records Reconciliation Re-Review

Completes the re-review of the variance items returned in the last quarterly reconciliation; all six are cleared in the reconciliation record.

Aldenmere State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0118

Record system: Aldenmere State Record. Filing body: Office of the State Auditor (Article V of the State constitution). Record type: Records reconciliation return.

Filed to the Aldenmere State Record by the Office of the State Auditor. The re-review of the six variance items returned to departments in the previous quarterly records reconciliation (NRS-Y14-0068) is complete. Each item was examined against the supporting documentation supplied by the responsible department, and all six are cleared in the reconciliation record.

This entry records the disposition of the reconciliation items only. It is not a finding of fiscal irregularity and changes no appropriation, expenditure or debt-service entry.

End of record.
