NRS-Y13-0744

Routine Public Counter Queue-System Maintenance Return

National Record System · Year 13, Month 12

Record Identification

Reference: NRS-Y13-0744
Type: Routine operations return
Filing Authority: Federal Public Access Administration
Date: Year 13, Month 12

Work completed

The Public Access Administration records completion of scheduled maintenance to appointment and queue-display systems at administrative counters. Service personnel tested numbering, accessibility prompts and fallback paper-ticket procedures.

Continuity

During maintenance, affected counters issued paper tickets and continued receiving applications. The maintenance return reports no change to appointment priority, eligibility, statutory filing periods or retention rules.

Scope

The filing concerns ordinary equipment operations. No new administrative authority or policy is established.

End of record.
