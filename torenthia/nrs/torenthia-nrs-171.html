Varek Public Works Directorate — Snow-Marker Placement Completion Return

The Directorate reports seasonal snow-marker placement complete on State-maintained routes, with three replacement markers still on order.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0083

Record system: Varek Record System. Filing body: Varek Public Works Directorate (State constitution §6.1). Record type: Completion return.

The Varek Public Works Directorate has completed seasonal placement of snow markers along State-maintained routes on the upper Toren tributaries. Crews installed markers at 14 crossings and approach bends, replacing 9 damaged or buried markers from the prior season. Three replacement markers remain on order and will be set when received.

The return records no road closure, no change to route classification and no change to County maintenance responsibilities.

This is an ordinary seasonal completion notice.

End of record.
