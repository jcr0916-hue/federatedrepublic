Western Trunk Route — Bridge Follow-Up Measurements Completed

The Federal Transport Maintenance Service completes the two follow-up measurements rescheduled in its quarterly reconciliation; the findings go to engineering review and no restriction is issued.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0063

Filing body: Ministry of Transport — Federal Transport Maintenance Service. Record type: Ordinary inspection return.

The Federal Transport Maintenance Service completed the follow-up measurements at the two bridge approaches on the western trunk route that NRS-Y13-0745 placed in the next inspection round. Both were measured under the deck and expansion-joint protocol of the existing inspection award (NRS-Y13-0720).

Measurements and photographs were entered in the maintenance system and passed to the Service’s engineering review. No weight restriction, lane closure or urgent repair order was issued. A further return will follow if the review orders repair work or another visit.

This return changes no load rating, inspection frequency or maintenance priority. Routine filing complete under §10.1.

End of record.
