Town of Tessom — Market Ground Winter Schedule

The municipal office posts the winter schedule for the Tessom market ground through Month 3; days, hours, fees and stall assignments are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0120

Filing body: Town of Tessom, municipal office. Record type: Service schedule.

The municipal office posts the schedule for the Tessom market ground for the remainder of Year 14, Month 2 and through Month 3. The market meets on the usual two mornings each week at the ground off the Valedon road. Stall placements remain as assigned at the start of the season, and the office accepts stall registrations for the following month at its counter until the last working day of the current month.

The schedule adds no midweek market and does not extend market hours. It changes no market day, fee or stall assignment, and no use of the surrounding land.

End of record.
