National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0028

Elections Panel — Legat Consul Qualification Determinations and Initial Allocations

The Elections Panel has completed qualification review of the four declarations of candidacy for the spring Legat Consul election (NRS-Y14-0014). Ines Carrow, Seren Mak, Tobias Vael and Pell Sandris are each qualified to appear on the ballot.

Each qualified candidate who applied has received the same initial public financing allocation under §8.4. Acceptance does not bar private fundraising, and all contributions remain subject to full disclosure under §8.1.

Ballot order will be determined by public lot under the Panel's published standards. The lot has not yet been held. This record sets no election date.

End of record.
