Archive Scanner Maintenance — Quarterly Service Return

The NRS Panel records completion of the next quarterly maintenance of its archive scanners; access, retention and classification arrangements are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0050

Filing body: NRS Panel — Administration. Record type: Routine equipment maintenance return.

The NRS Panel's administration records completion of the scheduled quarterly maintenance of the high-volume scanners used to image records submitted for public archival retention, following the service recorded in NRS-Y13-0719. Each scanner was cleaned, its consumables were replaced, and its output was checked against standard test sheets.

The work was carried out during ordinary duty hours. Routine public-record intake remained available, no scanning backlog was created, and no record was altered, deleted or reclassified in connection with the maintenance.

The maintenance does not change the retention schedule, access arrangements or classification status of any imaged series. The next service remains quarterly under the standing maintenance calendar.

End of record.
