Ardenmere County Public Works — Route 17 Bridge and Culvert Inspection Return

County crews complete the scheduled inspection of bridges and culverts along County Route 17; one deck joint is listed for resealing and no load restriction or closure is needed.

Corindal Register · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0076

Record system: Corindal Register. Filing body: Ardenmere County Public Works Department (State constitution §6.1). Record type: Maintenance return.

County crews completed the scheduled inspection of eleven bridges and twenty-six culverts along County Route 17 and its connected county roads, including the West Branch crossing near the southern end of the corridor. The culvert inlet at mile marker 6.2 was cleared of debris. The deck joint on the West Branch span is listed for resealing within the standing maintenance cycle.

A follow-up inspection of the mile 6.2 culvert, made after an earlier drainage complaint, recorded no change in its condition. The complaint remains on file.

No bridge was posted with a load restriction and no road closure was required. This return records inspections only. It makes no engineering or environmental finding and changes no road designation, load limit, maintenance budget or county boundary.

End of record.
