Westport Port — Equipment Maintenance Return

The port maintenance office files its monthly equipment return under the standing inspection schedule; no equipment failed its check.

Arvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0089

Record system: Arvane State Record. Filing body: Westport Port maintenance office. Record type: Maintenance return.

The Westport port maintenance office files its monthly return for equipment under the standing inspection and service schedule. Shore crane one and shore crane two passed their routine load-cycle checks. The fuel-dispensing station passed its metering and leak check. Dock fendering along the north pier was inspected; one rubber section at berth six shows the usual tidal wear and was rotated within existing stock.

No equipment failed its check. This return makes no change to port hours, berthing assignments, or vessel schedules.

End of record.
