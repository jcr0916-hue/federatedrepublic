Merath Industrial Safety — Inspection Completion Return

The industrial inspection office reports completion of the inspections on the Month 1 calendar, with no emergency order and no change to the calendar.

Merath State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0104

Record system: Merath State Record. Filing body: Merath industrial inspection office. Record type: Completion return.

The Merath industrial inspection office reports completion of the inspections scheduled under the industrial inspection calendar published in Month 1 (NRS-Y14-0011), under the Department of Labor's ordinary administration of that calendar (State §2.11(a)). Inspections at the listed production facilities and loading yards were completed during the period. At one loading yard the scheduled inspection was completed on a second visit after a shift change left the yard's safety representative unavailable; the delay is noted in the inspection record and no penalty was assessed.

No emergency order was issued and no facility was closed. The calendar is unchanged, and follow-up visits, where scheduled, remain on the standard schedule and will be reported in their own returns. This return determines no facility's compliance beyond the completed inspection and does not alter any employer's operating status.

End of record.
