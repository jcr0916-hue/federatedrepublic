Elections Panel — Assembly Counting Guidance Published

The Elections Panel issues a public explanation of single-member and multi-member Assembly counting methods; it announces no election date, ballot order or result.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0041

The Elections Panel publishes general explanatory guidance on the counting methods used in elections to the Assembly. The document is intended for voters, candidates and local election administrators. It changes no voting rule or eligibility determination.

For a single-member district, the count uses majority-based Ranked Choice Voting under §3.2. For a multi-member district, the count uses proportional Ranked Choice Voting for the number of seats assigned to that district. Votes and transfers are counted within each district; a reported national first-preference share does not itself determine the allocation of five hundred Assembly seats.

Administrators may reproduce the guide in accessible formats and translate its explanatory examples. Such examples are hypothetical and do not predict the outcome of any district contest.

This publication does not set an election date, certify ballot order, alter the published candidate rolls in Dorven or Ostara, or make a finding about the Civic Consul's support in a future Assembly.

End of record.
