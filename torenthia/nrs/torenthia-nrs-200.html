Solara Port Archive — Cargo-Booking Extract Preparation

The Port Archive begins preparing the next Ostara–Westport cargo-booking extract under the transport office's standing request; the entry changes no schedule.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0112

Filing body: Solara Port Archive. Record type: Records request and extract return.

The Port Archive has begun preparing the next extract of Ostara–Westport cargo-booking records requested by the territorial transport office. The extract will be compiled from the vessel and cargo registers for the current winter period and delivered by the same procedure used for previous extracts. No records are requested beyond the established scope.

Preparation is informational. It assigns no capacity, sets no freight rate and alters no booking procedure at either terminal. It does not change the published winter timetable.

End of record.
