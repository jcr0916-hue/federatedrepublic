Calden Port Authority — Shore-Power Equipment Delivery Acceptance

The Authority accepts delivery of shore-power cable reels and replacement connectors for the main terminal, holding some reels as spare stock; no berth or tariff changes.

Caldenmere Registry · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0075

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Calden Port Authority (Article VI of the State constitution). Record type: Delivery acceptance.

The Authority accepted delivery, under an existing procurement contract, of twenty shore-power cable reels and sixty replacement connectors for vessel berths at the main terminal. The reels are of the standard type already in the berth inventory. Eight of the twenty are held in storage as spare stock, beyond the number of berths now fitted with shore power. Connectors were tagged and entered in the maintenance inventory.

Delivery was completed on the agreed schedule, and no berth service was interrupted.

This acceptance does not extend any lease, tariff or concession, authorizes no new shore-power installation and changes no berth allocation.

End of record.
