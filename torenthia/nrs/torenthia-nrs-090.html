NRS-Y14-0002

Westport Harbor Marker Maintenance Return

Record identification

Reference: NRS-Y14-0002
Filing authority: Federal Maritime Maintenance Service
Type: Ordinary navigational-infrastructure maintenance return
Location: Westport, Arvane
Date: Year 14, Month 1

Work recorded

A rotating maintenance crew completed scheduled visual inspections, battery checks and fixture cleaning on the harbor approach markers in Westport. Completed work orders have been returned to the public maintenance register. Two non-urgent paint and fastener replacements have been added to the next ordinary maintenance round.

Operations

The return reports no lost marker, channel closure, safety directive or alteration to permissible navigation. Harbor operating authorities retain their existing responsibilities. This notice is not related to the separate Lake Varda civilian-navigation conference.

Disposition

Routine filing complete under the ordinary public-record practice of §10.1; no constitutional or regulatory finding is made.

End of record.
