Ministry of Maritime Affairs — Cold-Storage Recorder Calibration Check

The Ministry records a follow-up calibration check on the replacement temperature recorder at the municipal fishing-harbor cold-storage units; no schedule, quota or inspection standard changes.

Caldenmere Registry · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0124

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Ministry of Maritime Affairs (§2.8 of the State constitution). Record type: Calibration check return.

The Ministry has completed the follow-up calibration check on the temperature recorder replaced during preventive maintenance of the cold-storage units at the municipal fishing harbors (NRS-Y14-0073). The replacement unit reads within its published tolerance across the operating range, and the Ministry has reviewed the three units’ logs since the maintenance visit. No further corrective work is scheduled.

This return records the equipment check only. It alters no storage schedule, catch quota or inspection standard.

End of record.
