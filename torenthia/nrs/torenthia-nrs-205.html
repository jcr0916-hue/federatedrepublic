Terenne County Public Works — Replacement Traffic Counter Calibration

Records field calibration and placement of the two replacement traffic counters noted in the county bridge-inspection register; the inspection schedule is unchanged.

Aldenmere State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0117

Record system: Aldenmere State Record. Filing body: Terenne County Public Works (county authority under State constitution §6.1). Record type: Equipment calibration return.

Filed to the Aldenmere State Record by Terenne County Public Works. The two replacement traffic counters identified in the county bridge-inspection register (NRS-Y14-0067) have been field-calibrated at their assigned locations and placed in service, and are recording axle counts at the standard interval for the inspection cycle. The inspections of the remaining twelve county bridges stay scheduled across Months 3 through 6 as previously entered.

This entry records equipment readiness only. It changes no inspection date, road classification, posted load limit or traffic-management measure.

End of record.
