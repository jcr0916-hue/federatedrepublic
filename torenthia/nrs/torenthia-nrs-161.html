Caldenmere Ministry of Maritime Affairs — Fishing-Harbor Cold-Storage Maintenance Return

The Ministry records completed preventive maintenance on three cold-storage units serving municipal fishing harbors, with no change to fees, ice rates or schedules.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0073

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: Ministry of Maritime Affairs (State constitution §2.8). Record type: Maintenance completion return.

The Ministry completed scheduled preventive maintenance on three cold-storage units serving municipal fishing harbors. Each unit passed its refrigerant-pressure test. Door seals were replaced on two units, and one unit received a new temperature recorder after the previous recorder failed calibration.

The work was carried out outside landing hours. One harbor closed for a single evening while the recorder was replaced. Each unit’s service log is retained in the Ministry’s maintenance files.

This return records completed maintenance only. It changes no landing fee, ice rate or service schedule and commits no future maintenance or capital spending.

End of record.
