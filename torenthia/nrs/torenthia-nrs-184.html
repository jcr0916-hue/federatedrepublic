Office of the Chief Administrator — Winter Motor-Pool Inspection Schedule

The State administration schedules winter-condition inspections for State motor-pool vehicles; no vehicle is taken out of service by this entry.

Selvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0096

Record system: Selvane State Record. Filing body: Office of the Chief Administrator. Record type: Maintenance schedule.

The State administration has posted the Month 2 inspection schedule for State motor-pool sedans, light trucks, and service vans assigned to department lots. Inspections cover tires, wiper hardware, heater and defroster function, and undercarriage corrosion checks for vehicles that served on treated roads during the previous winter. The schedule assigns each lot a two-day inspection window and directs drivers to report any deferred item already noted on a vehicle's condition card before the vehicle is inspected. Completed inspection sheets will be retained by the Office.

Vehicles found to need service will be listed in a later maintenance return. This entry does not place any vehicle out of service, does not authorize repair expenditure, and does not change any vehicle assignment.

End of record.
