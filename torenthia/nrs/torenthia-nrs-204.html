Public Record Access Service — Reading-Room Filter Replacement Notice

Schedules replacement of filtered ventilation units in the principal public reading room; the room closes for one afternoon and the service counters stay open.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0116

Filing body: Public Record Access Service, Facilities. Record type: Facilities schedule notice.

Replacement of the filtered ventilation units in the principal public reading room is scheduled for a single afternoon this month. The reading room will be closed for that afternoon; public records service counters remain open, and access notes will be posted at the entrance as usual.

The replacement does not affect catalogue access, reproduction services or any record’s availability. The notice changes no reading-room hours, service schedule or access rule. Routine filing complete under §10.1.

End of record.
