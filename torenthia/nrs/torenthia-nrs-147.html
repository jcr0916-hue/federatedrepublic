Ostara Harbor Office — Winter Berth Allocation

The Ostara Harbor Office posts its winter berth assignments and service hours; mooring fees and the waiting-list procedure are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0059

Filing body: Ostara Harbor Office, municipal administration. Record type: Schedule.

The Ostara Harbor Office has posted the winter berth allocation, assigning seasonal berths to vessels whose winter mooring applications arrived by the posted deadline. The allocation list, the service hours of the fuel dock and pump-out station, and the procedure for joining the winter waiting list are displayed at the harbor office and on the east breakwater notice board.

Mooring fees are unchanged from the published schedule. The allocation does not affect vessels on the ferry run, which berth at the ferry terminal, and adds or removes no moorings.

End of record.
