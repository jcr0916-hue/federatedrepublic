Elections Panel — Accessible Ballot Demonstration Materials

The Elections Panel releases nonbinding practice ballot materials for single- and multi-member Assembly contests and the separate Legat Consul ballot, without assigning an election date or ballot order.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0044

The Elections Panel makes demonstration ballots and accompanying voter instructions available to election administrators, public libraries and local service counters. The materials use invented names and clearly identify themselves as practice examples rather than live ballot papers.

The Assembly examples distinguish the majority-based ranked-choice count for a single-member district from proportional ranked-choice counting in a multi-member district. An additional demonstration explains that the Legat Consul is elected through its own national and State components, not through the Assembly constituency count.

Administrators may reproduce the illustrations in large print and accessible electronic formats. Local offices are invited to report unclear instructions and presentation defects to the Panel's public guidance desk. These reports concern the legibility and usability of the materials, not the validity of a candidate nomination.

The examples do not add or remove qualified candidates, set actual ballot order, announce polling arrangements, certify the readiness of any particular voting station or establish an election date. The public lot announced for the Legat Consul ballot has not been held by this publication.

End of record.
