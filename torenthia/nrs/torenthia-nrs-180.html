City of Ilyr — Winter Shoreland Walkway and Landing Maintenance Schedule

Ilyr Public Works posts its winter maintenance schedule for the shoreland walkway and public boat landings; no change to navigation, emergency authority or shoreland rules.

Norvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0092

Record system: Norvane State Record. Filing body: City of Ilyr, Public Works. Record type: Seasonal maintenance schedule.

The City of Ilyr Public Works office posts the winter maintenance schedule for the shoreland walkway and the public boat landings within the city. Through the cold season the walkway will be sanded on a rotating three-day cycle, with priority service after freezing rain and heavy snow. Gangway and slip inspections at the two public landings will continue on the established fortnightly interval, with an additional inspection called after any reported ice breakup event. Sand and grit stores were topped up in the first week of the month.

The schedule affects maintenance only. It does not change public access rules, boating or navigation conditions, any emergency declaration, or the lake-shorelands management arrangements under State law.

End of record.
