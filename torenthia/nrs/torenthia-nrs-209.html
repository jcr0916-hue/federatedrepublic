Morantine Territorial Authority — Territorial Health Services Office, Tessom Clinic Schedule

The territorial health services office posts the consultation schedule for the Tessom community clinic through the end of Month 2; services, staffing and fees are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0121

Filing body: Morantine Territorial Authority — Territorial Health Services Office. Record type: Service schedule.

The Territorial Health Services Office posts the consultation schedule for the Tessom community clinic for the remainder of Year 14, Month 2, as part of the territorial administration of health delivery (Morantine Territorial Compact, Article 6). The clinic holds appointment consultations on weekday mornings and walk-in hours on two weekday afternoons. Residents are invited to telephone the clinic to arrange appointments.

This is a routine schedule. It establishes no new clinic, facility or service class and changes no clinic service, staffing, fee or opening hour.

End of record.
