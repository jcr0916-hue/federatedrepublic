Federal Transport Maintenance Service — Western Trunk Route Bridge Follow-Up, Engineering Review Complete

Completes the engineering review of the follow-up measurements at two bridge approaches on the western trunk route; no added restriction and no repair ordered.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0115

Filing body: Ministry of Transport — Federal Transport Maintenance Service. Record type: Inspection review completion return.

The Service’s engineering review has completed its assessment of the follow-up measurements taken at the two western trunk route bridge approaches reported in NRS-Y14-0063, under the existing inspection award (NRS-Y13-0720). The measurements showed no change requiring added restriction, and the review orders no repair or further visit at this time. The follow-up work order is closed as complete.

This return does not amend the inspection award, set a new inspection interval or alter any bridge’s load or service status. Routine filing complete under §10.1.

End of record.
