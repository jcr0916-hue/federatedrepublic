Orath — Dorven Freight Estimate Source-Table Access

The Orath transport office will make the source tables underlying the Dorven agricultural freight estimates available for inspection at designated county counters. Printed copies may be requested through the ordinary records service.

Carriers and cooperatives may submit written correction requests identifying a table and supporting record. Staff will maintain a public correction log distinguishing verified changes from disputed entries. The estimates remain provisional for planning and do not authorize new works, freight charges or appropriations.

End of record.
