Solara — Winter Ferry Timetable: Administrative Explanation

Solara explains the operational basis and limits of its existing two-sailing winter weekday timetable without changing fares, funding, or service.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0048

Filing body: Solara Territorial Authority, transport office. Record type: Public administrative explanation.

The Territorial Authority publishes this explanation of the winter weekday timetable already in force on the Ostara–Westport ferry route. The schedule provides two weekday sailings in place of the three offered before the seasonal reduction. Weekend service and fares remain unchanged.

The office says the winter timetable was prepared to leave a wider operating interval for routine vessel servicing, terminal work, and weather-related adjustments. These are operational considerations, not a finding that Solara lacks the fiscal capacity to provide a constitutionally required service. The office publishes no cost comparison, passenger-volume estimate, or claim that any single factor alone required the reduction.

Passenger notices remain available through both terminals and the ordinary service-information channel. The office acknowledges that the longer intervals between weekday sailings affect connections and service planning on the Arvane shore; the published timetable is the reference until a revised one is formally issued.

This explanation does not restore the third weekday sailing, change the summer timetable, approve spending, amend a contract, or make any finding under §12.6. No new ferry service decision is entered by this notice.

End of record.
