Northgate County Public Works Department — Grader Cutting Edges Received and Fitting Schedule

Northgate County records receipt of replacement grader cutting edges and a fitting schedule at its three highway yards; no road or service change results.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0110

Record system: Harren State Record. Filing body: Northgate County Public Works Department. Record type: Maintenance return.

The Northgate County Public Works Department records the receipt of replacement cutting edges for the two graders noted in its winter service readiness return. Fitting is scheduled at the three highway yards before the next forecast wintry weather. All three yards report salt and sand supplies at ordinary seasonal levels.

No road or service change is made by this entry, and no additional equipment is authorized.

End of record.
