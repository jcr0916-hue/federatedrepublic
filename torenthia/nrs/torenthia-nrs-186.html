Civil Service Commission — Shared Local Assessment Service Update

The Commission's shared assessment service for smaller local governments will offer three open assessment sessions during Month 3.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0098

Record system: Selvane State Record. Filing body: Civil Service Commission. Record type: Service update.

The Civil Service Commission's shared assessment service for smaller local governments will offer three open assessment sessions during Month 3 at its regional centers. The sessions are scheduled for the first and third full weeks of the month, with one evening session to accommodate part-time applicants. The service provides written assessment materials, structured evaluation, and a certified candidate pool for participating local governments that lack the capacity to run separate competitions.

Participation remains open to any city, town, or county that has requested shared personnel administration under State §48(c). The service will publish aggregate results by community only after each competition closes. This update does not open a new application cycle for any particular position, does not name candidates, and does not change the Commission's rules for certification or review.

End of record.
