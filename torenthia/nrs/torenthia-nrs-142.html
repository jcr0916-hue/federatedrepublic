Harren State Records Division — Quarterly Preservation Reconciliation

The State Records Division reconciles the quarter's Harren State Record entries and confirms their integrity and federal interoperability; no entry is altered, removed or added.

Harren State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0054

Record system: Harren State Record. Filing body: State Records Division, under the State Administrator’s administrative departments. Record type: Quarterly maintenance and reconciliation return.

The State Records Division completed its quarterly reconciliation of Harren State Record entries as of the close of the prior month. The reconciliation covered 1,284 entries received during the quarter, including enacted laws, administrative rules, certifications, and county and municipal filings, and verified checksums for all migrated legacy volumes.

No entry was deleted, overwritten or reclassified. Where one filing contained a technical error, a correction entry was appended alongside the original with a dated audit trail, consistent with §9.2(c) of the State constitution. Interoperability tests with the federal National Record System succeeded without reformatting delay.

The division’s accession backlog of pre-consolidation county paper registers stands at 312 volumes, unchanged from the prior quarter; processing continues on the existing calendar. This entry changes no law, rule or official act and creates no new obligation.

End of record.
