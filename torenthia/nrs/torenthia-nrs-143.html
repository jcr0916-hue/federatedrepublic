Northgate County Public Works — Winter Service Readiness Return

Northgate County inventories road materials and equipment ahead of the snowfall season; no procurement, staffing or service change is authorized.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0055

Record system: Harren State Record. Filing body: Northgate County Public Works Department. Record type: Seasonal readiness return.

Northgate County Public Works filed its winter service readiness return for the coming season. Stock at the county’s three highway yards is inventoried at 1,420 tons of treated road grit, 380 tons of salt and 310 tons of cold-patch, with replacement cutting edges on hand for twelve plow units and eight graders.

Seasonal equipment inspections are complete; two graders await fitting of worn cutting edges in existing maintenance slots. Patrol schedules remain at the prior season’s frequency on all county routes, and snow-fence placement follows the standing calendar.

The return notes no change in staffing, fleet or service obligations. It is an inventory and readiness statement only and authorizes no procurement, expenditure or alteration of any standing order.

End of record.
