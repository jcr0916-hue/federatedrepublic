Kell’s Hollow County Public Works Office — Winter Bridge Inspection Schedule

The county posts its Month 2 inspection schedule for three bridges over upper feeder streams, with no closure or weight restriction.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0082

Record system: Varek Record System. Filing body: Kell’s Hollow County Public Works Office (State constitution §5.2). Record type: Schedule.

The Kell’s Hollow County Public Works Office will carry out its scheduled winter inspection of the county’s three bridges over the upper feeder streams during Month 2. Inspections are set for two mornings in the month, weather permitting. Each inspection covers the deck, abutments and approach surfaces, with a written note of any maintenance item for the spring work list.

The schedule is posted at the county records office and at the two road sheds used by the county crews.

These inspections are routine. The schedule authorizes no closure or weight restriction.

End of record.
