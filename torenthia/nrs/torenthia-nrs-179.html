State Treasurer — Intrayear Cash-Flow Register Reconciliation

The Treasury reconciles its intrayear cash-flow register and confirms outstanding instruments mature within the fiscal year; no certification under the spending or debt limitations is made.

Arvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0091

Record system: Arvane State Record. Filing body: State Treasurer's office. Record type: Register reconciliation.

The State Treasury completed its routine reconciliation of the intrayear cash-flow register for the current fiscal period under the Treasurer's custody and fiscal reporting duties (State §§3.7(f) and 7.6). Deposits recorded in the Treasury's custody of public funds were matched against reasonably anticipated revenue for the period. Intrayear instruments outstanding were confirmed to mature within the present fiscal year and were not renewed or applied to finance an operating deficit, as §7.8(c) requires for temporary obligations of that kind.

No discrepancy required adjustment. This reconciliation records only the Treasury's own custody and reporting functions under Article VII and makes no certification under §7.4 or §7.7, which remain the State Controller's responsibilities.

End of record.
