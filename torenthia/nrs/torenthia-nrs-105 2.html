National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0017

Calden Harbor Authority — Winter Pilotage Tariff Schedule

The Calden Harbor Authority publishes its winter pilotage tariff. Rates are unchanged from the prior season. The night-transit surcharge applies as before between the published tide windows.

The Calden pilots' association was consulted on the schedule. Vessel agents may request the full tariff table from the Authority's harbor office.

End of record.
