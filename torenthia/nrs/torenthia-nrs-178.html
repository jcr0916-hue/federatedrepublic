Westport Municipal Office — Water-Frontage Permit Batch

A routine batch of twelve waterfront permits for shore maintenance and minor works; no new construction, change of use, or exclusive privilege is authorized.

Arvane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0090

Record system: Arvane State Record. Filing body: Westport municipal office. Record type: Permit batch.

A batch of waterfront permits issued by the Westport municipal office for shore maintenance and minor works. Four permits authorize repair of existing pier decking and mooring cleats. Six renew seasonal shore-stairs and float permits. Two permits authorize replacement of private pilings in place of deteriorated ones, on condition that the work is completed within the existing footprint without extending into the public channel.

All permits are subject to the customary conditions regarding navigational clearance and notification to the port maintenance office. No permit authorizes new construction, a change of use, or occupation of public moorings, and none confers an exclusive privilege. This batch changes no local fee schedule.

End of record.
