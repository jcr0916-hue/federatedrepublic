Lake Varda Stewardship Office — Quarterly Water-Sampling Return

The stewardship office completes its second quarterly sampling round at twelve shoreline stations, with laboratory results pending and no finding issued.

Rhovane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0079

Record system: Rhovane State Record. Filing body: Lake Varda Stewardship Office (State constitution §§6.2 and 6.4). Record type: Monitoring return.

Field crews completed the second quarterly water-sampling round at twelve regularly visited stations along the upper-western shore, from the northern county line to the southern inlet. Surface and near-bed samples were taken under the standing collection schedule, logged by station, and delivered to the State laboratory under chain of custody. Containers, collection times and station identifiers were reconciled against the station register before transfer.

No analytical results have been reported, no exceedance has been identified, and no swimming, fishing or navigation advisory has been issued. The next regular round follows the standing quarterly calendar.

This return records collection only. It changes no access right, permit, shoreline survey or water-quality determination.

End of record.
