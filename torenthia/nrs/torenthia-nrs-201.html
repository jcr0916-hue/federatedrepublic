Ostara Harbor Office — Winter Mooring and Gangway Inspection Return

The harbor office completes its routine winter inspection of public moorings, catwalks and gangways; no berth allocation or fee changes.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0113

Filing body: Ostara Harbor Office (municipal). Record type: Inspection return.

The Ostara Harbor Office has completed its scheduled winter inspection of the public mooring lines, catwalks and gangways in the municipal harbor. Two catwalk boards on the north jetty were replaced and a mooring cleat on the fish-market quay was reset and re-fastened. All public gangways were rated for use. One access ladder on the inner harbor wall is noted for re-coating before spring.

Seasonal berth allocations remain as published and no harbor fees change. The inspection did not cover the ferry’s passenger ramp, which the transport office maintains.

End of record.
