Morantine Territorial Authority — Business Registration Office, Year 14 Renewal Period Notice

The Business Registration Office opens the Year 14 renewal period and sets its closing date; the renewal form and its fees are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0119

Filing body: Morantine Territorial Authority — Business Registration Office. Record type: Administrative period notice.

The Business Registration Office gives notice that the Year 14 renewal period for territorial business registrations opens this month and closes on the last working day of Month 3, following the renewal materials in NRS-Y14-0070. Renewal forms are available at the Tessom office counter and by mail, and completed forms may be returned by either route. A business whose registration lapses must reapply as a new registration.

The renewal form is unchanged, including the declaration line concerning receipts from currency not issued by the Monetary Authority, which is to be marked as the form’s instructions direct. This notice sets an administrative closing date only and changes no fee or registration requirement.

End of record.
