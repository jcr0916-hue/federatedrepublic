Toren Tributary Water Board — Quarterly Register Reconciliation

The Board reconciles its water-rights register for the quarter, records no new transfers, and leaves all pending applications and existing allocations unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0107

Record system: Orath State Record. Filing body: Toren Tributary Water Board. Record type: Register reconciliation.

The Toren Tributary Water Board, established under State §10.2, has completed its quarterly reconciliation of the water-rights register. The reconciliation covered allocation records for the upper and lower tributary districts, municipal supply entries, and the standing ecological-minimum notations. During the period the Board received routine meter-reading returns from the districts and processed no applications for transfer of water-use rights.

One transfer application from an upstream producer remains on the pending register; the file is incomplete and was not acted on. The reconciliation records no new allocation, approves no transfer, and makes no change to any existing water right, household or community access entry, or ecological minimum. It does not assess current water availability or forecast conditions for the coming season.

End of record.
