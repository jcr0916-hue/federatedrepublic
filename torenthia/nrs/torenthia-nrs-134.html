Council of Ministers — Session Summary, Year 14 Month 2

Filing body: Council of Ministers. Record type: Session summary.

The Council met in Year 14, Month 2, with Civic Consul Elin Thoss and Legat Consul Casimir Rehn present, together with ministers and domain officers for the items discussed.

On foreign affairs, the Legat Consul's domain officer for foreign affairs reported on the status of the Lake Varda civilian-navigation conference in Valedon. The briefing concerned the Legat Consul's domain. The Civic Consul received it for information under §2.5(5), in respect of its bearing on domestic interests, and no civic-domain action was taken.

The Council reviewed the statutory budget-preparation calendar. This discussion was informational only: it authorized no expenditure, altered no appropriation, and transferred no authority between the domains. Each Consul retains authority over the preparation and submission of the budget for their own domain.

The Council exercised no executive authority, proposed no temporary operational lead under §2.14.a, resolved no domain dispute, and submitted no question to the Supreme Court. No decision is recorded.

End of record.
