Department of Health — Regional Water Laboratory Calibration Award, Closing Return

Closes the regional drinking-water laboratory calibration service award with all scheduled groups complete and no instrument removed from service.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0114

Filing body: Department of Health, Procurement Office. Record type: Award closing return.

The Department records completion of all calibration groups scheduled under the regional drinking-water laboratory calibration service award (NRS-Y13-0718), following the group completion returns in NRS-Y13-0726, NRS-Y13-0730 and NRS-Y13-0734. No instrument was removed from service during the work, and no off-schedule part or service was installed or performed. The award’s work items are complete and the award is closed.

This return changes no laboratory calibration schedule, testing standard, eligibility for future service awards or instrument operating status. Routine filing complete under §10.1.

End of record.
