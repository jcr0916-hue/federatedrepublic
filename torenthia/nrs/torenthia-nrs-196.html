Eastmoor County — Seasonal Gravel and Culvert Return

Eastmoor County reports routine gravel grading and culvert inspection for the period, with no closures and no change to posted load limits.

Orath State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0108

Record system: Orath State Record. Filing body: Eastmoor County, rural roads administration. Record type: Maintenance return.

Eastmoor County reports its seasonal maintenance return for county roads, which counties may administer under State §5.2(c). Crews graded about fourteen miles of gravel surface in the western and northern districts, reshaped drainage along six miles, and inspected twenty-two culverts. Three culverts were cleared of accumulated sediment; two are scheduled for replacement in the next maintenance cycle.

The County Road 12 crossing received its scheduled inspection and remains posted at its existing load limit. No county road was closed during the period, and no posted weight limit was changed. The return commits no funds, awards no contract, and changes no maintenance schedule previously published.

End of record.
