Caldenmere State Treasury — Monthly Receipts Reconciliation Return

The State Treasury records its ordinary monthly reconciliation of public money held in the Treasury, with no unresolved difference and no appropriation.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0074

Record system: Caldenmere Registry. Filing body: State Treasury (State constitution §6.2). Record type: Reconciliation return.

The State Treasury completed its ordinary reconciliation of public money held in the Treasury for the preceding month. Receipts, disbursements and investment balances were matched against institution statements, and no unresolved difference remained at filing. Vouchers and warrants issued during the period are listed in the supporting schedule.

The next reconciliation will be filed after the close of the following month.

This return is informational. It appropriates no funds, authorizes no withdrawal and creates no new obligation of the State.

End of record.
