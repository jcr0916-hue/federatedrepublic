National Record System · Year 14, Month 1 · NRS-Y14-0025

Office of the Civic Consul — Statement of Domestic Priorities

The Civic Consul publishes this statement of domestic priorities. It describes the Civic Consul's intentions within the civic domain. It is not legislation, does not bind the Legislature, and makes no appropriation.

For the remainder of the current Assembly session.

Fiscal equalization. The Civic Consul asks the Joint Committee to complete its statutory review of the §12.6 mechanism and report to the Assembly before the session ends. The civic-domain ministries will make a drafted statutory correction available to the Committee and to any member for consideration.

Northern arrival housing. The Civic Consul supports consideration and passage of the Northern Arrival Housing Capacity Act during this session.

Social State administration. The responsible ministries will publish monthly figures for claims and audits pending under Article XII and will reduce the backlog within existing authority and appropriations.

For the session following the spring election.

Civil service. The Civic Consul will propose statute establishing merit-based appointment and promotion in the civil service, protected from changes in executive leadership.

Regulatory clarity. The civic-domain ministries will identify standing areas of administrative discretion and propose settled statutory rules to replace them.

Budget. The civic-domain budget proposal will be submitted under §12.2(1) on the statutory schedule.

The Office of the Civic Consul does not use its official communications for campaign purposes.

End of record.
