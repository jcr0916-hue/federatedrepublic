Terenne County Public Works — Bridge Inspection Register Reconciliation

Terenne County reconciles its biennial bridge-inspection register and logs two replacement traffic counters; no inspection result or load posting changes.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0067

Record system: Aldenmere State Record. Filing body: Terenne County Public Works (county authority under State constitution §6.1). Record type: Register reconciliation and equipment receipt.

Public Works reconciled the county bridge-inspection register for the current biennial cycle. Fourteen county-owned bridges are scheduled for inspection: two were inspected in Month 1, and the other twelve are assigned across Months 3 through 6. These dates are administrative assignments and may move for weather or staffing.

The county also recorded receipt of two replacement traffic counters for the Seamark Hill and Terenne approach roads. The units are at the maintenance yard awaiting field calibration.

The reconciliation records no inspection results and changes no maintenance obligation, load posting or capital plan.

End of record.
