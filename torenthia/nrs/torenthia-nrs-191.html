Office of the State Superintendent of Public Instruction — Quarterly Calendar and Attendance Return

The Superintendent's office files the district-reported calendar and attendance return for the period ending Month 2; it makes no funding or evaluation decision.

Kelvant State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0103

Record system: Kelvant State Record. Filing body: Office of the State Superintendent of Public Instruction. Record type: Quarterly return.

The Office of the State Superintendent of Public Instruction files this quarterly return of district-reported school calendars and attendance data for the period ending Month 2, Year 14, in the course of its supervision of public instruction under State §8.2. Districts reported adopted calendars, scheduled instruction days, and attendance aggregates for the period. All reporting districts submitted calendars; two districts filed calendar amendments during the quarter, and those amendments are recorded as new entries alongside the original calendar filings. Attendance data are reported in aggregate and do not include personally identifying student information.

The return is an administrative filing. It does not evaluate district performance, adjust State funding, or alter any school calendar.

End of record.
