Federal Maritime Maintenance Service — Westport Harbor Marker Maintenance Return, Month 2

The Service completes the scheduled Westport harbor marker round with no items carried over.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0088

Filing body: Federal Maritime Maintenance Service. Record type: Maintenance return.

The Service completed the scheduled Westport harbor marker maintenance round for Year 14, Month 2. Federal navigational markers in the harbor were inspected, paint touch-up was applied where the inspection checklist identified surface wear, and fastener checks were completed.

No marker required replacement, and no repair authorization was requested. All work was entered in the Service's work-order log. No items are carried over to the next round.

This record does not alter navigational aids, channel markings, or the Service's inspection schedule.

End of record.
