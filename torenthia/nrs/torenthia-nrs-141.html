Westport Harbor Marker Maintenance Return — Carried-Over Items

The Federal Maritime Maintenance Service completes the two paint and fastener replacements carried over from its previous Westport return; marker positions and channel status are unchanged.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0053

Filing body: Federal Maritime Maintenance Service. Record type: Ordinary navigational-infrastructure maintenance return.

The Federal Maritime Maintenance Service records its next ordinary maintenance round on the federal harbor approach markers at Westport. The crew completed the two non-urgent paint and fastener replacements added to this round in NRS-Y14-0002, and repeated the routine visual, battery and fixture checks on the remaining markers.

Completed work orders have been returned to the public maintenance register. No marker was moved and no light characteristic was changed.

The return reports no lost marker, channel closure, safety directive or alteration to permissible navigation. Harbor operating authorities retain their existing responsibilities. Routine filing complete under §10.1; no constitutional or regulatory finding is made.

End of record.
