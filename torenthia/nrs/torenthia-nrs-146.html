Solara Port Archive — Winter Quarter Register Reconciliation

The Port Archive reconciles the territorial vessel and cargo registers for the winter quarter and supplies a standing records extract to the transport office; no entry changes except one correction.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0058

Filing body: Solara Port Archive. Record type: Register reconciliation.

The Port Archive completed its winter quarter reconciliation of the territorial vessel and cargo registers, covering new registrations, transfers and renewals recorded since the previous quarter. One transcription error in a cargo-manifest entry was corrected against the original filing.

The Archive also fulfilled the transport office’s standing request for extracts of the preceding three winters’ cargo-booking records for the Ostara–Westport route, supplied in the usual format. This is the third extract supplied under that standing request.

The reconciliation found no unregistered transfers and no missing manifest entries. It does not alter any registration, booking, fare or sailing, and takes no view on the winter timetable.

End of record.
