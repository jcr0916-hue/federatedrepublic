Federal Environmental Laboratory Service — Regional Water-Sampling Equipment Calibration Notice, Month 2 Group

The Service completes the Month 2 calibration group for regional water-sampling equipment; the rotating schedule continues.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0085

Filing body: Federal Environmental Laboratory Service. Record type: Calibration notice.

The Service has completed the Month 2 calibration group for regional water-sampling equipment on its rotating schedule. Instruments in the group were checked against certified reference standards, calibration records were updated, and each unit's next calibration date was entered in the maintenance register.

No instrument in this group required removal from service, and no replacement was ordered. The next group on the rotating schedule remains to be completed.

This record does not alter sampling procedures, reporting requirements, or the certification status of any laboratory.

End of record.
