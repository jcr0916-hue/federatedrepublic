Harren — Hadrin Records Counter Reopening

Ventilation replacement is complete at Hadrin’s municipal records office, and document collection returns to its usual counter.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0038

The Harren administration records completion of the ventilation replacement at Hadrin's municipal records office, announced in NRS-Y14-0036.

In-person document collection has returned to the usual records counter. The temporary collection arrangement at the adjacent service counter has ended. Electronic access and filing channels continue on their existing terms.

Requests lodged during the works retain their original receipt dates and places in the processing queue. Applicants do not need to submit a second request because the counter has moved back.

This completion return changes neither records-access entitlements nor filing deadlines.

End of record.
