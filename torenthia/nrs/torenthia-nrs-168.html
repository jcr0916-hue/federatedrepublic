Wincott County Governing Body — County Road 9 Culvert Repair Completion Return

The county accepts the completed culvert and grading repair on County Road 9, leaving one shoulder-settlement item on its maintenance list.

Rhovane State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0080

Record system: Rhovane State Record. Filing body: Wincott County governing body (State constitution §5.1). Record type: Completion return.

The governing body accepted the contractor’s completion return for repair of the washout at the County Road 9 culvert crossing, between the lake access landing and the State Route 12 junction. Work included replacement of about twenty-four meters of culvert, recompaction of the approach shoulder, and regrading of about three hundred meters of ditch line. The county road supervisor certified the crossing open to traffic after a final inspection.

One previously noted section of shoulder settlement between the two bridges remains on the maintenance list; no date has been set for that work.

This return records completion of this segment only. It grants no new public access, changes no property boundary and commits no funds beyond the existing repair account.

End of record.
