Korda Ratification — Residence-Record Correction Procedure

The Elections Panel publishes how disputed residence records can be corrected during the Korda affected-electorate review; no final voter list or referendum date is certified.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0043

In the affected-electorate verification docket opened under NRS-Y14-0007, the Elections Panel publishes an administrative procedure for requesting correction of a disputed residence or district-reference entry.

A resident may identify an apparent mismatch between the recorded residence address and the certification-date electoral district boundaries. The Panel will preserve the original entry, the proposed correction, supporting evidence and its reasoned disposition in the review docket. Corrections to an administrative entry do not alter the fixed district boundaries authenticated as LC-N1, LC-N2 and LC-N3.

The Panel will make procedures available through local registration offices as well as its ordinary electronic channel. The published procedure does not determine whether any particular person belongs to the final affected electorate, and it does not establish a new voter-eligibility standard.

No final electorate list, ballot wording, referendum date or result has been certified. The Northern Lake Settlement remains a Convention proposal awaiting the constitutional next steps, not a completed territorial transfer.

End of record.
