Office of Sponsorship and Labor Integration — Mobile Office Hours Schedule

The Office publishes its mobile and remote service schedule for the coming weeks; the notice approves no sponsorship and changes no criteria.

Orath State Record · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0109

Record system: Orath State Record. Filing body: Office of Sponsorship and Labor Integration. Record type: Service schedule notice.

The Office of Sponsorship and Labor Integration has published its regional service schedule for the coming weeks, in keeping with the geographic-access duty in State §6.2(d). Mobile office hours will be held at the county administration buildings in Eastmoor, Gresham, and Linden Counties and at two cooperative halls in the eastern grain districts, on the dates listed in the schedule. Remote filing and consultation remain available through the Office's published channels for applicants who cannot attend in person.

The schedule is an administrative notice only. It approves no sponsorship application, denies none, and does not change the Office's published criteria or its pending applications.

End of record.
