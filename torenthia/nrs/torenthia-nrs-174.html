Federal Public Access Administration — Municipal Service-Desk Accessibility Equipment Return, Month 2

The Administration completes its scheduled accessibility-equipment maintenance return for municipal service desks; no equipment was removed from service.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0086

Filing body: Federal Public Access Administration. Record type: Maintenance return.

The Administration has completed the scheduled accessibility-equipment maintenance return for municipal service desks covered by its service schedule. Equipment on the accessibility inventory was inspected, including assistive-listening units, accessible counter fixtures, and signage. Inspection logs were updated and filed with the relevant district records offices.

No equipment was removed from service, and no replacement or repair authorization was requested. The next scheduled return remains on the Administration's maintenance calendar.

This record does not alter accessibility standards, the equipment inventory, or the Administration's maintenance schedule.

End of record.
