Fiscal Equalization Review — Source-Table Submission Window

The Joint Committee invites States and Territories to identify inconsistencies in the source tables for its pending comparative illustrations; no formula or funding assumption is adopted.

National Record System · Year 14, Month 2 · NRS-Y14-0040

The Joint Committee conducting the §12.6 fiscal equalization review invites State and Territory administrations to submit corrections and explanatory notes concerning the source data to be used in its forthcoming comparative illustrations.

The Committee's previous direction asked for three reform approaches to be illustrated against a common reference period and a common funding assumption. This request concerns the comparability of the underlying tables; it does not invite a vote on the formula or provide an additional appropriation.

Submissions may identify inconsistent reporting periods, duplicated entries, or service-category definitions that are not comparable across jurisdictions. The Committee secretariat will preserve each submission alongside any published revision to a source table, so changes can be checked against the underlying record.

The Committee has not published completed distributional estimates, a preferred weighting, or a date for a legislative markup. The Executive Monitor has issued no new finding of constitutional noncompliance in this review.

End of record.
