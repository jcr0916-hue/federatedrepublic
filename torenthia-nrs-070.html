Regional Water Laboratories — Third Calibration Completion Return

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0734
Record Type:Routine contracted-work completion return
Filing Authority:Department of Health — Procurement Office
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Completed service

The Department files completion certificates for the third scheduled group of drinking-water laboratory instruments under the existing calibration award. The certificates record calibration, verification and return to routine service.

Continuity

Participating laboratories report no interruption of routine sample intake. Samples requiring an unavailable instrument were transferred under the standing chain-of-custody procedure until local equipment returned to service.

Remaining work

Additional instruments remain on the staggered service schedule. This return applies only to the third completion group and neither closes the award nor authorizes work outside its existing scope.

End of record.
