The Large-Print Shelves Move Downstairs

A Verentum branch library has moved its large-print collection beside the ground-floor reading room. Opening hours and borrowing rules stay the same.

TNB City Desk Verentum Bureau · Verentum Year 13, Month 11

A Verentum branch library reopened its ground-floor reading room this morning after staff spent the evening moving the large-print collection from the upper floor.

The books now occupy four low shelves beside the windows. The gardening and household manuals that previously stood there have moved upstairs, next to the rest of the practical reference collection.

Library staff said readers had asked to have the large-print books closer to the entrance and the comfortable chairs. The move also leaves enough space between shelves for a walking frame to turn without backing out.

Borrowing limits, opening hours, and the location of the returns desk are unchanged. Books already on loan can be returned as usual; readers do not need to bring them in early because their shelf has moved.

Two chairs were briefly left in the entrance while the shelves were rearranged. Both are now back by the windows. The small table between them still carries the newspapers.
