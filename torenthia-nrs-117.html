Verdmont Territorial Authority — High-Pass Road Winter Closure Schedule

The Verdmont Territorial Authority publishes its seasonal closure schedule for high-pass sections of the Territorial road network. Closures take effect at the first sustained snowfall and remain until spring clearing is complete.

Valley routes remain open, subject to ordinary weather notices. Freight operators serving extraction sites should file alternate route plans with the Authority's transport office.

End of record.
