Reporting Period:Year 13, Month 9–11
Summary

Eleven applications for admission to the standing Judicial Pool were received during the reporting period. Nine applicants met the eligibility criteria under §9.4.a and are admitted effective this filing. Two applications were returned for insufficient documentation of judicial service history and may be resubmitted.

Pool Status

The standing Judicial Pool now numbers 214 eligible individuals across all qualifying categories. No individual's admission to the Pool constitutes nomination to any specific vacancy; admission establishes eligibility only.

Disposition

Filed per statutory quarterly cycle. Next notice due no later than Year 14, Month 2.

End of record.
