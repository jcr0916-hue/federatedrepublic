Harren State Archives — Reading Room Winter Hours

The Harren State Archives in Hadrin publishes its winter reading-room hours. The reading room closes one hour earlier on weekdays for the season. Saturday hours are unchanged.

Requests for stored records placed by noon are retrieved the same day. Requests placed later are retrieved the following morning.

End of record.
