Regional Water Laboratory — Calibration Service Award

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0718
Record Type:Routine laboratory service award
Filing Authority:Department of Health — Procurement Office
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Award

A routine service contract is awarded for annual calibration and certification of spectrometry, turbidity and conductivity instruments used by regional drinking-water laboratories.

Continuity

Work will be staggered among participating laboratories so that no region is without ordinary testing capacity during scheduled service. Emergency repair and replacement parts remain subject to separate authorization.
