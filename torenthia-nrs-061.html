Supplemental Source Return — Shared Facilities Title and Service Records

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0725
Record Type:Supplemental response to committee reconciliation request
Filing Authority:Korda Territorial Administration
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Material supplied

The territorial administration submits a further group of source records corresponding to entries left open in NRS-Y13-0721. The filing includes title-register extracts, maintenance instruments and transportation-service records held in the territorial files or returned by participating public bodies.

The Committee register records each source record against the facility or service entry to which it applies. A source record received after the first partial return is entered as additional evidence rather than as a replacement for the earlier return.

Service-pattern notes

The supplied records confirm that several facilities serving the southern lake districts also carry service obligations extending into interior Korda. Records associated with a number of northern corridor facilities describe service areas concentrated within the certified corridor or linked northward through existing transportation arrangements.

These entries describe present service and contractual reach. They do not establish that any facility must transfer with a district, that an existing arrangement would survive a status change unchanged, or that present service geography determines a constitutional boundary.

Outstanding records

Some title and long-term contract entries remain pending archive verification or return by another public body. Those entries remain marked outstanding. No estimated term, owner or value is substituted for a missing source record.

Scope

This filing supports reconciliation of the Committee's working evidence register. It adopts no valuation, transition-cost allocation, ownership determination, viability finding, boundary proposal or constitutional-status recommendation, and it is not the Committee's preliminary compilation.

The filing does not alter the Committee's non-binding working target or toll the Convention period under §15.5.a(4).

End of record.
