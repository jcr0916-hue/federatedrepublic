Summary

The Elections Panel publishes the certified Executive Monitor candidate pool as it stands at this filing and records that the nomination for the office is open. The Executive Monitor General is nominated jointly by the Assembly Speaker and the Senate Speaker from the pool maintained by the Panel (§9.3). The nominee is confirmed by national popular vote at the next federal electoral period, administered by the Panel; §9.3 requires that timing ensure seamless succession.

The incumbent serves a ten-year non-renewable term and continues to hold the office until that term concludes (§9.1, §9.1.d). A person confirmed to a full Monitor General term is not eligible to enter any Monitor General pool (§9.3); the incumbent does not appear on the roster.

Pool Status

The Executive Monitor pool numbers seven eligible members. The constitutional minimum is five (§9.8(4)); the minimum is met. Eligibility was applied on the objective criteria of §9.8(6) only. The pool is subject to audit jointly by the Legislative Monitor and the Judicial Monitor (§9.3, §9.8(2)).

Roster, in alphabetical order by family name:

 Pell Ardane
 Teodor Lind
 Edda Marsh
 Maud Ostrander
 Odile Perrin
 Joren Tavish
 Ilse Warrend

Placement on this roster establishes eligibility only. It is not a nomination.

Nomination

The certified roster has been delivered to the Assembly Speaker, Marit Hosk, and the Senate Speaker, Lucan Brandt. The nomination window opens on the date of this filing. Where the Speakers fail to nominate jointly within the period §9.3 provides, the Senate selects from this pool by 2/3 vote. Judicial Monitor audit findings on the nominee’s disclosure record are published to the NRS before the confirmation vote (§9.3).

The close of the window will be recorded in the Panel’s scheduling notice for the confirmation vote.

Disposition

Filed under §9.8(1). Any change to the pool before a nomination is made will be published to the NRS as an amended roster.

End of record.
