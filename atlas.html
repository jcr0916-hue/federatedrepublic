The Atlas

Torenthia is a specific nation in a specific place, and the place has neighbors. Some of them are friendly, one of them is coming apart, and one of them holds the headwaters of the river the Republic drinks from.

This is the study. The Republic at a Glance is the domestic directory — where things are happening right now. Here is the region those events sit inside.

Republic at a Glance · domestic map and jurisdictions →The Republic Now →

Torenthia occupies a temperate west-facing region of the northwestern continent, between the Western Sea to its west and Lake Varda to its east. Caldris lies beyond the northern mountain system, Sunderland east of Lake Varda, and Valedon to the south. The other continents and islands are largely unlabeled. This continental overview does not establish detailed domestic boundaries. Torenthia's regional map on Republic at a Glance remains the detailed geographic authority and includes an accessible State and Territory directory.

Beyond the Edge
The Map Is Not Finished

What is drawn here is what the Republic has had reason to name. There are ocean routes west of Marisvia that appear on no chart in Verentum, country beyond the far side of Caldris that Torenthian maps mark only as a direction, and land north of the frontier that the Alps have kept nobody's business but its own.

The Republic's own law is settled and exacting — every provision of the Constitution holds or it does not. The world outside its borders is under no such obligation. It is simply the part of the map nobody has needed yet.
