Records Cabinet Placement Return — Administrative Archive Rooms

Record Identification

NRS Reference:NRS-Y13-0735
Record Type:Routine equipment-placement return
Filing Authority:NRS Panel — Records Administration
Date of Filing:Year 13, Month 12

Placement completed

The Panel records placement and inventory tagging of the twenty-four records-storage cabinets accepted under equipment order RP-13-118. The units have been assigned to existing administrative archive rooms according to the facilities schedule attached to the order.

Inventory control

Each cabinet has been entered in the fixed-equipment register and matched to its delivery serial record. No quantity variance is reported between the accepted order and the placement return.

Scope

This filing concerns equipment placement only. It does not alter any record's classification, retention period, disclosure status or lawful access restriction, and it does not authorize destruction or transfer of underlying records.

End of record.
